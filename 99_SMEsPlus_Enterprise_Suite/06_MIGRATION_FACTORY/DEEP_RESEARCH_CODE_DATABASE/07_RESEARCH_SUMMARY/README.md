# 07 — Research Summary

Purpose: consolidate verified research outputs by domain without replacing detailed evidence registers.

## Summary Pack Requirements

Each domain summary must include:

1. Domain identity and bounded-context proposal
2. Research coverage and denominator
3. Verified business facts
4. Mathematical/accounting/inventory models
5. Business rules, validations, and edge cases
6. Entity lifecycles and state machines
7. Domain events with preconditions and postconditions
8. Vendor-neutral conceptual/logical ERD
9. Cross-domain dependencies
10. Migration-relevant facts
11. Clean-room classification and independent-design rationale
12. Open gaps, risks, and reviewer position
13. Evidence index

## Planned Domain Grouping

- Platform Foundation and Shared Services
- Finance and General Ledger
- Accounts Receivable
- Accounts Payable
- Tax and Localization
- Treasury, Cash, Bank, and Reconciliation
- Fixed Assets
- Sales and Order-to-Cash
- Procurement and Procure-to-Pay
- Inventory, Warehouse, and Costing
- Manufacturing and MRP
- Quality and Maintenance
- CRM and Service
- HR and Payroll
- Project and Timesheet
- POS and Commerce
- Reporting and Analytics
- Integration and External Interfaces

The grouping is vendor-neutral and must not mirror Odoo module packaging automatically.
