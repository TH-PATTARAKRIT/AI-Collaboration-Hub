## Assignment Completion Rule

A domain may move to `ASSIGNED` only when these fields are confirmed:

- assigned AI identity or agent instance
- session or task ID
- human sponsor
- assignment date
- due date
- backup owner
- acceptance responsibility

Until then, the domain may be planned but cannot claim verified execution progress.
