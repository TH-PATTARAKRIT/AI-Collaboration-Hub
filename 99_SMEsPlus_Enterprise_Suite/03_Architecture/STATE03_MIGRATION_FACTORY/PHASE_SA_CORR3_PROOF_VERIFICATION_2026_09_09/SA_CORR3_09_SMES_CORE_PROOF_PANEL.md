## 5. `C3-P-01` — what this panel structurally could not find, stated as a measurement

**Perspective 17 — External ERP Advisor / Challenger — is not satisfied and was not simulated.**

The evidence for what that costs is in this round's own results: **three of the four falsified CORR2
negatives were falsified by an *instrument* change, not a reasoning change** — a spaced pattern that
could not reach run-together identifiers; a clean-room scrub that removed the tokens the pattern needed;
a diff-based clause that could not reach an inherited blob. **In every case CORR2's reasoning was sound
and its control fired.**

> **`C3-P-02` — the generalisation, and it is the most useful thing this panel produced.**
> **Every falsified negative in this package had a passing positive control.** The control proved the
> instrument reached *something* — it never proved the instrument reached *the thing the claim was
> about*. **A positive control must be chosen to sit inside the blind spot the pattern is suspected of
> having**, not merely inside the corpus. A control drawn from the same vocabulary as the failing pattern
> will fire and prove nothing.
>
> **This panel found that defect three times because nine differently-scoped executors used nine
> different vocabularies. A single executor, however diligent, would have used one.** That is the
> measurable value of role differentiation — **and it is also its ceiling, because all nine drew from one
> corpus assembled by one party.**
