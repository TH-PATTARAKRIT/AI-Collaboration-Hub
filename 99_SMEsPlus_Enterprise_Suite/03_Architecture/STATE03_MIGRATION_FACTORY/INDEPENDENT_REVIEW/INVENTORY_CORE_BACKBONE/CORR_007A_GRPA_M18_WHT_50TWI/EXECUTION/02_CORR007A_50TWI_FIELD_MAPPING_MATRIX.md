## What this proves / does not prove

Proves: the overwhelming majority of visually-present fields on the official 50-twi form (payer, payee, tax IDs, addresses, all income-type rows and sub-rows, amounts, amount-in-words, dates, signature image) are implemented, wired to real model fields, and CSS-positioned to align with the specific background image Boss supplied.

Does not prove: full statutory completeness. Three concrete, named gaps exist (branch number never rendered; 3 of 7 tax-form checkboxes unsupported; 2 of 4 WHT-condition checkboxes unsupported) and are listed above with exact source citations rather than asserted from memory. Whether these three gaps are legally material is a question for Accounting/Tax (Team 4 boundary + Team 5 disposition), not something this team is authorized to decide.
