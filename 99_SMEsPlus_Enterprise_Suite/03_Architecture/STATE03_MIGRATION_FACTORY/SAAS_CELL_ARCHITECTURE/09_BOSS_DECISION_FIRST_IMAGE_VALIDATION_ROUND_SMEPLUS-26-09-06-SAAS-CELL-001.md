## 2. Visual Identity Direction

The validation round must pair the labels with a coherent SMEsPlus Domain Icon Language.

The objective is not to create unfamiliar terminology merely to appear different. SMEsPlus should preserve recognizable business vocabulary where that vocabulary is already understood, and create differentiation through:

- SMEsPlus icon family
- visual identity
- layout and interaction consistency
- domain visual recognition
- AI assistance
- control/evidence behavior
- Knowledge of Truth

Principle:

> Recognition before Differentiation.

> ชื่อให้คนเข้าใจ — ภาพและความสามารถให้คนจำว่าเป็น SMEsPlus

## 3. First Image Validation Metrics

The validation must collect evidence for at least:

1. Recognition Time
2. Unaided Comprehension
3. Navigation Accuracy
4. Ambiguity Rate
5. Visual Recall

Additional useful metric:

6. Relearning Cost

## 4. Validation Rules

- Do not claim PASS based on preference alone.
- Do not report percentages without human evidence.
- Test text-only comprehension separately from text+icon recognition.
- Do not expose explanatory hints before the unaided comprehension round.
- Top-level domain labels must not be confused with child document/function names.
- Familiar terminology is acceptable when it reduces relearning and improves navigation accuracy.
- Visual identity may differentiate SMEsPlus without forcing unfamiliar vocabulary.
