## Boss Direction

SMEsPlus Standard is intended for real SMEs and may use a relatively low recurring base subscription, approximately 1,500–3,500 THB/month as an initial commercial direction, while material additional consumption and optional services are charged separately through prepaid service credit.

Customers may prepay a larger amount once and have that balance progressively deducted over time rather than being required to make a manual payment every month.

## Commercial Model

`Prepaid Service Credit / Wallet Balance`

Deduction order may include:

1. Base platform rental / subscription.
2. Measured additional consumption beyond included capacity.
3. Additional storage.
4. Additional processing / workload.
5. API / integration consumption.
6. Approved third-party and optional services.

The wallet does not reset at month-end. Usage counters may reset by billing period, while historical usage and billing evidence remain immutable/auditable.

## Customer Protection

Mandatory transparency:

- Current wallet balance.
- Current period usage.
- Estimated monthly burn.
- Projected month-end balance.
- Estimated service runway.
- Projected depletion date.
- Recommended top-up amount.
- 30-day forecast-based advance notice before projected insufficiency/depletion.

The 30-day notice is not credit. If actual usage accelerates and prepaid balance becomes insufficient before the projected 30-day depletion date, top-up or other approved secured funding is required before further chargeable usage.

## Risk Controls

- No Unsecured Postpaid Overage.
- No Surprise Billing.
- No Evidence = No Chargeable Overage.
- Prepaid Before Usage for chargeable additional consumption.
- Additional capacity is activated only after sufficient prepaid or secured credit exists.
- Customer data ownership does not imply free unlimited export/processing capacity.

## Business Rationale

A low base subscription supports SME affordability while usage-based prepaid services protect SMEsPlus from customers with disproportionately high transaction/resource demand. Customers who use little retain their credit longer; customers who use heavily deplete credit faster and must top up sooner.

The model also reduces monthly collection overhead for low-value subscriptions because customers can prepay multiple months of service credit and the platform deducts charges automatically according to measured usage.
