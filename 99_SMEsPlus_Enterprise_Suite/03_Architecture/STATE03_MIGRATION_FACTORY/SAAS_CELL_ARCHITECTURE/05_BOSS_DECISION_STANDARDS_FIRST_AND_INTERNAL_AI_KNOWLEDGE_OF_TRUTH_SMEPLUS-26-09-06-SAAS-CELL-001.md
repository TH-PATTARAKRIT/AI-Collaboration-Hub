## 1. Boss Decision

Boss APPROVES the following principles as permanent architecture/governance inputs for SMEsPlus:

1. Requirement decisions must be standards-first and evidence-backed, not personal-opinion driven.
2. New requirements must be traceable to Law, Regulation, Normative Standard, Control Framework, Industry Control, Business Evidence, or Strategic Product Value.
3. Absence of a standard alone is not an automatic rejection; legal, regulatory, industry, contractual, operational, or strategic evidence may still justify a requirement.
4. Customer-specific requests that lack broad product value must not automatically change Standard Core.
5. SMEsPlus shall maintain a versioned Standards Applicability & Traceability Register.
6. SMEsPlus Internal AI shall use an evidence-bound **Knowledge of Truth** model as its canonical knowledge layer.

## 2. Frozen Requirement Governance Principle

> NO PERSONAL OPINION AS PRODUCT REQUIREMENT AUTHORITY.

Every material requirement decision must trace to one or more of:

- Law / Regulation
- Normative Standard
- Control Framework
- Industry Requirement / Practice
- Contractual Requirement
- Business Evidence
- Strategic Product Value

Customer Request != Automatic Standard Core Scope.

## 3. New Requirement Decision Flow

`NEW REQUIREMENT`

1. LAW / REGULATION MANDATORY?
2. NORMATIVE STANDARD SUPPORT?
3. COMMON ERP / INDUSTRY CONTROL?
4. BROAD PRODUCT VALUE?
5. CUSTOMER-SPECIFIC ONLY?
6. DOES IT VIOLATE CORE / FINANCIAL / SECURITY / TENANT INVARIANTS?

Possible dispositions:

- MANDATORY SUPPORT
- STANDARD PRODUCT CANDIDATE
- CONFIGURATION SOLUTION
- PRODUCT EXTENSION
- ENTERPRISE-SPECIFIC EXTENSION
- FUTURE VERSION CANDIDATE
- HOLD / EVIDENCE REQUIRED
- REJECT

## 6. Internal AI Knowledge of Truth

Boss requires a future **SMEsPlus Internal AI** capability.

Its canonical knowledge architecture shall use the concept:

> KNOWLEDGE OF TRUTH = VERIFIED, PROVENANCE-BOUND, VERSIONED, TRACEABLE KNOWLEDGE.

AI itself is NOT the source of truth.

Authoritative truth sources may include:

- Approved laws/regulations
- Published normative standards
- Approved SMEsPlus constitutional decisions
- Verified business semantics
- Approved architecture decisions
- Controlled configuration
- Verified transaction facts
- Reconciled financial facts
- Approved evidence records
- Versioned product documentation

## 7. Knowledge of Truth Rules

KOT-01  NO SOURCE = NO AUTHORITATIVE CLAIM.  
KOT-02  NO PROVENANCE = NOT TRUSTED KNOWLEDGE.  
KOT-03  NO VERSION = NO NORMATIVE ASSERTION.  
KOT-04  SUPERSEDED KNOWLEDGE MUST REMAIN TRACEABLE BUT MUST NOT BE TREATED AS CURRENT.  
KOT-05  AI MUST DISTINGUISH FACT / INTERPRETATION / RECOMMENDATION / UNKNOWN.  
KOT-06  CONFLICTING VERIFIED SOURCES = HOLD / ESCALATE, NOT GUESS.  
KOT-07  VERIFIED BUSINESS FACTS MUST BE TENANT/COMPANY SCOPED.  
KOT-08  CROSS-TENANT KNOWLEDGE LEAKAGE IS PROHIBITED.  
KOT-09  EVERY MATERIAL AI ANSWER SHOULD BE TRACEABLE TO SOURCE EVIDENCE WHERE APPLICABLE.  
KOT-10  AI MAY REASON OVER TRUSTED KNOWLEDGE BUT MAY NOT SILENTLY ALTER THE CANONICAL SOURCE.  
KOT-11  HUMAN OR AI OPINION DOES NOT OVERRIDE NORMATIVE/VERIFIED EVIDENCE WITHOUT A CONTROLLED MATERIAL DELTA.  
KOT-12  NO EVIDENCE = NO VERIFIED PROGRESS / NO VERIFIED CONFORMANCE.

## 8. Knowledge Classification

Internal AI knowledge should classify information at minimum as:

- NORMATIVE REQUIREMENT
- VERIFIED BUSINESS FACT
- VERIFIED SYSTEM FACT
- APPROVED DECISION
- CONTROLLED CONFIGURATION
- OBSERVATION
- INTERPRETATION
- RECOMMENDATION
- HYPOTHESIS
- UNKNOWN
- SUPERSEDED

This classification prevents an AI-generated interpretation from being mistaken for a normative requirement or verified fact.

## 10. Product Positioning Rule

SMEsPlus does NOT claim that the customer is certified merely because SMEsPlus implements standards-aligned controls.

> Standard Alignment belongs to the Function. Certification belongs to the Customer.

SMEsPlus provides process controls, traceability, configuration, testability, and evidence needed to support customers' own audit/certification/readiness activities.
