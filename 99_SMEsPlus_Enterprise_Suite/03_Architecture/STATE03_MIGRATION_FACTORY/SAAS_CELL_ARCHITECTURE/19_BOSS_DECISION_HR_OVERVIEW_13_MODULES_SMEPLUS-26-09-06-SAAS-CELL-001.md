## Previously clarified boundaries
- Shift / Work Schedule / OT Request / OT Approval / Attendance belong under Time & Attendance.
- Leave Request / Leave Approval / Leave Balance belong under Leave.
- Approved time and leave results may feed Payroll & Benefits.
- Attendance != Timesheet.
- Actual Attendance != Approved Overtime.
- Missing Clock-out should not automatically create overtime; regular time may be capped by approved shift policy with an exception record and controlled correction/approval.
