## Fields applying uniformly to all 19 types (stated once, not repeated per row)

- **Source:** Core Accounting source `account_type` enumeration (`account_account.py` lines 44–72, Team A `SE-17`), constrained to the Boss-approved 19-value active baseline (`AJ` ruling).
- **System/control dependency:** every account carries exactly one type from this fixed set; the type drives `include_initial_balance` (year-end carry-forward behaviour) and `internal_group` (asset/liability/equity/income/expense) — Team A `SE-18`, `BR-15`, `BR-16`.
- **Base Kernel candidacy:** the *type* itself is not a kernel candidate (types are structural, not accounts); which specific accounts within each type belong to the `~32`-account working-expectation kernel is explicitly **COA-G02** scope, not decided here.
- **Canonicalization relevance:** canonical identity is the account's business meaning + type + evidence lineage — never Account Code or Account Name (SI-05, repeated across `AG`/`AJ`/`AL`/`AO`/`AP`). Template entry (Platform), Company COA Instance (Tenant/Company), and Posting Account (ledger-level) are three distinct layers per the AQ SaaS Context Clarification §3 — this document classifies at the *template-entry concept* layer only.
- **Clean-room status:** business meaning and accounting classification only; no Odoo source code, ORM field name, table name, or schema structure is asserted as SMEsPlus architecture. Where a source field name appears (e.g. `account_type`), it is cited as *evidence*, not adopted as an identifier.

## Explicit statement on evidence-strength floor

Three types — **Credit Card, Other Expenses, Off-Balance Sheet** — carry **no or minimal direct source-row observation** and exist in the 19-type target baseline purely by Boss ruling. This is not a defect: `l10n_th`/workbook omission was already ruled non-prohibitive by the AJ ruling (*"Template omission != Business prohibition"*, `c530138` §4, a statement consistent with and not contradicted by Round 1's own findings). It is recorded here so COA-G02/G03 do not mistake "Boss-required, thin evidence" for "fully source-verified" when selecting kernel candidates.

No Evidence = No Progress. Never Skip Gate. Boss is the sole Final Approver.
