# P01 — LANDED COST: 44-COMPANY DEPLOYMENT REALITY

Session: `SMEPLUS-26-09-05-…-EVIDENCE-VERSION-DEPLOYMENT-INTEGRITY-001`
Layer: **1.** Produced under an explicit **disproof** assignment.

---

## 1. VERDICT

> ### `SURVIVES — materially REFINED`, and **CONTRADICTED** in its most natural reading

The arithmetic reproduces: **43 of 44 companies cannot compute a landed-cost adjustment**,
because they have no product category on a cost method that supports one, so the computation
raises before it can produce a line.

**But three refinements change what the number means.**

---

## 2. THE DEFINITIONS THE CLAIM DEPENDED ON — NOW STATED

The claim was unusable until these were separated:

| Term | Definition used | Measured |
|---|---|---|
| **Installed** | present in the deployed module registry with installed state | **all four archives** |
| **Configured** | the prerequisites the computation needs exist: a product category on a supporting cost method, plus the valuation accounts | **1 of 44** in `E-1` |
| **Usable / reachable** | a user can open the document and save it | **44 of 44** — the menu carries no group restriction and the record saves; the block is **only at the compute step** |
| **Exercised** | landed-cost documents exist | **0 in every deployment** |

---

## 3. THE THREE REFINEMENTS

### 3.1 The denominator was ambiguous
"44 companies" did not state **which** database. Two archives each report 44 — and this session
proved they are **the same estate observed twice**. So the claim's population is one deployment's
44 companies, not two independent samples.

### 3.2 "Unusable" overstates it
The capability is reachable and the document saves. The failure is **at the compute step only**.
That is a narrower defect than *unusable*, and a different remediation.

### 3.3 **It inverts when weighted by activity** — the finding that matters
- The **one** configured company in `E-1` is **the only one of the 44 that transacts** — only 7
  of 44 have any stock movement at all.
- The **operating deployment** `E-2` is **fully configured for landed cost** — categories on
  supporting cost methods, both FIFO and average.

> **Read as *"the business cannot use landed cost"*, the claim is CONTRADICTED.** The companies
> that cannot compute it are the companies that do not transact; the deployment that does
> transact is configured for it.

---

## 4. THE ZERO THAT SURVIVES EXPLANATION — THE ACTUAL FINDING

> **Landed cost is unexercised in the operating deployment despite every prerequisite being
> satisfied — against a denominator of 103,949 stock movements.**

That is the diagnostic result, and it is **not** the 43.

Against the seven-way distinction the directive requires, this zero is:

| Candidate explanation | Assessment |
|---|---|
| No business need | **the leading candidate** — and it is a *business* fact, not a defect |
| Configuration incomplete | **excluded** — the operating deployment is configured |
| Module unavailable | **excluded** — installed everywhere |
| Workflow unreachable | **excluded** — reachable; the document saves |
| Data absent | **excluded** — 103,949 movements |
| Deployment policy | **UNKNOWN — class D** |
| Unknown | residual |

**A zero that survives every mechanical explanation points at a business fact.** P01 records it
as such and does **not** conclude the feature is broken.

---

## 5. THE DEFECTS REMAIN — AND ARE NOW CORRECTLY RANKED AS LATENT

The landed-cost defects previously recorded — the later series booking only the on-hand portion
and silently producing nothing when the goods are already sold, and a commented-out cost-price
update — are **unaffected by this refinement**. They are **latent**: installed everywhere,
exercised nowhere, and therefore never yet able to cause harm.
