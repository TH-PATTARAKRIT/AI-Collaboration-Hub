# Boss Gate

Purpose: Store the Boss's explicit domain-level Gate decision after Team A evidence, ChatGPT independent audit, and PMO verification.

Boss is the sole Final Approver.

Recommended artifact naming:
`DOMAIN_{NN}_{DOMAIN_NAME}_D_BOSS_GATE_DECISION.md`

Allowed decisions may include:
- APPROVE HANDOFF TO TEAM B
- CONDITIONAL APPROVE
- HOLD
- REJECT
- RETURN FOR REVISION

No downstream Team B design work may treat a domain as approved without the corresponding Boss Gate decision artifact.
