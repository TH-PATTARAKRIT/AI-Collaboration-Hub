## 4. Statutory vs. Management Boundary — Explicit Statement

- **Statutory financial accounting** (IAS/TAS 16 depreciation, IAS/TAS 2 overhead absorption) governs what appears in the fixed-asset register, the balance sheet, and the P&L's depreciation/cost-of-sales lines. Nothing in files `13` or `14` (post-depreciation internal usage cost, off-balance mechanism) is a statutory accounting concept — both are explicitly management/internal costing concepts with no standards-level basis, and must never be represented as satisfying any IAS/TAS 16 or TAS 2 requirement.
- **Management/cost accounting** (Hypothesis A/B/C, the post-depreciation formula, off-balance ledger) is a legitimate but separate discipline with no single governing standard equivalent to IAS 16 — its soundness is judged by internal consistency and usefulness, not standards compliance, and this file does not attempt to certify it against any accounting standard because no such standard applies to it.
