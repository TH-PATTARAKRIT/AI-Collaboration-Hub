CONTRADICTED`. Boundary of my finding: `addons/account_reports/` only.
- **CLASSIFICATION.** `VERIFIED FACT` as to the existence of the mechanism and its rate source. `INFERENCE` as to the consequence (that revaluation cannot repair a par-valued item because it re-derives from the same absent rate).
- **REQUIRED RESOLUTION.** C06 §7 must be corrected: the row is not "no mechanism exists" but "a mechanism exists and is **subject to the same defect**". `GAP-H01` should be reclassified from a gap to a **contaminated control**. The reviewer notes this change *strengthens* C06's `REJECT` recommendation while invalidating one of its stated facts.
