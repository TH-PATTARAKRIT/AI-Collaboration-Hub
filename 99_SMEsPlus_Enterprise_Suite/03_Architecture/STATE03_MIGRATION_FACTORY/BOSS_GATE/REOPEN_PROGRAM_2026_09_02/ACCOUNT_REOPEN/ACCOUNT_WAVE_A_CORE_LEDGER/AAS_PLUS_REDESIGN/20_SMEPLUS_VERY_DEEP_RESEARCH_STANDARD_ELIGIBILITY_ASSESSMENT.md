## 3. The method result that should survive regardless

The parent stated it and this assessment endorses it as the transferable finding:

> ### A denominator is `POPULATION` + `PATTERN` + `PATH SET` + `UNIT`, and none of the four may be chosen by the author of the claim it bounds.

`GB-04` learned *population*. `GB-07` learned *pattern*. The closure round learned *path set* — twice,
once against itself — and *unit*. **The fifth clause, independence, is the one never implemented, and
it is why the other four keep failing.**

The supporting fact is stark: **five consecutive rounds, every material correction came from an
independent reviewer and none from the author** — including the round convened to diagnose that
pattern, and the round convened to close that round. In the closure round the author caught **1 of its
own 4 errors**, and the reviewer brief the author wrote **contained a wrong path that only a reviewer
noticed**.

> **A control designed by the author is not independent of the author.**
