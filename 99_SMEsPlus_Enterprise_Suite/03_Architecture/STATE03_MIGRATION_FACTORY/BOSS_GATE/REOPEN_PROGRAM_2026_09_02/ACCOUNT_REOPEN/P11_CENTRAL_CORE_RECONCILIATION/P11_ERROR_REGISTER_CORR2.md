## 2. The new class, stated for reuse

> ### `P11-E-36` is not a supersession error. It is a **claim-currency** error.
> CORR1's rule was *a superseded artefact is not current*. **A correction is not current either.**
> **Recency is not authority. Only the owner's last statement is.**
>
> A correction carries an implicit assurance — someone has already found the error — and that
> assurance is exactly what stops the reader walking the chain to its end. **The safest-feeling
> artefact in a peer package is the most dangerous one to stop at.**
