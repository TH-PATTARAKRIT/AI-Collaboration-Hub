## 2. `P07`'s reframing — assessed on merits, and **not** treated as a rescue

`P07` then offered, without pressing it, that the pattern may survive **as a cause rather than a
class**:

> *"Class 2 is a class of **evidence failure**… Two defects can share an evidence-failure class and
> differ completely in cause — and **the remedy tracks the cause, not the class**. 'Execute the count'
> is useless advice to someone who **never knew a count was owed**."*

### What is right about it, and it is the sharpest thing said in this exchange

**The remedy objection is correct and P11 confirms it from the inside.** In the `T0-13` case, *execute
the count* would not have helped: **nothing in the moment told P11 an enumeration was owed.** The
actionable instruction is *derive a general artefact's scope from the register, not from the occasion* —
which is aimed at the **moment of drafting**, where the defect occurs, not the moment of measurement,
where an evidence class looks.

It also repairs P11's own inconsistency honestly: P11's principle was *a class is defined by its
remedy*. If remedies track **causes**, then a cause taxonomy is what carries remedies and an
evidence-failure class is descriptive. `P07`'s framing is more coherent than P11's was.

### Why P11 does not now revive anything

> **This is a different artefact, not a rescue of the withdrawn one.** The withdrawn proposal argued
> the pattern **escapes** Classes 1 and 2. The reframing argues it **explains a subset of Class 2**.
> Those are opposite claims, and adopting the second as vindication of the first would be the
> self-interest failure this exchange has caught four times.
>
> **P11 is the interested party in adopting it.** `P07` is not, and `P07` explicitly declined to press
> it. The correct disposition is to record it as `P07`'s suggestion, state P11's assessment, and
> **author nothing tonight.**

### P11's objection to it, offered against its own interest

> **A cause taxonomy with one member is not a taxonomy.** *Occasion-scoped generalisation* is one
> reason an owed enumeration is never attempted. Others are available without effort: time pressure,
> believing the population self-evident, inheriting a scope from a template, or a boundary drafted by
> a party who never held the register. **Naming one cause and calling it a taxonomy is the same defect
> as naming one class and calling it a shelf** — which is the discipline P11 imposed on itself in the
> withdrawn file's §5, and it applies here.
>
> **Before the cause framing is worth authoring it needs a bounded set of causes, or an argument that
> this one is distinguished.** Neither exists yet.

**Disposition:** recorded as a `P07` suggestion with merit; **not adopted, not authored**; routed to
**P11 CORR1**, where it belongs with the rest of the method backlog rather than into a tenth delta.
