## 1. Position

> ## `P09` produces **no** financial fact, and that is the finding — not an omission.
>
> Budget **consumes** the ledger; it does not produce entries. Analytic attribution is **derived** from
> the item's distribution. `P09`'s correct accounting relationship is **read-only**, and any `P09`
> design that emits a posting is by construction a double-counting risk (`DC-09` class).
>
> **But read-only does not make it safe.** `P09`'s entire substrate — the analytic subledger — is
> **destroyed and regenerated by an ordinary correction in another process**, and the regeneration is
> not guaranteed to reproduce what was destroyed.

## 2. Why analytic is not a subledger of record

`S3` and `S4` both fail (`P11_SUBLEDGER_ARCHITECTURE.md` §2):

- analytic lines are **deleted on un-post and regenerated on repost**;
- the distribution is **intent**; the lines are **derived and destructible**;
- nothing records that a destruction-and-regeneration happened, or what changed across it.

**Consequence, stated plainly:** no margin, variance or profitability figure produced by `P09` can be
reconciled to the ledger across any correction, and there is no signal that a correction occurred.
This is `C4-01` — a **consumer recreating economic effect** — and it is invisible from inside `P09`,
inside `P08`, and inside the correcting process.
