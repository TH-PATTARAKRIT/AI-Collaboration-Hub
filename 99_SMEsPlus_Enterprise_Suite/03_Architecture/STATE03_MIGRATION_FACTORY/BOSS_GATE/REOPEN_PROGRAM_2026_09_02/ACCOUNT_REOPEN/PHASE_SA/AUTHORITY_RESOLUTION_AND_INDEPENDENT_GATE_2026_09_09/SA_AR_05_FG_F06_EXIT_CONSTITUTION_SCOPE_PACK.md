## 3. READING B — IT DOES NOT BIND PHASE SA

### 3.1 Every operative noun is "Module" or "State". Phase SA is neither.

§3: *"**A Module or State** may advance only when ALL eight criteria are satisfied."*
§4 is titled **Module Exit Rule**; §5 **State Exit Rule**.
§11's two operative rules are: *"NO **MODULE** MAY ADVANCE TO THE NEXT CONTROLLED **STATE**…"* and
*"NO **STATE** MAY ADVANCE TO THE NEXT STATE…"*

**Phase SA is a phase inside `STATE03`, and it is not a Module.** The Modules in this programme are
Account, Inventory, Manufacturing and the rest; Phase SA is a cross-module assurance activity that
serves them. §4's *"next controlled phase or State"* is the **Module's** next phase — the subject of
the sentence is *No Module*. **On a strict reading, Phase SA has no exit clause in this constitution.**

### 3.2 The constitution is scoped to Very Deep **Research**, and designation is Boss's act

The title, the ID (`DR-EXIT`), and every criterion are written for research programmes. §2 item 5
reads: *"All future research programmes **designated by Boss** as Very Deep Research."*

**Searched: no artefact records Boss designating Phase SA as Very Deep Research.** The two hits that
come closest are a *return* to targeted Very Deep Research for a specific function, and one CORR3
sub-study self-labelled as targeted Very Deep Research. **Neither is a Boss designation of Phase SA.**
If designation is the trigger, it has not occurred.

### 3.3 Phase SA has its own explicit gate rules, and they have governed six rounds

Every Phase SA prompt sets its own terminal conditions, its own checkpoint ladder, its own prohibitions
and its own Boss-gate rule. **Six consecutive rounds — CORR1 through the Final Boss Gate — ran under
those rules and none invoked `EC-07`.** A constitution that was understood to govern this exit would be
expected to appear in the exit rules of the exits it governs.

### 3.4 `AR-F-02` — the single precedent applies §9, not §4

> **The one Phase SA citation is narrower than it has been read as being.**

`SA_CORR3_07` §2.5 grades the conformance package **`PROVISIONAL / NON-CANONICAL`**. That phrase is
**§9, the AAS+ / Design Handoff Rule** — *"AAS+ may explore designs in parallel only as `PROVISIONAL /
NON-CANONICAL` while parent Very Deep Research is not yet complete"* — **not §4's Module Exit Rule and
not `EC-07`.**

**Applying one clause of a constitution is not adopting it as your exit gate.** The parent round cited
this precedent as evidence the constitution reaches Phase SA; it evidences that **§9** reaches a Phase
SA design package. **On `EC-07` the precedent is silent.** Confirmed by search: the constitution ID
appears in **zero** Phase SA artefacts, and the concept appears in exactly one CORR-series artefact,
that one.

### 3.5 Governance risk if Reading B is right and Reading A was followed

Phase SA holds indefinitely for two independent passes that no rule required, at the cost of an
appointed challenger and two full review cycles — and it sets a precedent that any project-wide
research instrument silently governs every architecture phase, which would reach far beyond Phase SA.

**Dissent, recorded against the recommendation:**

3. **`EC-07` may be unsatisfiable as written for this phase.** It requires two consecutive passes with

   `AR-F-01` and `AR-F-02` — has produced one. **Boss should know that adopting `EC-07` may mean more
