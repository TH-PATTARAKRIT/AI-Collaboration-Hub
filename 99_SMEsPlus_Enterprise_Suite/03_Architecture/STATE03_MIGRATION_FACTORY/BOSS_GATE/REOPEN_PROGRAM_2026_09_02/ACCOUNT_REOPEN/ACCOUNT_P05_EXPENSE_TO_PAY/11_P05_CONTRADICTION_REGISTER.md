# 11 — P05 CONTRADICTION REGISTER

`LAYER 2 — AUDIT QUARANTINE`
Per `EC-05`, every material contradiction is dispositioned with traceable evidence and lineage.
**No material contradiction may remain merely as an unresolved difference of opinion.**

Three kinds are recorded: **(A)** the reference contradicting itself, **(B)** an independent reviewer
contradicting the primary research, **(C)** the primary research contradicting its own earlier
position. All three are resolved by evidence, not by seniority.
