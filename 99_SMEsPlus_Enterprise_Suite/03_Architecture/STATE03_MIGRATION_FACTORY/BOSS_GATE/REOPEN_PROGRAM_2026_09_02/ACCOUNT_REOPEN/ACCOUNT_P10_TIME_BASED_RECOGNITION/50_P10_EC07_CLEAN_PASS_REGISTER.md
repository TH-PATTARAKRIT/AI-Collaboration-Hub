## 2. What Constitutes a Clean Pass — the six failure conditions, made operable

| # | Condition | Operable test | Does a method-level defect trigger it? |
|---|-----------|---------------|----------------------------------------|
| 1 | New material population | The enumeration population moved | **Yes** — a population defect *is* a population change |
| 2 | New material finding class | A finding of a kind not previously carried | Yes, if the method defect produces one |
| 3 | New gating unknown | An unknown added and classed gating | Yes |
| 4 | Reopened tolerance-zero issue | A closed or partial tolerance-zero component reopens | Yes |
| 5 | New Gate-changing contradiction | A contradiction whose disposition changes a criterion | Yes |
| 6 | **Evidence-integrity failure** | The evidence base is found to have been misstated | **This is the explicit method-defect trigger** |

**Answering the directive's two questions directly:**

- **Does a method-level defect reset the counter?** **Yes**, via condition 6. An evidence-integrity failure is named in the criterion.
- **Does an evidence-base correction reset the counter?** **Yes**, via conditions 1 and 6. A corrected population *is* a moved population.

Neither is a P10 interpretation stretched to fit; both are the criterion's own words applied to the events.

## 3. Population Scanned by a Pass

A pass must scan: every Layer 1 finding still carried; every negative claim and its class; every enumeration and its declared population, pattern, unit and path set; every peer-sourced claim and the peer's status field; every tolerance-zero component; and the evidence base itself.

**The last two were not in scope for rounds 1 and 2.** A pass that did not scan them cannot be counted as clean for conditions 4 and 6, whatever it reported.

## 6. The Required Method for a Genuine Clean Pass

1. **Freeze the package.** A pass over a moving package measures nothing.
2. **Fresh reviewers** who did not author and did not review the previous pass.
3. **All six challenge classes** of `47` §2, including decision authority — the class that caught this round's governance breach and that rounds 1 and 2 did not have.
4. **Each of the six failure conditions explicitly tested and reported**, not inferred from the absence of complaints.
5. **Reviewer findings independently verified** before acceptance — `Independent Review != Truth`. This round verified one reviewer's whole-estate negative and **corrected it**.
6. **No self-certification.** The pass's own report states which conditions it tested and which it did not.
