## 4. The Defect That Recurred

`P10-R-08` was recorded in the parent round: an evidence-base limitation asserted without a search. The correcting document then **committed the same defect twice** — declaring an archive unreadable after running one tool, and declaring the veto immune to evidence with no search.

PMO's assessment: the original lesson was written as an exhortation ("test the evidence base before declaring it") and exhortations do not bind. The replacement is a **checkable rule**:

> A negative about your own capability must name the tool, its version, the search for alternatives, and the command output.

And a second, from `34` `W-34`:

> An artefact that was extracted and sized has not been read. A byte-size control proves the extraction is not empty; it proves nothing about whether anyone looked inside. **Print the column that answers the question, or record that you did not.**
