# VERY DEEP RESEARCH RE-ENTRY PROTOCOL

Status: BOSS APPROVED  
Authority: Boss — Sole Final Approver

## Purpose

Phase SA is not a point of no return. If a functional area, accounting behavior, control, edge case, business semantic, or clean-room rationale remains unclear, incomplete, contradictory, insufficiently evidenced, or too dependent on a reference architecture, Boss may direct a return to Very Deep Research.

## Re-entry triggers

Re-entry is appropriate when one or more of the following is true:

- the function is not understood deeply enough to design safely;
- business semantics remain unclear;
- evidence conflicts materially;
- the proposed design is too close to a reference implementation without independent justification;
- an important edge case is unproven;
- accounting correctness or statutory meaning is uncertain;
- Tenant/Company isolation is uncertain;
- control/audit behavior is not clean enough;
- the Phase SA team cannot explain what SMEsPlus is doing better or differently;
- Boss requests deeper learning before approving the design.

## Scope rule

Re-entry is targeted by default.

It does NOT reset the whole Account programme and does NOT erase Phase S evidence. The affected function returns to research with:

- prior evidence preserved;
- prior decisions preserved unless a material delta challenges them;
- exact unresolved question(s);
- exact evidence gap(s);
- exact clean-room risk(s);
- defined exit criteria.

Boss may explicitly authorize broader re-entry when the new evidence shows the problem is systemic.

## Research depth

The re-entry team may study source code, database, UI, runtime behavior, reference systems, documentation, experiments and alternative ERP patterns as necessary. The objective is comprehensive understanding, not source replication.

## Exit back to Phase SA

The function returns to Phase SA only when:

1. the material unknowns are resolved or defensibly bounded;
2. evidence provenance is explicit;
3. source behavior is separated from business meaning;
4. alternatives have been considered;
5. a clean-room SMEsPlus design rationale can be stated;
6. remaining Boss-only decisions are explicit;
7. the handoff is independently reviewable.

## Permanent rule

**Very Deep Research is an authorized learning loop, not a project reset.**

Phase SA may send a function back to research as many times as necessary to reach sufficient understanding and clean-room confidence. No team may treat schedule pressure as authority to design through a material unknown.
