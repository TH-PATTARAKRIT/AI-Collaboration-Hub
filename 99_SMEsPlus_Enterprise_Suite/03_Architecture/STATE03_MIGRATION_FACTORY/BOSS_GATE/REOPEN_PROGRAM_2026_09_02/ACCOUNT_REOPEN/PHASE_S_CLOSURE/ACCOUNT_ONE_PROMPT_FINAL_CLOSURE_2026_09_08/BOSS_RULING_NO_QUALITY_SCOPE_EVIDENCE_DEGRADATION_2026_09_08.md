## RULING

The move to `ONE PROMPT / ONE ACCOUNT TEAM / ZERO HANDOFF LOOP` changes **execution orchestration only**.

It does **NOT** reduce, waive, compress, downgrade, or supersede any previously required:
- Account research scope already approved;
- business/accounting coverage;
- SaaS / tenant / company isolation controls;
- evidence population and reproducibility requirements;
- correction requirements;
- challenge findings already accepted;
- verification criteria;
- Veto conditions;
- cross-package reconciliation obligations;
- Phase S closure criteria;
- Phase SA Input / Process / Output / Handoff readiness requirements;
- audit lineage preservation;
- Boss Final Gate authority.

## NON-DEGRADATION PRINCIPLE

> **FASTER CLOSURE MUST NOT MEAN LESS WORK, LESS EVIDENCE, LESS COVERAGE, OR LOWER QUALITY.**
>
> **The required output must remain equivalent to, or stronger than, the previously governed Account outcome.**
>
> **Only unnecessary waiting, duplicate questioning, duplicate full re-audits, repeated prompt/session creation, and ownership handoff loops are removed.**

## ACCEPTANCE TEST

When Boss performs detailed review, the Account closure package must be able to prove that:
1. every previously authorized material work item is either completed, explicitly superseded with evidence, or carried as an exact external dependency;
2. no prior accepted finding disappeared merely because the workflow was simplified;
3. no evidence requirement was replaced by narrative assertion;
4. no Gate was skipped;
5. no Veto was silently discharged;
6. no cross-package dependency was lost;
7. all changed material surfaces were tested;
8. final Phase SA readiness contains the same or stronger Input / Process / Output / Handoff clarity expected under the prior execution model;
9. historical evidence and superseded statements remain traceable;
10. Boss can reconstruct why every final disposition was reached.

If any of the above cannot be proven, the one-prompt closure is **NOT ACCEPTABLE** and must not be presented as equivalent completion.

`No Evidence = No Progress.`
