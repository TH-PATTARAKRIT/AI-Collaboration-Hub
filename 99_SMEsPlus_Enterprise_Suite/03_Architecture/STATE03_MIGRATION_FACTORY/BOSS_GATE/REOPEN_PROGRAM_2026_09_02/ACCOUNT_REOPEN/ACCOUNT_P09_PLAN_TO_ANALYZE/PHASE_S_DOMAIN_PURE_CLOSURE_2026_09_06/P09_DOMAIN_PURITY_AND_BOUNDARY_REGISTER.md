## 1. THE PROBLEM DOMAIN PURITY CREATES FOR P09 SPECIFICALLY

P09 is the domain most exposed to contamination in the whole programme, and the reason is structural rather than accidental.

**The management dimension is written into by everyone.** Assets, manufacturing, sales, expenses, timesheets, inventory and accruals all author management records. P09 owns the *dimension*; it does not own the *events*. So almost every interesting P09 question — *why does depreciation net to zero on a cost centre?* — has its mechanism inside somebody else's domain.

The programme's four earlier P09 rounds followed those mechanisms deep into adjacent domains. Under Phase S that is now contamination. **This round therefore had to hold a boundary the earlier rounds did not have.**

**This is not a repudiation of the earlier work.** It was correctly authorised when it ran. What changes is what P09 may *newly research*, not what it already knows.

## 3. THE HARDEST CALL — WHY DP-03 WAS STOPPED RATHER THAN EXTENDED

The zeroing finding is this programme's most quoted P09 result, and the obvious next step is to check the remaining symmetric mechanisms in the deployments now known to exist.

**That step is forbidden here, and correctly so.** Each of those mechanisms lives in an adjacent domain — assets, accruals, deferred recognition, manufacturing. Following them is precisely the contamination the prompt names. **The finding P09 is entitled to keep is the one stated in its own terms:**

> *An allocation applied to every row of a balanced set annihilates itself, because the management amount is the negated row balance. Which producers do that is an adjacent-domain question.*

**The arithmetic is P09's. The producers are not.**

## 6. A DISSENT THIS REGISTER RECORDS AGAINST ITSELF

Domain purity has a cost, and it should be stated rather than smoothed.

**P09's most decision-relevant findings are boundary findings.** The zeroing result, the ledger-less management records, the financial-surface shadowing — all three are about what crosses between P09 and its neighbours. A rule that forbids following those crossings makes P09's *own* package cleaner while leaving the programme's real exposure to be assembled later, by someone who must hold all eleven domains at once.

That is a deliberate sequencing choice by the Boss, and it is not P09's to overturn. **But P09 records that its bounded closure is not the same thing as its risk being bounded**, and that PHASE SA and PHASE B inherit that gap intact.
