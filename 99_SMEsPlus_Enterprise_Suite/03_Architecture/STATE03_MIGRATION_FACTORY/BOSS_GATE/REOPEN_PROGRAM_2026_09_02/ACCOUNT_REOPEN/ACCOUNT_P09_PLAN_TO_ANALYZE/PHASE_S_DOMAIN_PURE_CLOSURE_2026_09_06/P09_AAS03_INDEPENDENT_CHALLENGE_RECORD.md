## 3. THE PURITY BREACH THE PURITY REGISTER COULD NOT SEE

A module this package declared **P09-owned** extends **two purchase-domain models**, and the commitment amount P09 declared an *external* input is computed **inside that module**.

**The package cannot hold both positions.** Either the P09-owned surface must be declared at **file** granularity rather than module granularity, or `CI-06`, `CH-03` and `DP-01` are not external requirements.

And the purity scoreboard could not detect this, because **every row of it counts an author-chosen *act*** — paths I stopped, HOLDs I did not reinterpret — and none tests the *content* of the adjacent-domain assertions the package nonetheless carries. *"Paths stopped = 5"* is unfalsifiable from outside: a reader can only count the paths the author elected to name.

**Adopted:** the domain-purity verdict is **`NOT DECIDABLE` on the instrument used**, and `CONTRADICTED` on the narrow claim that the declared module surface is P09-pure.
