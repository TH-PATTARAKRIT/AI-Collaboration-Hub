## 3. THE FINDING NO SINGLE CHALLENGE PRODUCED

**AAS+-S-1 — The round's controls failed in the same shape as the defect they were built to catch.**

The census round ended by writing two rules: **publish what you processed against what your search returned** (`NC-12`), and **give every artefact its own positive control** (`NC-13`). This round then:

- ran a positive control that **fired in two of its four modules** and reported it as validating all four — an `NC-13` failure;
- ran a generation control that **compared a four-module population against a population excluding the module carrying the feature** — a denominator failure inside a control;
- ran a generation control that **re-published a wrong state enumeration as invariant**, converting a miscount into a claim of robustness.

**A control that repeats an error confirms the error.** The rules were written by this author one round earlier and were not applied to this author's own next round. **AAS+ regards this as the programme's most durable finding about itself**: the ratio of self-caught to externally-caught errors did not improve when the rule was written down. It improved only in the round where the author ran an adversarial control *against their own instrument* — and that round is the one that produced `NC-12`/`NC-13`.

**AAS+-S-2 — The `TERMINAL MANAGEMENT OUTPUT` label did boundary work it had not earned.** Declaring three outputs terminal exempted them from contract scope, while the same package sent two of them to a peer with a required sign convention. *Who consumes a figure* is not *what boundary the figure crosses*. This is a conceptual error, not a counting one, and it would have propagated into PHASE SA as a settled boundary.

**AAS+-S-3 — The one thing the round did better than any predecessor: it refused to assert a generation it could not establish, and published a control instead.** Three experts noted this independently. **That instinct is correct and should be the programme's default** — it is also, precisely, what was *not* done for the population.
