## 1. THE ATTACK I RAN AGAINST MY OWN CLAIM

`D01` states: *"17,404 of 17,405 paired entries net exactly 0.00 — 99.99 %."* That is an **entry-level** statement.

**Steelman:** if the two legs of an entry carry **different** analytic accounts, the *entry* nets zero while the *cost centres* do not — one gains, another loses. The headline would then be true and misleading.

**I tested it. The steelman is partly right.**

## 3. WHAT THIS CHANGES

| Statement | Status |
|---|---|
| entry-level net is 0.00 on 17,404 of 17,405 | **CONFIRMED — unchanged** |

> **A third failure mode, not previously described: in 977 entries the two legs land on DIFFERENT cost centres.** The entry balances; one cost centre is charged and a different one is credited. **Aggregate analysis cannot see this at all** — it nets to zero across the population while misattributing at every individual cost centre.

## 4. WHY IT HAPPENS — NOT SETTLED

Candidates, none adjudicated: the asset's allocation was changed between postings and only draft entries were rewritten; the two legs derived their allocations independently under the no-allocation branch; or an analytic account was re-pointed to another plan. **`UNRESOLVED — EVIDENCE REQUIRED`**, routed to **P04**.

> **Entry-level: 17,404 of 17,405 net exactly 0.00 (99.99 %).**
> **Cost-centre level: 16,427 of 17,405 fully annihilate (94.38 %); 978 entries leave a non-zero effect on an individual cost centre, and 977 of those are a redistribution between two cost centres that is invisible in any aggregate.**

**Both figures must travel together. Quoting only the first repeats the defect this round exists to fix — a true number in the wrong unit.**
