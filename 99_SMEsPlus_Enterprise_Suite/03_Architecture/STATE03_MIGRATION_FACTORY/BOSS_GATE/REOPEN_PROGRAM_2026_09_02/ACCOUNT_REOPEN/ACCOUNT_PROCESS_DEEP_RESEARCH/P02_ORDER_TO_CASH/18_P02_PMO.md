## 2. The Finding That Should Govern The Boss Decision

Stripped of everything else:

> **In the reference process the physical event is immutable, the accounting event is reversible, and the
> settlement history is freely destructible — including across a closed period. The durability ordering is
> exactly inverted relative to accounting importance.**

And, from the deployed database, the finding a Boss decision should weigh alongside it:

> **In a live Thai deployment the invoice-side cost mechanism has never run. Cost is recognised at delivery
> and revenue at invoice, with no position connecting them — so any shipment billed in a later period puts
> cost and revenue in different periods permanently, and nothing in the system can detect it.**

A completed delivery cannot be cancelled or reversed. A posted invoice can be reset to draft, which
**destroys its cost lines** and lets them re-derive at a different value. Matching rows — the record of
*what settled what* — can be created and destroyed with **no lock-date check at all**.

Every tolerance-zero candidate in §3 is an instance of that inversion.
