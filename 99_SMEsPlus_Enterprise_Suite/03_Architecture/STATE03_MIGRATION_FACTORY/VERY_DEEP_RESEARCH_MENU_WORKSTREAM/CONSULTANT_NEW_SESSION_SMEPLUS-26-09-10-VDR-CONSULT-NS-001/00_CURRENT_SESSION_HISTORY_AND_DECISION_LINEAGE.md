## 8. Multi-Layer Primary VDR — Approved
Boss explicitly approved stronger multi-layer verification because exposing more Gaps earlier allows correction earlier.

Primary VDR therefore contains multiple distinct internal passes while remaining one governed Primary VDR cycle:

`PASS A — Normal Process / Happy Path`
`PASS B — Data / State / Validation`
`PASS C — Roles / Controls / Audit`
`PASS D — Cross-Module / Cross-Domain Relationships`
`PASS E — Exception / Failure / Reversal / Concurrency`
`PASS F — SaaS / Tenant / Multi-Company / Company Context`
`PASS G — Migration / Historical / Reconciliation`
`PASS H — UX / Menu / Field / Interaction Implications`
`PASS I — Adversarial Missing-Scenario Attack`
`PASS J — Contradiction / Evidence Reconciliation`

Additional specialist passes are allowed when the bounded subject requires them.

Every layer must have a distinct Proof Obligation.

Anti-loop controls:
- `No repeated question without material delta.`
- Reuse verified evidence.
- Preserve evidence lineage.
- No arbitrary full VDR #2/#3/#4 rerun.
- Unknowns may not be silently omitted.
- Material Delta reopens affected scope only unless evidence proves broader invalidity.

## 9. Research Contract, Completeness Matrix and Research Sufficiency / Saturation Gate
Before Primary VDR begins, the responsible Domain Team must create a Research Contract.

The Research Contract must define at minimum:
- Research Subject ID
- bounded scope / explicit out-of-scope
- business purpose and lifecycle
- upstream/downstream dependencies
- existing evidence and Boss decisions to reuse
- evidence expectations
- Proof Obligations
- coverage domains
- known assumptions / unknowns
- cross-module checks
- Accounting / Tax / Control impact where applicable
- SaaS / Tenant / Organization / Company / Branch impact
- Security / privacy / permission impact
- Migration / historical behavior
- UX / Figma implications
- independent challenge requirements
- completion / saturation criteria

Before research exits, a Completeness Matrix must expose all material coverage states, including:
- VERIFIED
- PARTIALLY VERIFIED
- OPEN
- CONTRADICTION
- INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE
- OUT OF SCOPE
- DEFERRED WITH OWNER / TRIGGER

Research Sufficiency Gate dispositions:
- PASS
- CONDITIONAL PASS
- HOLD
- TARGETED RESEARCH REQUIRED

`PROMPT FINISHED != RESEARCH COMPLETE.`
