### `PMOM-02` — the fixture result is not what it was reported to be

land on identities that are not code paths at all.** The programme's cancel and reverse figures rest on

### `PMOM-05` — the headline agreement figure has no declared version basis
49.5% mixes three platform generations against one source tree; **53.9% on a constant basis.** 765
runtime-only rows exist only on off-version deployments and are scored as disagreement.

### `PMOM-06` — a defect narrative unsupported by its own artefacts
The harness's inline account of the v1 fixture failure is contradicted by the preserved v1 result. **The
correction is adopted and the preserved results stand as the record.**

## 5. Why DENIED rather than HOLD

**HOLD** would say the instruments are incomplete. They are not merely incomplete:

- the control that was supposed to make self-reference impossible **cannot fail**;
- two instruments **cannot fire** on the population they were built for;
- one has **half-wrong** output rates that nobody had measured;
- the fixture suite **checks one verdict in seven**.

**That is a failed foundation, not an unfinished one**, and calling it HOLD would understate what an
independent validator found.
