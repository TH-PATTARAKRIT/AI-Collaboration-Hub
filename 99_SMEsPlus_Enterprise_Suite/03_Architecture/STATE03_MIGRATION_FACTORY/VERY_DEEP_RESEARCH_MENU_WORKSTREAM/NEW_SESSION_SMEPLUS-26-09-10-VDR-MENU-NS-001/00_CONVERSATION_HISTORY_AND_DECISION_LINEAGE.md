## 7. SMEs Core Recommendation — Avoid Repeating Full Very Deep Research
Boss asked SMEs Core, Consultant and Secretary to strengthen the method so SMEsPlus does not need to repeat full Very Deep Research many times.

The accepted solution was to make the first bounded-subject research cycle substantially deeper and multi-layered, then perform only targeted research where evidence later proves a Gap.

Approved strategy:

`ONE PRIMARY DEEP BASELINE — MANY TARGETED DELTAS, NOT MANY FULL RESTARTS.`

Three research modes were established:

### PRIMARY VDR
Full-scope deep study of a new bounded subject before Phase S.

### TARGETED DELTA VDR
Narrow research triggered by a proven Gap, contradiction, unknown, new evidence, new requirement or downstream challenge.

### RE-VALIDATION VDR
Broader revalidation only when Material Delta makes the prior baseline unreliable across a wider scope.

## 8. Approved Primary VDR Internal Layers
Boss explicitly approved strong multi-layer checking because the more rigorously SMEsPlus checks, the earlier it exposes Gaps and the faster it can correct them before downstream propagation.

Primary VDR therefore contains multiple distinct passes while remaining one governed research cycle:

`PASS A — Normal Process / Happy Path`
`PASS B — Data / State / Validation`
`PASS C — Roles / Controls / Audit`
`PASS D — Cross-Module / Cross-Domain Relationships`
`PASS E — Exception / Failure / Reversal / Concurrency`
`PASS F — SaaS / Tenant / Multi-Company / Company Context`
`PASS G — Migration / Historical / Reconciliation`
`PASS H — UX / Menu / Field / Interaction Implications`
`PASS I — Adversarial Missing-Scenario Attack`
`PASS J — Contradiction / Evidence Reconciliation`

Additional specialist passes are permitted when required, but every layer must have a distinct Proof Obligation.

Anti-loop controls:
- `No repeated question without material delta.`
- Existing verified evidence must be reused and preserved.
- No arbitrary full VDR #2/#3/#4 restart.
- Unknowns may not be silently omitted.
- Material Delta reopens only affected scope unless evidence proves the broader baseline invalid.

## 9. Research Contract + Completeness Matrix + Saturation Gate
Before a Primary VDR starts, the responsible SMEs Core Domain Team must create a Research Contract defining:
- bounded subject;
- business purpose and lifecycle;
- dependencies;
- evidence expectations;
- Proof Obligations;
- coverage domains;
- known assumptions / unknowns;
- cross-module checks;
- expected Figma implications;
- explicit out-of-scope;
- saturation criteria;
- independent challenge requirements.

Before Primary VDR exits, a Completeness Matrix must classify material coverage, including:
- VERIFIED
- PARTIALLY VERIFIED
- OPEN
- CONTRADICTION
- INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE
- OUT OF SCOPE
- DEFERRED WITH OWNER / TRIGGER

The Research Sufficiency / Saturation Gate may dispose:
- PASS
- CONDITIONAL PASS
- HOLD
- TARGETED RESEARCH REQUIRED

`PROMPT FINISHED != RESEARCH COMPLETE.`
