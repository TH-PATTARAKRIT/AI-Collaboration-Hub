## 3. What PMO records against this round

### `PMO5-01` — a retraction was itself wrong, and the disproof was inside the package
PREP-004 retracted the premise *"current-generation deployments run periodic valuation"* on the strength
of a category property. That property is **company-dependent**, was set for **one company of 44**, and
the governing value is the company setting: **periodic on 44 of 44**.

**The file that disproves it — the company records — was extracted by that round, hashed, shipped, and
never read.** Not missing. Unexamined.

**The premise is restored.** A retraction that reverses a correct finding is the most expensive kind of
error this programme can make, because it is published with the confidence of a correction.

### `PMO5-02` — a positive control that measured the same negative twice
The accounting-link finding offered as its control *"could the column ever hold a value? 0 of 14,441."*
Both measurements were taken in the same configuration, in which the writer's precondition —
**0 of 525 locations carry a valuation account** — cannot be satisfied. **The control could not fire, and
a fourth route existed that no per-movement query could reach.**

### `PMO5-03` — the population is incomplete at hop zero
**652 field records and 138 action records on live deployments, declared by the domain's own modules, on
objects the population itself calls owned, are carried by nothing.** 251 models declared by those
modules are in neither set. **39 installed modules have no source in the declared path set at all.**

The boundary debate of the last two rounds was about hop 1 and hop 2. **The gap is at hop 0**, and no
hop rule reaches it.

### `PMO5-04` — both of the programme's closures are withdrawn
One was closed at roughly a quarter of the measurable population. One named no element a reviewer could
locate. **Neither withdrawal was forced by a number; both came from a reviewer going to the deployment's
own records.**

### `PMO5-05` — a categorical negative about the package's own capability, untested
*"No amount of further extraction will make these database records."* **415 of 1,144 are reachable** — 411
gated view elements are in the deployments' stored view definitions, a table never extracted.

## 5. Why DENIED rather than HOLD

**HOLD** says the evidence is incomplete and the process is sound. Three of the five findings above are
about the process:

- a retraction published on evidence the round held and did not read;
- a control that could not fire, offered as proof;
- a categorical negative about its own capability, untested.

**Those are not gaps in the domain. They are gaps in method**, and each was found by someone else.

## 6. What must be true before certification is reconsidered

1. The population re-derived **from what the domain's own modules declare**, not only from the relational graph — the hop-0 surface.
2. The boundary class rule replaced by something that is not ownership-based exclusion.
3. Configuration and optional function measured **per element**, joined to the process instrument that now exists.
4. A **class-appropriate security standard**, so that access-control objects stop scoring zero on their own dimension.
5. The two withdrawn closures re-derived against deployment records.
6. Every published negative accompanied by a control that **can fire in the configuration it is measured in**.
