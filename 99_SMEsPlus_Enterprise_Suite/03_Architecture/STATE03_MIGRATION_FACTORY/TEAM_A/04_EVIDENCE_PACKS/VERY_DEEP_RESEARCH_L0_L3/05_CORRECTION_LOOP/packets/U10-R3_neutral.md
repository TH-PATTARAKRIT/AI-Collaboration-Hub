# Correction packet U10-R3 — NEUTRAL KNOWLEDGE

> DEEPSEEK-REPORTED / PENDING CLAUDE VERIFICATION. Neutral layer for Odoo 19 Community: no vendor structure. Supplements the neutral statements of the stock valuation unit; supersedes nothing.

## Period-end accrual of goods and services not yet invoiced

- **WHAT:** The system offers an assisted period-end accrual for selected sales or purchase orders. It books one accrual entry and, in the same step, one automatic reversal dated in the following period. [N-U10R3-001]
- **WHAT:** The helper works in two directions. On the purchase side it accrues goods or services received but not yet billed. On the sales side it accrues goods delivered or services performed but not yet invoiced, and it also handles the opposite case of invoiced but not yet delivered, which produces amounts of opposite sign. [N-U10R3-002]
- **WHY:** The accrual makes the expense or revenue of a period complete before the supplier bill or customer invoice exists, and the reversal removes it again when the real document is booked in the next period.
- **BUSINESS RULE:** The accountant chooses an accrual date (default: the last day of the previous month), a reversal date (default: the day after the accrual date, and it must be later than the accrual date), a miscellaneous-type journal (default: the first one available in the company) and a counterpart accrual account (current liability type on the purchase side, current asset type on the sales side; this account type is only offered as a filter and is not re-checked on creation). [N-U10R3-003]
- **BUSINESS RULE:** When exactly one order is selected the accountant may enter a manual amount. The system then books a single line for that amount on the default expense or income account of the first line's product, instead of computing lines. The amount field is shown only when an amount is already given or when a single order has nothing to accrue. [N-U10R3-004]
- **BUSINESS RULE:** Started from orders, every order line with a product is a candidate; started from sales order lines, only the selected lines are. Notes, sections and down-payment lines are never accrued, and only lines whose amount to invoice at the date is not zero produce an entry line. The action is available on purchase orders, sales orders and sales order lines; no action was found for purchase order lines. [N-U10R3-005]
- **BUSINESS RULE:** Quantities are evaluated as at the accrual date when that date is before today: receipts, deliveries, kit movements and timesheets dated after it are ignored, and only bills dated on or before it count. When the accrual date is today or later, the current stored quantities are used instead. Lines whose received or delivered quantity is entered manually are not date-aware on the purchase side and contribute nothing at a past date on the sales side unless another module supplies a dated quantity. [N-U10R3-006]
- **BUSINESS RULE:** Purchase amount: quantity to bill multiplied by the discounted order price. Quantity to bill is received minus billed, or ordered minus billed when the product is controlled on ordered quantities (so goods not yet received are accrued for such products). When more has been billed than received by at least one unit, the price is derived from the posted bills instead of the order price. Returns reduce the received quantity; vendor refunds reduce the billed quantity. [N-U10R3-007]
- **BUSINESS RULE:** Sales amount: quantity delivered minus quantity invoiced at the date, multiplied by the discounted order price after tax adjustment. When more has been invoiced than delivered, the amount is taken from the posted invoice lines, taken in a fixed order, until the excess quantity is covered. Debit and credit direction is inverted compared with the purchase side. [N-U10R3-008]
- **BUSINESS RULE:** Everything is booked in the company currency. Foreign-currency amounts are converted at the exchange rate valid on the day the helper is used, not at the accrual date. All selected orders must share one currency; the foreign amount and currency are recorded on a line only when a single order is selected. [N-U10R3-009]
- **BUSINESS RULE:** Each accrual line carries the analytic distribution of its order line. One counterpart line labelled as accrued total, with the opposite sign, goes to the accrual account and carries an analytic distribution weighted by line value. [N-U10R3-010]
- **BUSINESS RULE:** Discounts reduce the price; taxes are removed from the unit price (taxes included in prices are stripped), and the price is expressed per product unit of measure. [N-U10R3-011]

## Optional behaviour: stock valuation and price difference

- **OPTIONALITY:** When the sales and stock modules are installed and a product is storable with perpetual valuation, the accrual uses the expense and stock variation accounts of the product; delivered value comes from the value of the done outgoing stock movements, reduced by what has already been expensed through invoices. On the purchase side the line goes to the stock variation account instead of the expense account. This path was read in the source only; the restored configuration uses periodic valuation, so it is not active there and its amounts are unconfirmed. [N-U10R3-012]
- **OPTIONALITY:** For standard-cost products with a price-difference account configured, the purchase accrual adds two offsetting lines for the difference between the standard cost and the price, on the price-difference account and the stock variation account. Without that account nothing is added. [N-U10R3-013]
- **DEPENDENCY:** The purchase and stock module contributes only the date filter on receipt movements and the received quantity; it has no extension of the accrual helper itself. The sales and stock module extends the helper, and because the extension sits on the shared helper it also applies to purchase accruals. [N-U10R3-014]

## Entries, reversal and dates

- **STATE:** Creation sequence: check that the reversal date is later, compute the lines, create the accrual entry, post it, create the reversal as a copy with opposite amounts and the reversal date, post it, then show both entries in a list. The reversal is automatic; there is no separate undo step. [N-U10R3-015]
- **BUSINESS RULE:** Closed periods: posting inside a closed period does not fail; the entry is re-dated to the first allowed date. The copy that becomes the reversal is moved to the day after the fiscal or hard lock if its date falls on or before it. Once posted, changing the date or state of either entry inside a closed period is refused. The tax lock is not triggered by accrual lines because they carry no taxes. [N-U10R3-016]
- **BUSINESS RULE:** A user-specific exception can lower the effective lock date for that user; locks of parent companies apply to child companies; a hard lock admits no exception. [N-U10R3-017]
- **STATE:** An entry dated after today is not posted immediately; it is marked to be posted automatically at its date. [N-U10R3-018]
- **CONSTRAINT:** The helper is offered to users holding the full accounting features role; the same role gets read, write and create (no delete) rights on the helper. Posting additionally needs the invoicing role, which that role includes. [N-U10R3-019]
- **CONSTRAINT:** One company per run: orders of different companies are refused, and journal and accounts must belong to the company, otherwise posting is refused. [N-U10R3-020]
- **OPTIONALITY:** There is no scheduled or automatic execution; the helper runs only on demand from the three actions. [N-U10R3-022]

## Risks and gaps

- **RISK:** The entries are not linked to the orders except through a text reference. The note meant to be left on each order is never written because the list of orders to annotate is never filled. No check prevents running the helper twice for the same orders and date, and no dedicated cancel exists. [N-U10R3-021]
- **RISK:** The closing report has an accrual section, but it is empty in the base, its display line is commented out, and the sales and purchase extensions read figures that nothing in Community produces; the helper calculations behind them are in files that are not loaded. A designer must therefore not expect the report or the month-end closing entry to show or book goods received or delivered not invoiced; the accrual helper is the only path in this revision. [N-U10R3-023]
- **DEPENDENCY:** The month-end closing entry compares the physical stock value with the posted balance of the stock valuation accounts and reads no order data. If accrual lines are booked on a stock variation account in a perpetual setup, they may enter the balance used by a variation-over-period closing; unconfirmed. [N-U10R3-024]
- **RISK:** After the reversal is posted, accrual and reversal lines are matched against each other only on accounts that allow matching. In the restored configuration all current-liability accounts allow it and only part of the current-asset accounts do; expense and income accounts never do. [N-U10R3-025]
- **CONSTRAINT:** Failure cases: reversal date not later than the accrual date, mixed companies, mixed currencies, nothing to accrue and no amount given (an empty entry cannot be posted), archived journal or archived accounts at posting. [N-U10R3-026]
- **RISK:** In the restored configuration no user holds the full accounting features role and the administrator role does not include it, so the actions may not be offered to administrators; this depends on how roles are assigned in practice. [N-U10R3-027]
- **RISK:** On the purchase side the price derived from posted bills adds bill subtotals without distinguishing refunds, while quantities do subtract refunds; the effect on refunded bills is not settled by the source. [N-U10R3-029]
- **UNKNOWN:** The exact date given to the accrual and to the reversal when a lock applies, including whether the accrual date can end up later than the reversal date; the sign and amount of the perpetual valuation lines; whether administrators can reach the action; and whether future-dated entries are posted automatically on their date require runtime confirmation. Cosmetic label reuse in the perpetual path is also noted. [N-U10R3-028]
