## 2. Mini-capability set (L3: D1 purpose · D2 objects · D3 flow)

**MC-1 Trigger and inputs.** D1: let an accountant book, at a period end, the not-yet-invoiced value of received (purchase) or delivered (sales) goods and services, and have it undone automatically in the next period. D2: transient wizard `account.accrued.orders.wizard` (inputs: company, journal, date, reversal date, optional amount, accrual account); started from the Action menu of purchase orders, sales orders and sales order lines (actions bound in `purchase/views/purchase_views.xml`, `sale/wizard/account_accrued_orders_wizard_views.xml`). D3: defaults compute (date = last day of previous month, reversal = date + 1 day, first general journal), preview is computed live from `_compute_move_vals`, button `create_entries`.

**MC-2 Amount computation (purchase and sales).** D1: accrue value that has moved physically (or by service delivery) but is not yet billed, evaluated as at the accrual date. D2: order lines expose non-stored as-at-date fields (`qty_received_at_date`, `qty_delivered_at_date`, `qty_invoiced_at_date`, `amount_to_invoice_at_date`) driven by context key `accrual_entry_date`; `purchase_stock`, `sale_stock`, `sale_mrp`, `sale_timesheet` add date filters to their quantity sources. D3: wizard filters lines with non-zero amount, computes per-line amount and label, adds one counterpart line to the accrual account (and optional price-difference / perpetual lines), converts to company currency.

**MC-3 Perpetual-valuation and price-difference extension.** D1: when stock is valued perpetually the accrual must use the stock variation / expense accounts and, for standard-cost products, book the price difference. D2: hook `_get_product_expense_and_stock_var_accounts` (base returns False, False; `sale_stock` fills it for storable perpetual products); `product._get_price_diff_account` (base False; `stock_account` returns the category price-difference account for standard cost). D3: ON-path branches in the wizard lines 195-196, 219-246, 275-311, 331-352. All RT.

**MC-4 Posting, reversal, dates, locks.** D1: the accrual is an ordinary journal entry; its reversal is a copy with swapped amounts dated later. D2: `account.move` create / `_post` / `_reverse_moves` / `copy_data`; company lock dates and user exceptions (`account/models/company.py`). D3: create entry, `_post()`, reverse with `reversal_date`, `_post()`; posting re-dates instead of refusing when a lock is violated; copy of the reversal is pre-moved to the day after the fiscal lock.

**MC-5 Multi-company, roles, ACL, failure paths.** D1: one company and one currency per run; accountant role needed. D2: `_check_company_auto`, company checks in `_compute_move_vals`, ACL row for `group_account_user`, action `group_ids`, `_post` invoicing-group check. D3: UserError and posting-validation messages (claims C006, C017-C020, C090-C091).

**MC-6 Closing-report accrual section (the empty getter).** D1: the Inventory Valuation (closing) report was designed to show an "Accrual" block of goods received / delivered not invoiced; in this revision it is disabled. D2: JS getter `accrual` in `stock_account`, patched in `sale_stock` and `purchase_stock`; Python helpers `_compute_goods_received_not_invoiced` / `_compute_goods_delivered_not_invoiced`. D3: template call commented out; patched getters read data keys nothing populates; helper files not imported.

## 4. State list (entry path)

```
(wizard open, defaults computed) -> (inputs edited) [user]
(inputs edited) -> (draft accrual entry created, date = accrual date) [create_entries]
(draft accrual entry) -> (posted accrual entry, date possibly re-dated by lock rules) [_post, soft]
(draft accrual entry, date after today) -> (draft + scheduled auto-post at the date) [_post, soft, future date]
(posted accrual entry) -> (draft reversal entry, date = reversal date or day after fiscal lock) [_reverse_moves]
(draft reversal entry) -> (posted reversal entry) or (scheduled) [_post]
(posted accrual + posted reversal) -> (lines reconciled where account is reconcilable) [_post of reversal]
```
