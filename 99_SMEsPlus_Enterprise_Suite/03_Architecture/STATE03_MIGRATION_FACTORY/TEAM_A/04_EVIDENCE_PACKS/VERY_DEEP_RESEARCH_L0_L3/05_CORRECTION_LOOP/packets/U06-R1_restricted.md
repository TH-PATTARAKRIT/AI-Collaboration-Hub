## 2. Findings by topic (prose, with claim ids)

**A. Bill creation entry points on a purchase order.** The order form header has no button that calls the bill-creation method (C001, C002; the only reference to such a statusbar button is a stale step in the purchase_stock tour, C052). Bills are started from: (1) the list header button "Create Bills" in the Purchase Orders list (C003, C004, C067); (2) the "Upload Bill" widget in the form header of a confirmed order (C001, C008) and in the list controller of the RFQ list (C005 to C007, C009 to C013); (3) the Auto-Complete field on a draft vendor bill (C022 to C025); (4) the Bill Matching stat button on the order and the Purchase Matching stat button on the bill, leading to the match list whose Match button can create a draft bill from selected order lines (C026 to C032); (5) automatic linking of a bill to orders after a document import (C015 to C021); (6) the reverse path, a purchase order created and confirmed from bill lines (C033). The order form also has a "Vendor Bills" stat button that only opens existing bills.

**B. Receipt validation acknowledges the order.** `stock.picking._action_done` first acknowledges the purchase orders of the picking (with elevated rights) and then runs the base logic (C042 to C046). The same flag is set by the Acknowledge button, by the portal link, and is used by the reminder selection and by the dashboard filters (C044, C047 to C051).

**C. Role needed to post bills.** Posting requires the invoicing group (C053 to C055); the bill form Confirm button is limited to that group (C054). The purchase user group can create, edit and delete bills (restricted to vendor document types) but does not imply the invoicing group (C057 to C059), so posting needs a user who also holds the invoicing role (or superuser for automated posting) (C053, C062 to C064).

**D. Ownership of the bill-side vendor price lookup.** The vendor price lookup used by the bill catalog is implemented in the `account` module (C036, C037) and is recorded under U02 (VDR-U02-C297, consistent). U06 owns the order-side lookup only; bills created from an order carry the order line price (C040), and a product added by hand to a bill gets the product cost, not the vendor price list (C034, C035). The two lookups differ in what they return (C037, C038). Ownership classification is an inference (C041).

**E. Segregation of duties (SoD) statements (source-derived).** C057 to C066 give the statements: buyer role vs poster role, no creator-versus-poster check, acknowledgement possible without purchase rights, approval role for large orders (U06 CAP-02), autopost conditions, the reverse path in which a buyer both creates and confirms an order from bill lines.

## 4. State list

```
(confirmed order) -> (draft bill linked to order lines) [Create Bills | Upload Bill | Auto-Complete | Match]
(draft bill) -> (posted bill) [Confirm, invoicing group]
(order with open receipt) -> (order acknowledged) [validation of any linked transfer]
(order not acknowledged) -> (order acknowledged) [Acknowledge button | portal acknowledge link | transfer validation]
```
