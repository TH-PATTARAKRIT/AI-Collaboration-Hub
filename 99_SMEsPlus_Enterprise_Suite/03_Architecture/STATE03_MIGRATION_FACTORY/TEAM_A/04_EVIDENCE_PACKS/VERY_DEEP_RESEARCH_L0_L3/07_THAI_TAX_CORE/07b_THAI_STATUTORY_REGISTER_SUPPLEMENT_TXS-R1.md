## How the reads were done

- The task constraint was official Thai sources only: Revenue Department (rd.go.th), the Royal Gazette copies it hosts, ETDA for the technical layer. Odoo is out of scope. Paraphrase only; no statute text is reproduced.
- Raw HTML pages were fetched with a plain HTTP client and converted to text locally; no summarising model sat between the page and the reading. The page footer date (Thai pages: last-updated date; English pages: Last updated) is given as the edition date. Where an instrument was read, its own date, number and Gazette citation are given.
- PDFs were converted with a text extractor. Royal Gazette copies whose extracted text was garbled (font encoding) were rendered to page images and read visually; these are marked page images.
- Read-mode tags in the evidence column: raw HTML, raw PDF text, page images, raw earlier (read raw in the original TXS pass and not repeated).
- Status vocabulary is unchanged from the register: VERIFIED-OFFICIAL, OFFICIAL-SUMMARY-ONLY, UNVERIFIED, CONFLICT. CONFLICT (narrowed) means the wording conflict is gone but the numbers cannot yet be reconciled.
