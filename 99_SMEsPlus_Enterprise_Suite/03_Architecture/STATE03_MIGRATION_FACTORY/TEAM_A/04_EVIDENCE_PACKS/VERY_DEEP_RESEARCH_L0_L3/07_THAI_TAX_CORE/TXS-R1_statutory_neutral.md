# TXS-R1 — Thai Statutory Requirements, Corrections and Additions (Neutral Statements)

DEEPSEEK-REPORTED / PENDING CLAUDE VERIFICATION — neutral statements for the statutory statements changed by correction packet TXS-R1, as read on 2026-10-02 from official Thai sources (Revenue Department pages and the Royal Gazette copies it hosts, the Ministry of Finance notifications it lists, and the electronic-transactions agency for the technical layer). Each statement points to its register entry (Stat-ID) in TXS-R1_statutory_conflict_resolution.md and names the earlier statement it supersedes or updates (N-TXS-number, in TXS_thai_statutory_neutral.md). This file names no software product and no vendor structure. It is not legal or tax advice and draws no coverage or compliance conclusion.

Status key: V = VERIFIED-OFFICIAL, S = OFFICIAL-SUMMARY-ONLY, U = UNVERIFIED, C = CONFLICT. Check currency marks items with frequent change. All sources in this packet were read as raw page text or raw document text unless stated.

## Taxpayer identification, VAT rate and registration

- **N-TXSR1-001** The taxpayer identification number has 13 digits; it replaced the 10-digit number from 1 Feb 2012 under a Director-General announcement of 11 Jan 2012. Use of the old number on documents was tolerated until 31 Jul 2013 and never on tax returns. An RD English page whose footer shows 2014 still says 10 digits and is outdated. Supersedes the conflict note in N-TXS-052. (S11-06, V)
- **N-TXSR1-002** The Revenue Code rate is 10%. Successive Royal Decrees keep the reduced rate; the RD decree index lists the chain from 2017 to a decree published on 23 Aug 2026 that extends the reduction to 30 Sep 2027. RD states that the reduced rate is 7% including local tax for 1 Oct 2026 to 30 Sep 2027, following a Cabinet decision of 27 Jul 2026. The rate after 30 Sep 2027 is not established. Check currency. Updates N-TXS-005 and N-TXS-006. (S02-03, S02-04, S02-05, S02-06, V; post-2027 rate U)
- **N-TXSR1-003** A person about to start may apply for VAT registration before starting. A business must apply within 30 days after its tax base exceeds the small-business limit, or within 30 days after the effective date of a decree that sets or lowers the limit; a business that elects to pay VAT applies within 30 days after notifying the Director-General. The decree limit may not be set below 600,000 baht a year. Updates N-TXS-002. (S01-02, S01-05, V)

## Time of supply, tax invoices and receipts

- **N-TXSR1-004** For goods, VAT liability arises on delivery, but earlier and in proportion on transfer of title, receipt of payment or issue of a tax invoice; for hire-purchase on each instalment due or earlier trigger; for services on receipt of payment, but earlier and in proportion on issue of a tax invoice or use of the service; for imports on payment or security of import duty, or on customs entry when no duty is due. Updates N-TXS-013. (S05-01, S05-02, S05-03, V)
- **N-TXSR1-005** A registrant issues a tax invoice and keeps a copy immediately when liability arises, per place of business unless permitted otherwise. A full-form tax invoice shows: the words tax invoice; the issuer name, address and taxpayer ID, all three; the buyer name and address; the serial number; description, type, quantity and value; the VAT amount stated separately; the issue date; and any further items the Director-General prescribes. It is in Thai language, Thai currency and Thai or Arabic numerals unless an exception is approved, and an approved foreign-currency invoice also shows baht values. Corrects N-TXS-016 (which wrongly joined the issuer items with or) and updates N-TXS-015 and N-TXS-019. (S06-01, S06-02, S06-03, V)
- **N-TXSR1-006** From 1 Jan 2015 a full-form tax invoice shows the buyer's taxpayer ID when the buyer is a VAT registrant and marks the issuer's establishment as head office (word, abbreviation or code 00000) or branch (number or five-digit code); the buyer's establishment is marked the same way when the invoice states the buyer's head-office or branch address. Updates N-TXS-017. (S06-06, V)
- **N-TXSR1-007** A debit note is issued when value rises after sale (quantity above the agreement, price miscalculated low, or other grounds the Director-General sets) and a credit note when value falls (not as agreed, damaged or short, price miscalculated high, goods returned, service contract ended, and Director-General grounds such as compensation, refund of deposits or advance payments, return or exchange of goods, defective or unperformed service). Each is issued in the tax month of the event, or the next tax month where necessary, states the original and correct values, the difference and the tax, and a short reason, and is a tax invoice. The seller adjusts output tax in the month of issue; the buyer adjusts input tax in the month it receives the note. Updates N-TXS-021. (S07-01, S07-02, V)
- **N-TXSR1-008** On the buyer's request, a lost, destroyed or materially damaged paper tax invoice, debit note or credit note is replaced by a substitute with the same content and a clear statement that it is a substitute; the issuer records it in the sales tax report of the month issued. Altering the items of a received tax invoice makes the related input tax non-creditable, except limited corrections of the issuer's address or taxpayer ID within one year of an official change, made with a signature or stamp. Updates N-TXS-020 and N-TXS-022. (S07-03, S04-05, V)
- **N-TXSR1-009** A payee must issue a receipt immediately on each payment above a Director-General amount, within statutory ceilings of 1,000 baht for VAT and specific-business-tax registrants and 10,000 baht for others. The amounts set are above 1,000 baht for small retailers, above 500 baht for registrants subject to the special regime in Sec. 82/16, and above 100 baht for other registrants and exempt persons. The stub or copy is kept at least five years. The notification setting the amounts dates from 1991 with amendment marks; later changes were not checked. Check currency. Updates N-TXS-023 (thresholds). (S08-02, V)
- **N-TXSR1-010** The RD announcement that sets standards for the form, sending, retention and information security of electronic tax documents is a Director-General announcement of the general series, signed 6 Jan 2023 and published 17 Feb 2023, applying from 19 Aug 2022 and issued under a 2022 Ministerial Regulation. It is a different instrument from the RD announcement of 7 Jun 2023 on electronic signatures and certificates. Supersedes N-TXS-047. (S14-12, V)

## Input tax and refunds

- **N-TXSR1-011** Input tax is non-creditable in six statutory cases: no tax invoice or no invoice proving payment (except reasonable cause on Director-General conditions); an invoice incorrect or incomplete in material parts; purchases not directly related to the business; entertainment-type expenses; an invoice from a person not entitled to issue; and cases the Director-General prescribes. The Director-General list includes cars and passenger vehicles up to 10 seats (with exceptions for sale, own rental and insurance of such cars), abbreviated tax invoices, assets and expenses of VAT-exempt activity, construction input tax on property later sold, leased or used in a non-VAT activity within three years (with a time-bound exception for debt restructuring by financial institutions between 1 Jan 2022 and 31 Dec 2026 and further listed exceptions), and invoices whose required items are not printed or computer-made, are copies, or were altered. Updates N-TXS-011. (S04-01, S04-02, S04-03, V)
- **N-TXSR1-012** Excess input tax is offset under Royal-Decree procedure or refunded with that month's return. Where it was not claimed that way, a refund application may be made within three years from the end of the filing period for that tax month; other refund cases within three years from the payment date; import-duty disputes within six months of the written decision or final judgment. Interest on refunds follows the Revenue Code. Updates N-TXS-010. (S09-06, V)

## VAT return and the internet-filing extension

- **N-TXSR1-013** The monthly VAT return with payment is due by the 15th of the following month unless the Director-General decides otherwise, including for months with no sales, at the area office of the place of business, separately per establishment unless combined filing is approved. Updates N-TXS-024. (S09-01, V)
- **N-TXSR1-014** The current extension for internet filing is a Ministry of Finance notification dated 12 Jan 2024 under Sec. 3 attha, amended in March 2024 and December 2025, for returns due from 1 Feb 2024 to 31 Jan 2027. It moves the VAT return and specific-business-tax return to the 23rd of the next month, and the payer-side VAT return and withholding returns (PND 1, 2, 3, 53, 54) to 15 days after month-end; it applies only if original and amended returns are all filed online, and the 23rd moves to the next business day when it is a holiday. A renewal beyond 31 Jan 2027 is not located. Check currency. Supersedes N-TXS-025 and updates N-TXS-027 and N-TXS-036 as to dates. (S09-02, S09-04, S09-09, S10-02, S13-08, V)
- **N-TXSR1-015** A payer remits VAT when paying a non-resident temporary seller that is not registered, for foreign electronic services only where the user is a VAT registrant, and for other foreign services used in Thailand for all users. Updates N-TXS-027. (S10-01, V)

## Foreign currency and rounding

- **N-TXSR1-016** For VAT on foreign-currency sales, if the currency is not sold for baht in the liability month, the base is converted at the average commercial-bank buying rate computed by the Bank of Thailand on the last business day of that month; imports use the rate Customs applies for duty. The English translation page of the Revenue Code says selling rate in this sentence; the Thai text, the RD exchange-rate order and the RD e-service guide say buying, and the Thai text prevails. Supersedes N-TXS-030 and resolves its conflict note. (S12-03, S12-04, V)
- **N-TXSR1-017** Foreign-currency conversion may use commercial-bank rates or the Bank of Thailand daily reference rate, chosen once and kept unless the Director-General approves a change. A seller's tax invoice converts at the buying-type rate; a payer converting for withholding or for payer-side VAT converts at the selling-type rate; a payer remitting through the electronic withholding system uses the reference rate two business days before remittance. Export and international-service invoices may stay in foreign currency. Updates N-TXS-029 and N-TXS-031. (S12-01, S12-02, S12-10, V)
- **N-TXSR1-018** The 1998 RD order on exchange rates was cancelled by the 2005 order, effective 1 Mar 2005, which is the order in force. A later rate movement does not require cancelling and reissuing an invoice already delivered; an invoice converted by a non-permitted method is treated as materially incorrect and the buyer loses the input tax. Updates N-TXS-031. (S12-05, S12-06, V)
- **N-TXSR1-019** The VAT amount on a full-form tax invoice is rounded to two decimals: a third decimal below 5 is dropped and 5 or above rounds up. If the amount shown differs from value times rate because of rounding, the registrant pays the calculated amount. No rounding rule for other tax amounts was found. Supersedes N-TXS-033. (S12-08, V; other amounts U)

## Withholding tax

- **N-TXSR1-020** Under the Director-General order on withholding for listed income, the rates are: rent 5% (foundations 10%, international-cargo ship rent 1%); independent professional fees 3% (foundations 10%); contract work 3% (5% for a foreign-law company without a permanent branch in Thailand, 3% with one); prizes 5%; advertising 2%; other services 3% (not hotel, restaurant or life-insurance premiums); sales-promotion rewards and discounts 3%; non-life insurance premiums 1%; transport 1%; interest to companies 1% (foundations 10%); dividends 10% with exceptions; specified agricultural purchases 0.75%; rice exporters 0.5%. A 1% rate replaced the 5%, 3% and 2% rows for payments made through the electronic withholding system from 1 Jan 2023 to 31 Dec 2025. The guide's 15% for non-resident individuals was not found in the order. Check currency. Supersedes N-TXS-035 (rates) and N-TXS-038. (S13-05, S16-05, V; non-resident individual rate U)
- **N-TXSR1-021** Withholding applies under that order only where the total payable under one contract is 1,000 baht or more, even if each instalment is lower. A separate form-use rule for government and local-government payers paying a company under Sec. 69 bis refers to payments of 500 baht or more per payment; the 500 figure is not a Sec. 3 tredecim minimum. Resolves the conflict note in N-TXS-035. (S13-06, S13-07, S13-15, V)
- **N-TXSR1-022** The reduced 1% rate for payments through the electronic withholding system is stated by RD to be approved by Cabinet on 16 Jun 2026 for 1 Jan 2026 to 31 Dec 2027, but the implementing instrument was not located by 1 Oct 2026; the order text read ends the 1% rows on 31 Dec 2025. The 1% rate for later payments is therefore not established. Check currency. (S13-14, U)
- **N-TXSR1-023** Withholding returns are due within 7 days after the end of the payment month (15 days for internet filing under the extension in N-TXSR1-014). Employer withholding forms are required as electronic data under a 2023 notification (RD states that electronic filing alone applies from 2024); from payments on 1 Jan 2025 the listed withholding forms are electronic-data forms with a written-notice exception when electronic filing is impossible; from payments on 1 Jul 2025 a payee company may act as withholding agent and remit by the 15th of the next month. Whether paper filing of PND 3 or PND 53 remains permitted is not established. Updates N-TXS-036. (S13-08, S13-09, V; paper-filing position U)
- **N-TXSR1-024** For a foreign company not carrying on business in Thailand, the Revenue Code says the payer deducts tax at the corporate income tax rate and remits within 7 days after the end of the payment month. RD's form instructions give 15% for most listed income and 10% for dividends and profit remittance, subject to treaties. The instrument that reduces the corporate rate to 15% and 10% was not found, so the numbers cannot be reconciled from the sources read. Check currency. Updates N-TXS-037; the conflict remains open but narrowed. (S13-10, S13-11, C)

## Representative offices

- **N-TXSR1-025** No statute-level rule specific to a representative office was found. An RD ruling of 2000 treats a representative office that only services its foreign head office like a regional office of a multinational and points to an RD announcement of 1992: head-office funding of the office's own expenses is not taxable income and no VAT arises, but a corporate income tax return is still due; services to others or affiliates make the income taxable and require VAT registration; foreign staff owe personal income tax and the office withholds. The ruling is case-specific and old. Updates N-TXS-056. (S16-07, U)

## Limits
