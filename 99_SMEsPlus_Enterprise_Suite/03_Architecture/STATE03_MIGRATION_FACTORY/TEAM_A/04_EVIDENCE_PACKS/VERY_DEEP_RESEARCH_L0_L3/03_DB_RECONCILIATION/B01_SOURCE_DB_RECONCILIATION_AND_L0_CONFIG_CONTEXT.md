## 1. Method (neutral)
For each of the 356 installed modules: (a) enumerate the data records the module's manifest declares (XML records, menus, templates, actions, reports, CSV rows) and compare, by external identifier, against the records the restored database holds for that module; (b) compare the models and fields statically declared in the module's Python source against the models/fields the database registers for that module; (c) classify every difference. Raw identifier lists stay in the restricted local area; only counts and classes are recorded here.
