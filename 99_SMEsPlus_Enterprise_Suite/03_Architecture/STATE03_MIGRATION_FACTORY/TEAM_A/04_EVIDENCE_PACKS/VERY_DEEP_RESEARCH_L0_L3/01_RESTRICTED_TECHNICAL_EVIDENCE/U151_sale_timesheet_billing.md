## CHAIN FLOW SUMMARY

1. **Product setup**: `service_policy='delivered_timesheet'` → `invoice_policy='delivery'`, `service_type='timesheet'`
2. **SO confirmation**: SOL `qty_delivered_method` computed as `'timesheet'`; project/task created via `sale_project` hooks
3. **Timesheet logging**: Employee logs time on task/project; `so_line` auto-computed via `_timesheet_determine_sale_line()` based on pricing_type
4. **Delivered qty**: `_prepare_qty_delivered()` aggregates `unit_amount` from analytic lines with matching `so_line` and `project_id != False`
5. **Invoice creation**: `_create_invoices()` calls `_link_timesheets_to_invoice()` which stamps `timesheet_invoice_id` on qualifying timesheets
6. **Lock**: Once invoice is posted, timesheets cannot be modified or deleted; credit note reverses the lock
