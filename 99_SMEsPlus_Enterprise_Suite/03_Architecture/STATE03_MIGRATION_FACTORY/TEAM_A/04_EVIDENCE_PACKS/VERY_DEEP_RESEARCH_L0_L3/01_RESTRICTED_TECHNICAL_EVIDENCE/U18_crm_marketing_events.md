## CAP-U18-01 Lead and opportunity lifecycle (create, qualify, win, lose, restore, convert, merge, deduplicate)

**Modules centrally involved:** `crm` (models `crm.lead`, `crm.stage`, `crm.lost.reason`, `crm.recurring.plan`; wizards lost / convert / merge), `mass_mailing_crm`, `crm_sms`, `iap_crm`

**Function-ID(s):** FUNCTION MAPPING REQUIRED

### D1 — Business purpose and process semantics
Business purpose: a single pipeline record (`crm.lead`) represents either an unqualified lead or a qualified opportunity; it moves through ordered stages to a won stage, or is archived as lost with a reason, and can later be restored. Lead and opportunity are the same model distinguished by `type` VDR-U18-C002; the type default depends on whether the 'Show Lead Menu' feature group is held by the creating user VDR-U18-C003. Won means: sitting in a stage flagged won, probability 100 VDR-U18-C015; lost means: archived and probability 0 VDR-U18-C015. A won lead cannot be lost without first moving it to another stage VDR-U18-C014. Converting a lead creates or links a customer record and sets type opportunity VDR-U18-C028; duplicates are detected by email domain, phone and commercial entity VDR-U18-C041 and may be merged into the most reliable record VDR-U18-C035.

### D2 — Architecture, data and object relationships
Objects: `crm.lead` (inherits mail thread with CC, blacklist, phone, activity, UTM, address and stage-duration mixins) VDR-U18-C001; `crm.stage` (won flag, fold flag, per-team visibility, rot threshold) VDR-U18-C006; `crm.lost.reason` VDR-U18-C009; `crm.recurring.plan` (monthly revenue computed from plan months) VDR-U18-C010; `crm.lead.scoring.frequency` and `.field` (predictive scoring tables) VDR-U18-C057; wizards `crm.lead.lost`, `crm.lead2opportunity.partner`, `crm.lead2opportunity.partner.mass`, `crm.merge.opportunity` VDR-U18-C023 VDR-U18-C029 VDR-U18-C039. Partner link: `partner_id` with email and phone propagation both ways VDR-U18-C044 VDR-U18-C045; company of the lead is derived from team, user and partner VDR-U18-C047; meetings link through `calendar.event.opportunity_id` and are detached on delete VDR-U18-C054.

### D3 — Source, technical and workflow logic
State machine (A -> B [trigger / precondition]):
- (new) -> lead|opportunity [create; type default from group VDR-U18-C003; stage defaults to first non-folded stage visible for the team VDR-U18-C004]
- stage X -> stage Y [write stage_id; sets last-stage-update date VDR-U18-C017; if Y is won: forces active=True, probability=100, automated_probability=100 VDR-U18-C018]
- open -> won [action_set_won picks the won stage following the current stage sequence, else the last earlier one; writes stage and probability 100 VDR-U18-C026; created directly in a won stage also stamps date_closed VDR-U18-C016]
- open -> lost [action_set_lost: archive, then write probability 0, automated_probability 0 plus lost_reason_id VDR-U18-C022; via wizard with optional closing note VDR-U18-C023]
- lost -> open [action_unarchive clears lost reason and recomputes automated probability VDR-U18-C024; action_restore additionally aligns probability to the automated value VDR-U18-C025]
- lead -> opportunity [convert_opportunity skips inactive and won leads VDR-U18-C027; writes type, conversion date and partner VDR-U18-C028; wizard refuses probability 100 VDR-U18-C029]
- {2..5 leads} -> 1 [merge_opportunity: at least two, at most five unless superuser VDR-U18-C034; head = highest confidence VDR-U18-C035; tails deleted with sudo VDR-U18-C038]
Derived fields on transition: date_closed set when probability >= 100 or inactive, cleared when probability > 0 or stage moves without probability VDR-U18-C019; date_open set when salesperson changes, cleared when removed VDR-U18-C020; every status change updates the predictive-scoring frequency tables VDR-U18-C021; a lead may not be both won and lost VDR-U18-C021.
Inheritance: base `crm.lead` is extended in installed modules by `sale_crm` (orders, revenue), `iap_crm` (reveal id merge) VDR-U18-C062, `crm_iap_enrich` (enrichment flags) VDR-U18-C063, `crm_iap_mine` (mining request link) VDR-U18-C064, `crm_livechat` (origin channel) VDR-U18-C065, `event_crm`, `survey_crm`, `mass_mailing_crm` (lead becomes a mailing recipient model) VDR-U18-C066.

## CAP-U18-02 Lead intake channels and assignment (teams, capacity, rules, scheduled and manual assignment)

**Modules centrally involved:** `crm` (`crm.team`, `crm.team.member`, settings, cron), `crm_livechat`, `crm_mail_plugin`, `survey_crm`, `event_crm` (rules), `crm_iap_mine`

**Function-ID(s):** FUNCTION MAPPING REQUIRED

### D1 — Business purpose and process semantics
Business purpose: new leads arrive from many channels (email alias of a sales team, live-chat bot, mail-client plug-in, survey answers, event registrations, lead mining) and must end up with a team and a salesperson. Two modes exist: simple mode, where a lead created without salesperson goes to the team leader VDR-U18-C095; and rule-based mode (parameter crm.lead.auto.assignment), where leads without team and salesperson are allocated to teams in proportion to team capacity and then distributed to members within their daily quota and domain filters VDR-U18-C076 VDR-U18-C085 VDR-U18-C073. Rule-based mode can run manually from the team form or from a scheduled job VDR-U18-C081 VDR-U18-C080; only sales managers and administrators may trigger it VDR-U18-C082. Assignment merges detected duplicates and deletes the absorbed ones VDR-U18-C087 VDR-U18-C088.
