## Cross-Module Chain Summary

```
SO.action_confirm()
  → sale_stock: SaleOrderLine._action_launch_stock_rule()
    → stock.rule.run(procurements)
      → purchase_stock: StockRule._run_buy()
        → purchase.order.create() with picking_type=dropship, dest_address=customer
          → PurchaseOrder.button_confirm()
            → _create_picking(): location_id=supplier, location_dest_id=customer.property_stock_customer
              → stock.move: location supplier→customer (is_dropship=True)
                → validate picking
                  → stock_account._action_done(): is_dropship moves get _set_value()
                    → SVL created; no internal stock touched
```

**Return flow:** Customer-location moves back to supplier are caught by `_is_dropshipped_returned()` and valued as a separate category.
