## CAP-U38-01 Predictive lead scoring (win-probability learning)

**Function-ID(s):** FUNCTION MAPPING REQUIRED

**D1 (business purpose and process semantics):** Gives every open lead or opportunity an automated win probability learned from the history of won and lost leads of the same sales team. Salespeople keep an editable probability that follows the automated one until they override it. [VDR-U38-C001; VDR-U38-C002]

**D2 (architecture, data and object relationships):** Lead (probability, automated probability, stage, team, scoring fields), frequency table (variable, value, won count, lost count, team), catalogue of allowed scoring fields, three system parameters (scoring fields, start date, auto-assignment flag shared with CAP-02), update wizard (administrators), scheduled recompute (inactive in the restored DB), live increments from the won/lost handler, team deletion folding. [VDR-U38-C043; VDR-U38-C044; VDR-U38-C018; VDR-U38-C045]

**D3 (source, technical and workflow logic):** Lead stage/team/scoring-field edit -> dynamic dependency recomputes probabilities -> naive Bayes helper reads the frequency table (team bucket or global bucket) -> product of per-value won/lost shares times team prior -> clamp 0.01..99.99 -> probability aligned only if it equalled the previous automated value. Win or loss -> live +1/-1 increment (only leads created on or after the start date). Rebuild path: truncate table, re-read all closed leads since the start date, then batched raw update of scores (5000 per statement, commit per batch, failures logged and skipped). Tooltip action recomputes and writes. [VDR-U38-C015; VDR-U38-C016; VDR-U38-C019; VDR-U38-C020; VDR-U38-C021; VDR-U38-C028; VDR-U38-C024; VDR-U38-C025; VDR-U38-C026; VDR-U38-C038]

State diagram:
- Lead pending -> Won  [stage flagged won or won action]; frequency won +1 (live increment)
- Lead pending -> Lost  [archive with probability 0]; frequency lost +1 for current and earlier stages
- Lead Won/Lost -> pending  [restore / leave state]; frequency -1 floored at 0.1
- Automated probability: absent -> stage-only prior -> product of value shares  [as history grows]
- Score unchanged  [team bucket has zero won or zero lost history]
