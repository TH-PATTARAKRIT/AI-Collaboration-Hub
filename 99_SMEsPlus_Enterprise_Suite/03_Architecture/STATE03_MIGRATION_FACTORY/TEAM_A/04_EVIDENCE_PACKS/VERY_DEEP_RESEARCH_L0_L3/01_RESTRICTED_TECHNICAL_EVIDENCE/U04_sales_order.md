## CAP-U04-01 Order state machine (quotation, sent, sales order, cancelled; lock flag)

**Function-ID(s):** FUNCTION MAPPING REQUIRED

### D1 — Business purpose and process semantics
Business purpose: a single sales document that moves from an editable quotation, through an optionally emailed or portal-viewed offer, to a binding sales order, or to cancellation, with a separate lock flag that freezes a confirmed order (there is no `done` state in this revision VDR-U04-C001). The state governs what can be edited, invoiced, delivered and reported; the document is labelled Quotation in draft, sent and cancel and Sales Order otherwise VDR-U04-C030. Every transition is user-, portal- or payment-driven; no cron changes state VDR-U04-C036.

### D2 — Architecture, data and object relationships
Objects and relations: `sale.order` (state, locked, date_order, validity_date, require_signature/payment, tracking) VDR-U04-C002 VDR-U04-C003; `sale.order.line.state` is a stored related copy of the order state, driving line qty_to_invoice, product_updatable, price recompute and portal editing VDR-U04-C028; `invoice_status` roll-up forced to `no` unless state is sale VDR-U04-C027; chatter tracking with dedicated subtypes VDR-U04-C032; entry points in views (buttons, server action Mark Quotation as Sent) VDR-U04-C005 VDR-U04-C012 VDR-U04-C019 VDR-U04-C023; wizard `sale.mass.cancel.orders` VDR-U04-C020; portal controller VDR-U04-C021; payment.transaction post-processing VDR-U04-C008.

### D3 — Source, technical and workflow logic
