## L9 WHT ACCOUNTING POSTING PATTERN

For a Thai vendor invoice with 3% WHT and 7% VAT (price-included):

**Invoice posting (DR/CR):**
- DR: Expense/Asset account (base amount excl. VAT, incl. WHT gross)
- CR: Accounts Payable (net payable = gross - WHT amount)
- DR: Input VAT transition account (if cash-basis) or Input VAT account
- CR: WHT Payable account (defined by WHT repartition line account_id)

**At payment:**
- DR: Accounts Payable
- CR: Cash/Bank
- CR: WHT Payable (if not already posted at invoice)
- DR/CR: CABA reconciliation if tax_exigibility='on_payment'

**Key mechanism:** The WHT repartition line's `account_id` must be a current liability account (e.g., "WHT Payable 3%"). The `factor_percent=100` on the WHT tax line means the full computed WHT amount posts to that account. The AP line is reduced by the WHT amount automatically through the `compute_all` output being used to build journal lines.

## GAP-046 FINDING

The underlying tax computation engine is standard Odoo Community with no Thai-specific WHT extensions. The `compute_all` method at line 4975 returns all necessary data (base, tax amounts, account_ids, repartition_line_ids) for PND form generation. **GAP-046 requires a custom module to read these values and format them into PND forms** — the core computation exists but the PND reporting extraction does not.
