## Summary

The Odoo 19 Community `hr` module integrates what was historically a separate `hr_contract` module into a single package. Contracts are now represented as `hr.version` records (one per effective date per employee), linked via `_inherits` delegation so contract fields surface transparently on the employee form but are gated behind the HR Administrator group. The employee model is heavily composed via four mixins (chatter, activity, resource, avatar) and delegates its name and active flag through the `resource.resource` layer. Department hierarchy uses Odoo's `_parent_store` nested-set mechanism for performance. Two security groups (Officer/Administrator) form a simple ACL ladder; the multi-company record rule on the employee model implements a rich four-condition OR to allow managers and their reports to see each other across company boundaries. The `hr.employee.public` SQL view is the safe read path for non-HR users.

Key gap finding: **`hr_contract` is not a separate community module in Odoo 19** — it was merged into `hr` as `hr.version`. Any migration assumption that a standalone `hr_contract` module needs to be installed should be updated.
