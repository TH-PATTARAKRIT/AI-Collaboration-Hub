## CAP-U23-01 Withholding tax registered at payment time

**Modules:** l10n_account_withholding_tax

**Function-ID(s):** FUNCTION MAPPING REQUIRED

### D1 Business purpose and process semantics
Lets a payer record, when a payment is registered, the part of an invoice or bill that is retained for the tax authority. The taxes involved are normal negative taxes flagged 'Withhold On Payment'; they are ignored when the document is computed (VDR-U23-C002, VDR-U23-C009) and re-enter through a table of withholding lines on the payment registration step or the payment form (VDR-U23-C036, VDR-U23-C043). The resulting payment entry pays out cash net of the withholding, still clears the full document amount on the receivable or payable side, and posts the tax plus an equal and opposite base pair for reporting (VDR-U23-C016, VDR-U23-C025, VDR-U23-C026, VDR-U23-C027). A withholding number is required per line and can come from a per-tax series (VDR-U23-C023, VDR-U23-C024). WHAT COMMUNITY PROVIDES: a generic, localization-agnostic mechanism delivered by Odoo S.A. under LGPL-3 (VDR-U23-C050) with one printed output, the extra table on the payment receipt (VDR-U23-C048, VDR-U23-C052). It does not provide a Thai withholding certificate, return form or rate table. NOTHING FROM Extra_Thailand (or any proprietary add-on) was opened or used; the Thai localization in this tree (l10n_th, Community) defines withholding as ordinary negative taxes and does not use this add-on (VDR-U23-C056, VDR-U23-C057, VDR-U23-C058, VDR-U23-C059).

### D2 Architecture, data, objects
Models: account.tax gains is_withholding_tax_on_payment and withholding_sequence_id (VDR-U23-C002, VDR-U23-C003); abstract account.withholding.line (carrying analytic mixin) with two concrete children, account.payment.withholding.line (persisted, cascade-deleted with the payment) (VDR-U23-C043) and the transient account.payment.register.withholding.line; account.payment gains should_withhold_tax, withholding_line_ids, display_withholding and a writable outstanding account (VDR-U23-C013); account.payment.register gains the same plus the net amount and outstanding account proposal (VDR-U23-C037, VDR-U23-C040); res.company gains withholding_tax_base_account_id (VDR-U23-C045); product.template overrides the price label helper (VDR-U23-C012). Client JS helper patch mirrors the server filter (VDR-U23-C010). Security: two ACL rows for the invoicing group, no rules/groups/cron (VDR-U23-C044). Core hook: account.payment._prepare_move_withholding_lines (VDR-U23-C015, VDR-U23-C018). Downstream Community modules that depend on it: PH, KH, LK, Saudi withholding add-on, POS bridge (none installed) (VDR-U23-C054).

### D3 Source/technical/workflow logic
Control flow: (1) tax configured with a negative amount and the flag; the form forces on-invoice recognition and price-excluded (VDR-U23-C004, VDR-U23-C005, VDR-U23-C006, VDR-U23-C007). (2) Document calculation: the override adds filter_tax_function unless the base line sets calculate_withholding_taxes, so invoice totals and journal items ignore the tax (VDR-U23-C009, VDR-U23-C011). (3) Register-payment wizard: display_withholding computed from existing flagged taxes by payment type and single-entry condition (VDR-U23-C035, VDR-U23-C029); default lines from the moves' rounded base lines, grouped by number/analytic/account/tax/currency (VDR-U23-C036, VDR-U23-C031, VDR-U23-C034); amounts follow a paid factor for partial or instalment payments (VDR-U23-C042, VDR-U23-C020). (4) Confirmation: net amount check, outstanding account set and made reconcilable, lines copied to the payment (VDR-U23-C038, VDR-U23-C039, VDR-U23-C041). (5) Entry build: core adds the extension lines; liquidity reduced, counterpart unchanged (VDR-U23-C014, VDR-U23-C016, VDR-U23-C017); numbers drawn if unnamed (VDR-U23-C024); refund handling (VDR-U23-C030).

## CAP-U23-02 Debit note issuance and numbering

**Modules:** account_debit_note

**Function-ID(s):** FUNCTION MAPPING REQUIRED

### D1 Business purpose and process semantics
Gives users a controlled way to raise an upward correction: a debit note is a new draft customer invoice or vendor bill that is linked to the posted document it corrects (VDR-U23-C068, VDR-U23-C078). It differs from a credit note, which reverses the original with a reversed-entry link and may be posted and reconciled at creation (VDR-U23-C090, VDR-U23-C091). A debit note of a credit note becomes an invoice or bill of the matching direction, i.e. it cancels the credit note's effect (VDR-U23-C074). Printed documents change title to debit note (VDR-U23-C087) and the document shows a counter button (VDR-U23-C069, VDR-U23-C089).

### D2 Architecture, data, objects
Models: account.debit.note (transient wizard, domain posted moves (VDR-U23-C070)) with date, reason, journal, copy_lines; account.move gains debit_origin_id, debit_note_ids, debit_note_count (VDR-U23-C068); account.journal gains debit_sequence (VDR-U23-C083). Views: wizard form and action bound to the move model (VDR-U23-C080), form button (VDR-U23-C081), three search-view filters (VDR-U23-C089), report template title overrides (VDR-U23-C087). Security: one ACL row for the invoicing group (VDR-U23-C082). Dependent localizations: (VDR-U23-C097).

### D3 Source/technical/workflow logic
Control flow: button or bound action opens the wizard; default_get validates selection (posted, not linked, allowed types) (VDR-U23-C071, VDR-U23-C072, VDR-U23-C073); create_debit builds default values per move (VDR-U23-C074, VDR-U23-C075), optionally clears lines (VDR-U23-C076, VDR-U23-C077) and calls copy with business fields (VDR-U23-C078); the result opens as form or list (VDR-U23-C079). Numbering: when journal.debit_sequence is on, the last-number search is split by origin presence (VDR-U23-C084) and the starting number is prefixed D for invoice and bill types (VDR-U23-C085). Chatter text notes the origin (VDR-U23-C086). Posting is outside the module: the entry is that of a normal invoice (VDR-U23-C092).
State machine:
- Posted invoice/bill/credit note -> Debit note wizard open [button, posted state, allowed type]
- Wizard confirmed -> Debit note (draft) with origin link [no module posting call]
- Debit note (draft) -> Posted [core invoice flow, U11]
- Posted debit note -> not debitable again [origin link set]
