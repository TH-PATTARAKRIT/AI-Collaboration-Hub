### Evidence Summary

All claims are C1 (confirmed from direct source reading). The mechanism operates in three mutually-reinforcing layers:

1. **Field-level binding**: `company_id` is required and immutable on Warehouse, Operation Type, and Stock Move. The Quant and Transfer company_id are derived, not settable, preventing override.

2. **Record-rule isolation**: 14 distinct `ir.rule` records in `stock_security.xml` (noupdate=1) filter every stock model to `company_ids in session`, so users never see another company's inventory records.

3. **Transit mechanism**: Community edition provides two transit patterns — a per-company internal transit location (for intra-company multi-warehouse resupply) and a global shared inter-company transit location (no company_id, activated when a second company is created). True inter-company trade (cross-legal-entity) routing via `account_inter_company_rules` is an Enterprise feature not present in this Community source baseline.

GAP-003 status: **PARTIAL** — warehouse-company binding and isolation are fully implemented in Community. Cross-company automated purchase/sale order generation (`account_inter_company_rules`) is not present in Community; only the transit locations are provided.
