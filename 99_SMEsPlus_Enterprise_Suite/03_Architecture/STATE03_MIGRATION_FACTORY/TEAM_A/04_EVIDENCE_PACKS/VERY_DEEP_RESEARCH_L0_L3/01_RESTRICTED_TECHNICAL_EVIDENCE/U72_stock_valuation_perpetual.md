## Key Finding: SVL Replacement

`stock.valuation.layer` does NOT exist in Odoo 19 Community (confirmed U71, verified U72).
The replacement architecture stores move value directly on `stock.move.value` (Monetary field)
and records price-history events in the new `product.value` model (`stock_account/models/product_value.py`).
