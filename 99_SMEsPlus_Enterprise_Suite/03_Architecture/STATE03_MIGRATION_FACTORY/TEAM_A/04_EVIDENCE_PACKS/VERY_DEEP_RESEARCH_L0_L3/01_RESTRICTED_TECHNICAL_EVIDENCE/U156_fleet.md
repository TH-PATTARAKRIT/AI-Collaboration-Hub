## Summary

- **Module present:** Yes
- **Depends only on base + mail:** Yes (standalone application)
- **Vehicle lifecycle:** `fleet.vehicle.state` configurable states (New Request, To Order, Registered, Downgraded + custom)
- **Driver assignment:** `driver_id` / `future_driver_id` with assignment log model `fleet.vehicle.assignation.log`
- **Odometer:** Dedicated `fleet.vehicle.odometer` log; last reading resolved by max value (not date)
- **Contract/Insurance:** `fleet.vehicle.log.contract` with start/expiration dates, recurring cost, and cron-driven state transitions
- **Service log:** `fleet.vehicle.log.services` with cost, date, state, and inline odometer capture
- **Cost analysis:** PostgreSQL view model `fleet.vehicle.cost.report` combining service and contract costs monthly
- **Accounting link:** None in community; `fleet_account` bridge required (absent in source tree)
- **Security:** Officer + Administrator groups with multi-company record rules
