## CAP-TXA2-01 Tax document types, states and issuing identity

**Function-ID(s):** SDV-F07 (credit note after invoicing, accounting leg only) on the credit-note claims; otherwise FUNCTION MAPPING REQUIRED

### D1 Business purpose and process semantics
Business purpose: decide what kinds of accounting document exist for tax purposes, in which state each can be, and what the user can do to change one after it has been posted. Process semantics: a document is prepared in draft, posted (becomes ledger-affecting and numbered), and may be reset, cancelled or reversed. Credit notes are produced by reversal; debit notes, replacement documents and tax-invoice-specific kinds are not part of the base (a debit-note module exists but is uninstalled). Receipts are first-class types that reuse the invoice machinery. (VDR-TXA2-C001, VDR-TXA2-C003, VDR-TXA2-C002)
