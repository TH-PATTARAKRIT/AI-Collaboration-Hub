## CAP-U14-01 Bill of materials (BoM) definition, kinds, lookup and kit expansion

**Function-ID(s):** BRP-F01 (partial, **CONTRA** on the three-type wording — see D3), BRP-F02 (matches), BRP-F08 (matches), BRP-F09 (partial, **CONTRA** on "resolved recursively" — see D3); BRP-F03 (subcontracting type) only noted as a DISCOVERED hand-off to U15; everything else `FUNCTION MAPPING REQUIRED (CAP-U14-01)`.

### D1 — Business purpose & process semantics
A BoM (`mrp.bom`) is the master recipe: finished product (template or one variant), produced quantity and unit, component lines (`mrp.bom.line`), optional by-product lines (`mrp.bom.byproduct`) with a cost-share percentage, optional operations (CAP-U14-02), and planning parameters (manufacturing lead time, days to prepare, readiness policy, consumption policy, batch size) C001 C009 C011 C012 C013 C014. Two kinds are defined by `mrp` itself: `normal` ("Manufacture this product") and `phantom` ("Kit") C002. A kit is never produced by a manufacturing order (MO); stock movements and procurements that name a kit are replaced by movements/procurements for its components C041 C042 C044. A third kind, `subcontract`, only appears when `mrp_subcontracting` (U15) is installed C003 C005. The BoM is also the source of cost-estimate and availability reports (CAP-U14-10) and of the lead-time maths in replenishment (CAP-U14-09).

### D2 — Architecture / data / object relationships
- `mrp.bom` (company optional → shared when empty) 1—n `mrp.bom.line` (`product_id` component, `product_qty`, `product_uom_id`, `operation_id`, `bom_product_template_attribute_value_ids`, computed `child_bom_id`) and 1—n `mrp.bom.byproduct` (`product_id`, `product_qty`, `cost_share`, `operation_id`) and 1—n `mrp.routing.workcenter` (operations) C016 C020 C026.
- `product.template.bom_ids`, `product.product.variant_bom_ids`, computed `is_kits` (a phantom BoM exists for the product) C046.
- Stock side: `stock.move.bom_line_id`, `stock.move.byproduct_id`, `stock.move.cost_share`; `stock.warehouse.orderpoint.bom_id` (CAP-U14-09).
- Project side (`project_mrp`): `mrp.bom.project_id` C059.
- Attachments: documents flagged `attached_on_mrp='bom'` on the product are shown on the BoM; `ir.attachment` created against a BoM is re-pointed to the product C058.

### D3 — Source / technical / workflow logic
**Lookup** `_bom_find(products, picking_type, company_id, bom_type)`: services never have a BoM; domain = (variant match OR template-level with no variant) AND active AND company in [False, company] AND optional operation type in [type, False] AND optional kind; ordered `sequence, product_id, id`; variant-specific BoM wins over template-level one C032 C033 C034 C035. `_compute_bom_id` on an MO asks for `bom_type='normal'` only C036; `explode()` asks for `bom_type='phantom'` only C037.
**Explode** `explode(product, quantity)`: iterates lines, skips lines not applicable to the variant (`_skip_bom_line`), recurses **only** into kit sub-BoMs (phantom), rounds each leaf component quantity UP in the line unit, returns `(boms_done, lines_done)` C038 C037 C039. A line whose component has a *normal* BoM is therefore a leaf: the MO gets a raw move for the sub-assembly (phantom children are skipped when MO moves are built C040). **This is the evidence for the CONTRA on BRP-F09**: the pilot register says a component's own BOM is "resolved recursively"; in source the recursion in `explode` is for kits only, while manufactured sub-assemblies cascade into child MOs only through procurement (CAP-U14-09) and appear nested only in the BoM Overview report C065.
**Type routing (BRP-F01):** `mrp` alone defines two kinds C002; `subcontract` is added by `mrp_subcontracting` with a constraint that a subcontract BoM has no operations or by-products C003 C004. In the restored DB all three values are present because `mrp_subcontracting` is installed C005. The statement in the register that the type is the "single field that routes … downstream stock and financial treatment" is only **INFERENCE** from source: the kind decides MO vs explode vs subcontract flow; financial treatment follows product valuation settings in `stock_account`/`mrp_account` (U10/U15), not the BoM type C006.
**Kit expansion in stock:** `stock.move.action_explode` finds a phantom BoM for each move product, creates one move per exploded line scaled by `move qty / bom.product_qty`, then cancels and unlinks the original move; `_action_done` explodes kits that skipped confirmation; kits bypass reservation C041 C042 C043. Quants of a kit cannot be written directly C045. Kit availability = min over components of (component quantity ÷ quantity per kit, rounded DOWN), × BoM quantity, integer part, for `virtual_available`, `qty_available`, `incoming_qty`, `outgoing_qty`, `free_qty` C047.
**Constraints & lifecycle:** cycle check, variant/by-product/cost-share checks, kit vs reordering-rule exclusion in both directions, positive quantity, delete blocked while unfinished MOs exist C021 C022 C023 C024 C025 C030 C031 C010 C052. `write` on structure fields flags draft/confirmed MOs `is_outdated_bom` C049 C050; `create` with `parent_production_id` context links the new BoM to the originating MO ("Generate BoM") C051; archive/unarchive cascades to operations and from product/variant archive to BoMs C053 C054 C055.

State diagram (BoM is not a state machine; active flag only):
- `active -> archived [action_archive on BoM, or archive of its product/variant: operations archived too]`
- `archived -> active [action_unarchive / product unarchive: operations restored]`

## CAP-U14-02 Work centers, BoM operations (routing) and productivity-loss master data

**Function-ID(s):** BRP-F04 (partial, **CONTRA** on "Allowed Employees"), BRP-F05 (matches); other items `FUNCTION MAPPING REQUIRED (CAP-U14-02)`.

### D1 — Business purpose & process semantics
A work center (`mrp.workcenter`) is a machine/line/team that performs operations: it has a resource calendar and time efficiency (inherited from `resource.mixin`), setup and cleanup minutes, an hourly cost, optional per-product capacities, optional alternative work centers, and live productivity statistics (OEE, blocked/productive time over the last month) C074 C075 C076 C080 C078 C085. An operation (`mrp.routing.workcenter`) is one step of a BoM bound to a work center, with a duration that is either fixed ("manual") or computed from the average of the last N finished work orders ("auto"), a cost basis (actual tracked time vs estimated time) and optional dependencies on other operations of the same BoM C092 C093 C097 C098 C094. Productivity-loss reasons classify time logs as productive / performance / availability / quality so that OEE and work-center blocking can be computed C105 C106 C084.

### D2 — Architecture / data / object relationships
- `mrp.workcenter` 1—n `mrp.routing.workcenter` (`workcenter_id`); `mrp.workcenter` 1—n `mrp.workorder` (`order_ids`), 1—n `mrp.workcenter.productivity` (`time_ids`), 1—n `mrp.workcenter.capacity`, m—n alternatives (`mrp_workcenter_alternative_rel`), m—n `mrp.workcenter.tag` C078 C080.
- `mrp.routing.workcenter.bom_id` is required with `ondelete='cascade'`; `bom_line_ids.operation_id`, `byproduct_ids.operation_id` and `stock.move.operation_id` point back to it C092 C019.
- `mrp.workcenter.productivity.loss` (reason, `manual` flag) → `mrp.workcenter.productivity.loss.type` (selection availability/performance/quality/productive) C105 C106.
- Seeded data: 4 loss types, 7 loss reasons C110.

### D3 — Source / technical / workflow logic
**Time maths (operation):** `time_total = setup + cleanup + cycle_number × time_cycle × 100 / workcenter.time_efficiency`, `cycle_number = ceil(quantity / capacity)`, capacity/setup/cleanup come from `workcenter._get_capacity(product, unit, default)` (product-specific capacity row first, then unit-only, else default capacity = BoM quantity and the work center's own setup/cleanup) C095 C082. `time_cycle` = `time_cycle_manual` in manual mode; in auto mode = total real duration ÷ number of cycles over the last `time_mode_batch` (default 10) done work orders with produced quantity, else falls back to manual C094. `cost = time_total / 60 × workcenter.costs_hour` C096.
**Dependencies:** `blocked_by_operation_ids` is acyclic (`_has_cycle`) and only meaningful when the BoM has `allow_operation_dependencies` C098 C099; MO work orders copy them at confirmation (CAP-U14-07).
**Work-center status:** computed from open productivity logs (no `date_end`): none → `normal`; open log with loss type productive/performance → `done` ("In Progress"); otherwise `blocked` C083. Blocking = saving a productivity log with a manual loss reason from the block wizard; `unblock` closes all open logs; starting a work order on a blocked center is refused (CAP-U14-07) C087 C086. OEE = productive ÷ (productive + non-productive) over the last month C085.
**Capacity finder:** `_get_first_available_slot` scans the work-center calendar in 14-day windows, at most `mrp.workcenter_max_planning_iterations` (default 50 → 700 days), skipping slots taken by other work orders' calendar leaves; returns `(False, 'No available slot 700 days…')` on failure C088 C089.
**Settings coupling:** turning the "Work Orders" setting off sets every operation inactive; turning it on re-activates the operations written last; turning off dependencies clears `allow_operation_dependencies` on all BoMs C103 C104.
**BRP-F04 CONTRA:** the register describes a work center with "Cost per hour, Allowed Employees". Cost per hour exists C075; there is **no allowed-employee field** on `mrp.workcenter` in Community `mrp` (DB table has none); the only employee-related items are extension hooks `without_employee_cost` and a help text mentioning "real employee costs" C077 C097.

State diagram (work-center status, derived):
- `normal -> blocked [productivity log with non-productive loss type and no end]`
- `normal -> done(In Progress) [log with productive/performance loss and no end]`
- `blocked -> normal [unblock() closes open logs]`
- `done -> normal [log closed, e.g. work order paused/finished]`

## CAP-U14-03 Manufacturing order (MO) creation, quantity change, confirmation, lifecycle state and cancellation (incl. project link)

**Function-ID(s):** `FUNCTION MAPPING REQUIRED (CAP-U14-03)` for all claims in this capability (no existing BRP/MFG ID covers the MO document lifecycle itself); BRP-F02/BRP-F08 only where the BoM-to-MO derivation touches kits/by-products.

### D1 — Business purpose & process semantics
An MO (`mrp.production`) instructs the plant to make a quantity of a product: it carries the finished-product move, component (raw) moves, optional by-product moves, optional work orders, planned dates, responsible user, source/destination locations, a priority, and a consumption policy. It is created by a user, by a replenishment need (CAP-U14-09), as a backorder (CAP-U14-06), by a merge/split, or from a reordering rule. A draft MO is a quotation of the work: its components and operations are *computed* from the BoM and can be freely edited; confirmation commits the stock moves and starts reservation (CAP-U14-04). The MO state is derived from the states of its moves, work orders and the produced quantity, with a few explicit transitions C123 C125 C126 C127 C128 C129.

### D2 — Architecture / data / object relationships
- `mrp.production` (company, `picking_type_id` code `mrp_operation`, `bom_id`, `product_id`, `product_qty`, `product_uom_id`, `location_src_id`, `location_dest_id`, `location_final_id`, `date_start`, `date_finished`, `date_deadline`, `priority`, `user_id`, `consumption`, `is_locked`, `is_planned`, `orderpoint_id`, `origin`) C118 C133 C137 C136 C172.
- Moves: `move_raw_ids` (`raw_material_production_id`), `move_finished_ids` (`production_id`, includes by-products), `move_byproduct_ids` (computed subset); `workorder_ids`; `scrap_ids`; `unbuild_ids`; `move_dest_ids` (`created_production_id`) C138 C139 C140.
- **Production group** (`mrp.production.group`, m2m parent/child groups) replaces the old procurement-group concept for MOs: every MO and its moves point to a group; backorders share the group; MOs generated to supply another MO become child groups C143 C119. Moves are also linked to `stock.reference` records created per MO (`reference_ids`, many2many with `mrp.production`) C144.
- Operation type (`stock.picking.type`, code `mrp_operation`) provides the sequence (`WH/MO/00001` pattern), default locations, backorder policy, label/print options C120 C187.
- Project link (`project_mrp`): `mrp.production.project_id` stored compute from `bom_id.project_id` C179 C180.

### D3 — Source / technical / workflow logic
**Creation:** `create` fills `name` from the picking-type sequence, creates the production group, creates one `stock.reference` per MO and pushes group/reference to its moves C120 C143 C144. Defaults: `date_start` now, `date_finished` start + 1 h (or from `default_date_deadline`) C131; in draft, computes derive `product_id`, `product_qty`, `product_uom_id`, `bom_id` (normal BoM of the company), `picking_type_id`, locations, raw moves, finished moves and work orders from the BoM C134 C135 C133 C136 C137 C138 C139. `date_finished` = `date_start` + `bom.produce_delay` days; if equal to start, postponed by planned work-order minutes or 60 min C132.
**Confirm** `action_confirm`: company check; copy `bom.consumption` onto the MO; force unit of measure of the product for serial-tracked products; `_adjust_procure_method` on raw moves; `_action_confirm(merge=False)` on all moves; confirm work orders and snapshot their cost mode; trigger scheduler for components; confirm linked pickings; set `state='confirmed'` only for drafts C145 C146 C147 C148 C149 C150.
**Lifecycle (state is stored compute):** `draft` (default) → `confirmed` (explicit) → `progress` (explicit `action_start`, or any work order progress/done, `qty_producing` ≠ 0, or any raw move picked) → `to_close` (all work orders done/cancelled, or no work orders and `qty_producing ≥ product_qty`) → `done` (all raw and finished moves done/cancelled, or explicit write at completion) ; `cancel` when state already cancel or all finished moves cancelled C124 C125 C126 C127 C128 C129 C130.
State diagram:
- `draft -> confirmed [action_confirm]`
- `confirmed -> progress [action_start | first work order started | qty_producing set | component picked]`
- `confirmed|progress -> to_close [all work orders done/cancel, or qty_producing >= product_qty without work orders]`
- `progress|to_close|confirmed -> done [button_mark_done → _post_inventory; or all moves done/cancel]`
- `draft|confirmed|progress|to_close -> cancel [action_cancel; or all finished moves cancel; or all raw moves cancel via move cancel]`
- `cancel -> done [special: cancel of a non-done MO whose BoM policy is flexible and that still has done raw moves]`
**Editing rules:** product cannot be changed after draft (silently dropped) C151; `date_start` cannot be changed on done/cancel (UserError) and un-plans a planned MO C152 C153; changing the operation type re-draws the name from the new sequence and re-reserves raw moves C154; adding raw/finished moves/work orders after confirmation auto-confirms them C155. "Quantity to produce" change after confirmation uses wizard `change.production.qty` (new/old ratio applied to raw moves rounded UP, finished moves incremented or copied if they have destination moves, work-order durations re-estimated) C156 C157 C158 C159.
**Update/generate BoM:** `action_update_bom` re-aligns moves/work orders with the (changed) BoM (`_link_bom`); `action_generate_bom` builds a BoM from the MO's moves and work orders and links it back (manager-only button) C160 C161 C162.
**Cancel:** done MOs cannot be cancelled; `_action_cancel` logs exception activities on downstream documents, cancels unfinished work orders, finished and raw moves (context `skip_mo_check`), and cancels linked pickings that have no downstream move and no done MO; a flexible-policy MO left "in progress" is set to done C163 C164 C166 C167 C165. Delete is allowed only on cancelled MOs; done MOs can never be deleted C168.
**Lock:** new MOs are locked unless the user's group implies "Unlocked by default"; `action_toggle_is_locked` has no group check; completion sets `is_locked=True` C169 C170 C171.

## CAP-U14-04 Component reservation, MO readiness and 1/2/3-step manufacturing routing

**Function-ID(s):** `FUNCTION MAPPING REQUIRED (CAP-U14-04)` (existing GRV/SDV IDs describe receipt/delivery steps; no existing ID covers MO reservation or pick-before-manufacture). ### D1 — Business purpose & process semantics

### D3 — Source / technical / workflow logic

- `False (draft|done|cancel) -> confirmed|assigned|waiting [MO confirmed; derived from raw move states]`

## CAP-U14-06 Backorders, splitting and merging of manufacturing orders

## CAP-U14-07 Work-order generation, execution (start/pause/finish) and planning/scheduling on work-center calendars

### D1 — Business purpose & process semantics
