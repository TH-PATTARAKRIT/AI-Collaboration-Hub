## CAP-U22-01 Customer-demand-linked purchasing (traceability, notifications, alternatives)

**Modules in scope:** `sale_purchase_stock`, `sale_purchase_project`, `purchase_requisition_sale` (+ discovered base `sale_purchase`, `sale_stock`, `purchase_stock`, `purchase_requisition`)

**Function-ID(s):** FUNCTION MAPPING REQUIRED

**Claims:** VDR-U22-C001 to VDR-U22-C048

### D1 Business purpose and process semantics
Business purpose: when something sold has to be bought in (goods replenished on demand from a vendor, direct vendor-to-customer shipments, or sold services that are subcontracted), the purchase side must stay tied to the customer order that caused it, so that (a) either document can open the other, (b) the customer's delivery address, routes and analytic context reach the vendor document, (c) the salesperson is warned when no vendor can be found or a purchase is cancelled, and (d) competing vendor quotations created from an order keep the same link. Process semantics: for goods the link runs through the stock reference record shared by the sale order, the moves and the purchase order (a reference replaces the older procurement group in this revision); for subcontracted services the link is the sale-line to purchase-line relation created at order confirmation. The three owned modules are thin bridges that only extend those flows; all state transitions belong to the base order, receipt and procurement engines (U04, U06, U07, U08).

### D2 Architecture, data and object relationships
Objects: `sale.order` (`order_line`, `stock_reference_ids`) <-> `purchase.order` (`order_line`, `reference_ids`) through `stock.reference` (`sale_ids`, `purchase_ids`, many2many tables added by `sale_stock` and `purchase_stock`); `sale.order.line.purchase_line_ids` <-> `purchase.order.line.sale_line_id` (added by `sale_purchase`; related `sale_order_id`); `stock.move.sale_line_id` (set by this bridge only when the final location is customer or transit); `purchase.order.project_id` (via `project_purchase`, populated by `sale_purchase_project`); `purchase.order.dest_address_id` (delivery address, stored computed, readonly when the order has source sale orders). `product.template.service_to_purchase` drives the service path (base `sale_purchase`).

### D3 Source, technical and workflow logic
Control flow, goods: procurement `_run_buy` finds a vendor; with no vendor, moves that propagate cancel are cancelled and others fall back to make-to-stock, then `_notify_responsible` (empty in base, filled by this bridge) posts a message addressing the product responsible and the salesperson on the origin sale orders. When a purchase line is built from a procurement, the bridge sets `sale_line_id` only if the procurement has no downstream move (direct-delivery style), and `_find_candidate` then merges only into purchase lines of the same sale line; `_prepare_stock_moves` copies `sale_line_id` to the generated incoming move only if the move's final location is a customer or transit location and copies the sale line's routes. Control flow, services: `sale.order._action_confirm` -> `_purchase_service_generation` (sudo) -> `_purchase_service_create` -> find or create a draft purchase order of the same vendor/company/sale order -> create purchase line with `sale_line_id`, analytic distribution; `sale_purchase_project` adds the project analytic distribution (only if the sale line has none) and `project_id` on the created purchase order. Cancel paths: `purchase.order.button_cancel` -> activity on source sale orders; `sale.order._action_cancel` -> activity on generated purchase orders. Alternatives: the create-alternative wizard builds line dicts via `_get_alternative_line_value`; the bridge adds `sale_line_id`. Inheritance chain for purchase-line building: purchase_stock -> sale_purchase_stock; for service line values: sale_purchase -> sale_purchase_project.

State diagram:
- sale order draft/sent -> sale [action_confirm; services flagged service_to_purchase generate/extend a draft vendor order via sale_purchase]
- vendor order (none) -> draft [first service line confirmed with no draft vendor order for that sale order, or procurement run for goods]
- vendor order draft -> purchase [normal purchase approval; link stays on the lines]
- vendor order any -> cancel [button_cancel -> warning activity on each source sale order that has a linked line]
- sale order sale -> cancel [_action_cancel -> warning activity on each non-cancelled generated vendor order of service_to_purchase products]
- goods procurement with no vendor: moves (MTO) -> make_to_stock [no matching vendor; salesperson and product responsible notified]

## CAP-U22-02 Promotion, coupon, gift card, eWallet and loyalty program definition, card issuance and balance history (base `loyalty`)

**Modules in scope:** `loyalty` (all models, wizards, portal controller, data, security); order-side application is `sale_loyalty` (U05, referenced only)

**Function-ID(s):** FUNCTION MAPPING REQUIRED

**Claims:** VDR-U22-C049 to VDR-U22-C130

### D1 Business purpose and process semantics
Business purpose: let the company define marketing and prepaid-value programs (promotions, discount codes, coupons, buy-X-get-Y, next-order coupons, loyalty cards, gift cards, eWallets), say how points are earned (rules) and what they can be exchanged for (rewards), issue cards or coupons manually or in bulk, adjust balances by hand with a recorded reason, notify holders by email at creation or when a point milestone is crossed, and show customers their own cards and history in the portal. The base module only defines and stores; it does not apply programs to documents (that is the sales/PoS layer: `sale_loyalty`, U05). Process semantics: a program of a given type is created with type-specific default rules, rewards and communication plan; a card (coupon) belongs to one program, optionally to one partner, carries a points balance, an optional expiry date and a unique code; every manual issue or balance change writes a history line.

### D2 Architecture, data and object relationships
Objects: `loyalty.program` (type, applies_on, trigger, validity dates, usage limit, pricelists, company, currency) 1-N `loyalty.rule` (points earned: per order / per money / per unit; minimum quantity/amount; product filter by ids, category, tag, domain; optional code), 1-N `loyalty.reward` (type product or discount; discount mode percent / per order / per point; applicability order / cheapest / specific; required points; max discount; own hidden service product `discount_line_product_id`), 1-N `loyalty.mail` (communication plan: at creation / when reaching points, mail template on `loyalty.card`); `loyalty.card` (code unique, points, partner, expiry, history) 1-N `loyalty.history` (issued, used, description, generic order reference via `order_model` + `order_id`). Transient: `loyalty.generate.wizard`, `loyalty.card.update.balance`. Extensions of other models: `product.product`/`product.template` (protect reward products and the seeded gift-card and eWallet products), `product.pricelist` (archive guard), `res.partner` (active card counter, merge handling), `base.partner.merge.automatic.wizard` (card merge), portal controller `CustomerPortalLoyalty`.

### D3 Source, technical and workflow logic
Control flow: program creation -> `default_get` and `_compute_from_program_type` rewrite rules/rewards/communication plan with the defaults for the chosen `program_type` (`_program_type_default_values`); reward creation -> `_create_missing_discount_line_products` creates one hidden service product per reward; card creation -> `_send_creation_communication` (skipped by context flags or when the card has no customer); card write of `points` -> `_send_points_reach_communication` (highest crossed milestone only, never on decrease). Generation: `loyalty.generate.wizard.generate_coupons` builds cards (one per selected partner, or `coupon_qty` anonymous) then one history line per card. Manual adjustment: `loyalty.card.update.balance.action_update_card_point` computes the difference, writes history, then sets the points. Partner merge: `_merge_loyalty_cards` sums points of nominative cards per program onto one surviving card and zeroes/archives the others. Portal: card history and reward list routes restricted to the user's own cards. Archiving a program propagates `active` to rules, rewards, communication plan and discount products. Inheritance: `loyalty` is extended by `sale_loyalty` (cards get an order link, history gets order URL, program/reward/rule gain order logic), `sale_loyalty_delivery` (free shipping reward, CAP-U22-03) and, when installed, point-of-sale/website modules (not installed here).

State diagram:
- program active -> archived [write active=False; rules, rewards, communication plan and reward products archived]
- program archived -> active [write active=True; children and reward products unarchived]
- program active -> deleted blocked [unlink raises while active]; archived -> deleted [allowed]
- card created -> (mail at creation sent if plan exists and holder known)
- card points A -> B [generate wizard, update-balance wizard, sale-side order flow, partner merge]; history line written by the wizards
- card active -> archived with 0 points [partner merge for surplus nominative cards]
- program type X -> Y [write program_type; rules, rewards, communication plan replaced by the new type's defaults]

## CAP-U22-03 Free-shipping reward and prepaid-instrument restrictions on delivery payment (`sale_loyalty_delivery`)

**Modules in scope:** `sale_loyalty_delivery` (+ discovered `sale_loyalty`, `delivery`, `stock_delivery`, `payment`)

**Function-ID(s):** FUNCTION MAPPING REQUIRED

**Claims:** VDR-U22-C131 to VDR-U22-C167

### D1 Business purpose and process semantics
Business purpose: let a promotion or loyalty reward waive the shipping charge, keep delivery charges from distorting the minimum-purchase and points rules, keep the free-delivery threshold meaningful when part of the order is paid by a gift card or eWallet, and stop customers combining prepaid instruments with pay-on-delivery payment methods. Process semantics: the reward is a negative order line equal to the delivery line price (capped by an optional maximum), created and recomputed by the loyalty engine whenever the order is updated; it never changes the carrier or the delivery line itself.

### D2 Architecture, data and object relationships
Objects: `loyalty.reward.reward_type` gains `shipping` (selection_add, `ondelete set default`); `loyalty.reward.discount_max_amount` reused as the maximum; `sale.order` overrides for `_get_reward_values_free_shipping`, `_get_reward_line_values`, `_get_claimable_rewards`, `_get_no_effect_on_threshold_lines`, `_get_not_rewarded_order_lines`, `_compute_amount_total_without_delivery`; `loyalty.program` template/default overrides; `payment.provider._get_compatible_providers` and the website payment controller `_create_transaction`. Order line flags used: `is_delivery`, `is_reward_line`, `coupon_id`, `reward_id`, `points_cost`.

### D3 Source, technical and workflow logic
Control flow: loyalty engine (`sale_loyalty`) -> `_get_reward_line_values(reward, coupon)` -> shipping branch -> `_get_reward_values_free_shipping` returns one line dict (price_unit = -min(max amount or infinity, first delivery line price or 0), quantity 1, points cost = required points or the whole wallet when clear_wallet, taxes of the delivery product mapped by the fiscal position, sequence after all non-reward lines). Claimable rewards: when any applied reward on the order is a shipping reward, shipping rewards are removed from the claimable set so only one can be active. Threshold and points: delivery lines and shipping-reward lines are excluded from the minimum-amount/quantity check, and delivery lines are excluded from reward point earning. Free-delivery threshold: `_compute_amount_total_without_delivery` = base (total minus delivery lines) minus the sum of `price_unit` of gift card and eWallet lines (negative values, hence added back) - used by carrier `free_over` and by `stock_delivery.send_to_shipper`. Payment: provider compatibility removes pay-on-delivery providers when the order has points entries of gift card or eWallet programs; the controller override raises ValidationError when a transaction on such a provider is created for such an order. Inheritance chain: `sale` -> `delivery` -> `sale_loyalty` -> `sale_loyalty_delivery` for the order hooks; `payment` -> `delivery` (cash on delivery codes) -> `sale_loyalty_delivery` for provider filtering.

State diagram:
- order without shipping reward -> order with shipping reward line [loyalty engine applies reward; one at a time]
- shipping reward line price A -> B [engine recompute after delivery line or carrier changes; capped by max amount]
- order with gift card or eWallet points -> pay-on-delivery providers hidden [provider compatibility]
- order with prepaid instrument + pay-on-delivery transaction -> ValidationError [controller transaction creation]

## CAP-U22-09 Print-on-demand fulfilment through an external service (Gelato): order forwarding, shipping quotes, status webhook, product sync

### D1 Business purpose and process semantics
Business purpose: sell products that are printed and shipped by an external print-on-demand service.
