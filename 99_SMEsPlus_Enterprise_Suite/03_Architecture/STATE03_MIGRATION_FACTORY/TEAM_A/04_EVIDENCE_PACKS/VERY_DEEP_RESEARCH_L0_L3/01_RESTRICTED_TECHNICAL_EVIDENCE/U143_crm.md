## SUPPLEMENTARY NOTES

### Lead Lifecycle State Machine
```
create (type='lead') → qualify → convert_opportunity (type='opportunity') → stages → action_set_won (won) OR action_set_lost (lost/archived)
```
- won: stage.is_won=True AND probability=100 AND active=True
- lost: active=False AND probability=0
- pending: everything else

### No Accounting Effect
CRM leads/opportunities have zero accounting journal entries. `expected_revenue` and `recurring_revenue` are pipeline forecast fields only. The only financial integration is via `sale_crm`: a confirmed sale.order can update `expected_revenue` upward.
