## 3. Key Finding — GAP-018 Resolution

**GAP-018 status: CLOSED (isolation IS enforced at posting time)**

The prior U71 assessment (NOT_PROVEN for runtime isolation) is resolved. Source evidence at `account/models/account_move.py:5673-5679` confirms that `AccountMove._post` enforces cross-company account isolation by:

1. Building the authorized company set as `move.company_id.parent_ids` (move company + all ancestors)
2. Filtering all line accounts for those with `company_ids` disjoint from the authorized set
3. Raising a `UserError` listing the offending accounts, preventing posting

**Design nuance:** The isolation is hierarchically aware — a parent company's accounts are accessible from child companies, and a child posting against a parent account is explicitly permitted. Cross-hierarchy sharing (sibling companies, or unrelated companies) is blocked at posting time. Draft-state entries are not protected (see C-021 — GAP flag).

**Bank/Cash absolute prohibition:** Bank and Cash accounts (asset_cash type) are additionally prohibited from any multi-company sharing at all (C-005), creating a stricter sub-class of accounts.
