## Summary

GAP-027 CLOSED. `_check_tax_lock_date()` is called at **7 distinct enforcement points** across `account_move_line.create` (line 1795), `account_move_line.write` (lines 1888, 1925), `account_move_line.unlink` (line 2004), `account_move.write` (lines 3951, 3956, 4002), plus a bank-statement unreconcile sub-path (line 1882). Enforcement targets only lines where `_affect_tax_report()` is True. No security group bypass exists within the enforcement function; the only bypass paths are the sentinel context key or a structured lock exception record. The `_post` method auto-advances dates rather than hard-blocking.
