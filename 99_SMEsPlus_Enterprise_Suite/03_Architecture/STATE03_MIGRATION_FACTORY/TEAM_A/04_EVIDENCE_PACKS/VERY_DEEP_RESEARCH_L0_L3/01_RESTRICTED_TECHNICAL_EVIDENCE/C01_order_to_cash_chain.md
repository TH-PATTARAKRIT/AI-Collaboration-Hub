## CAP-C01-01 Happy path end-to-end (quotation to paid invoice)

**Function-ID(s):** `SDV-F01`, `SDV-F02`, `SDV-F04`, `SDV-F05`, `PDT-F01`, `PCO-F01` (partial matches, assigned claim by claim); the chain itself has no single existing ID: FUNCTION MAPPING REQUIRED.

### D1 — Business purpose and process semantics
The chain turns a customer offer into delivered goods, a legally numbered invoice and settled money. It deliberately separates five acts: commitment (confirmation), fulfilment (delivery), billing (draft invoice), ledger recognition (posting) and settlement (payment plus reconciliation) VDR-C01-C257 VDR-C01-C008 VDR-C01-C049 VDR-C01-C066. Each act is a separate user action with its own role: the sales user confirms and may create draft invoices in superuser mode, the stock user validates deliveries, the invoicing user posts invoices and registers payments VDR-C01-C041 VDR-C01-C050 VDR-C01-C236. Numbers appear at different moments (order and delivery at creation, invoice and payment entry at posting) VDR-C01-C001 VDR-C01-C020 VDR-C01-C052. The order has no "paid" status; paid-ness is a property of the invoice and the payment VDR-C01-C075 VDR-C01-C071.

### D2 — Architecture, data and object relationships
- `sale.order` 1—n `sale.order.line`; the line is the reconciliation unit between ordered, delivered and invoiced quantity VDR-C01-C034 VDR-C01-C033 VDR-C01-C044.
- Delivery side: `stock.reference` (grouping, replaces procurement group) VDR-C01-C014, `stock.picking` 1—n `stock.move` 1—n `stock.move.line`; link back to the order through `stock.move.sale_line_id` and computed `stock.picking.sale_id` (sale_stock/models/stock.py:15-17, 184-195) VDR-C01-C024.
- Billing side: `account.move` (out_invoice) 1—n `account.move.line`, linked to order lines by the many2many `sale_line_ids` / `invoice_lines` VDR-C01-C044; payments by `account.payment` + its own entry (`origin_payment_id`) and the invoice link `matched_payment_ids` VDR-C01-C069 VDR-C01-C070.
- Valuation side: `stock.move.value` stored per done move, optional `account.move.line` of type cogs, optional stock journal entry linked by `stock.move.account_move_id` VDR-C01-C027 VDR-C01-C058 VDR-C01-C060.
- Extension chain for `_action_confirm`: sale (empty hook) + sale_stock + sale_project + sale_purchase (+ delivery, repair, event, loyalty, crm, partnership per U04 C083-C095); effective order of effects is load-order dependent VDR-C01-C076.

## CAP-C01-02 Invoicing-policy variants (ordered vs delivered)

**Function-ID(s):** `SDV-F04` (invoicing policy), `PDT-F01` (per-shipment alignment, sales side), `PDT-F04` (cross-shipment quantity reconciliation, sales side). Service-policy and automatic-invoice variants: FUNCTION MAPPING REQUIRED beyond what U05 CAP-U05-01/02 mapped.

### D1 — Business purpose and process semantics
Each product states whether the customer is billed for what was ordered or for what was delivered; the chain's sequence of invoice creation relative to delivery differs accordingly VDR-C01-C259 VDR-C01-C077. Goods default to ordered basis in 19 and are reset to ordered whenever the product type is recalculated; delivered basis must be chosen deliberately VDR-C01-C078 VDR-C01-C079. Services pick among prepaid (ordered), manual delivered, milestones and timesheets, all mapped onto the two bases VDR-C01-C094 VDR-C01-C095.

### D2 — Architecture, data and object relationships
Product `invoice_policy` (stored, tracked) -> read live by `sale.order.line._compute_qty_to_invoice` (no store trigger on the product, so no retroactive recompute) VDR-C01-C081 -> `qty_to_invoice` -> line `invoice_status` -> order `invoice_status`; delivered quantity from `stock.move` (sale_stock) or manual/analytic/timesheet/milestone sources VDR-C01-C034 VDR-C01-C035 VDR-C01-C036 VDR-C01-C095. Three "to invoice" measures exist with different timing VDR-C01-C098.

### D3 — Source, technical and workflow logic
Sequence difference (goods):
| Aspect | Ordered basis (`order`) | Delivered basis (`delivery`) |
|---|---|---|
| Billable quantity | ordered - invoiced from confirmation VDR-C01-C083 | delivered - invoiced; zero until a done outgoing move counts VDR-C01-C084 |
| Earliest invoice | right after confirmation (no delivery needed) | after the first completed delivery |
| What blocks creation | state not sale; nothing left to bill; cancelled order VDR-C01-C082 VDR-C01-C046 | same, plus no delivered quantity (error text suggests switching policy) VDR-C01-C091 |
| Status values on line | to invoice -> invoiced; upselling when delivered > ordered and nothing left VDR-C01-C085 VDR-C01-C086 | no -> to invoice (per shipment) -> invoiced; closure rule when moves are all done/cancelled and some delivered VDR-C01-C087 |
| Partial delivery | no effect on billing (already billable in full) | each completed shipment raises delivered quantity; next invoice carries the delta VDR-C01-C097 |
| Backorder declined | no effect | remaining moves cancelled; after billing the delivered part the line shows invoiced although billed < ordered VDR-C01-C087 VDR-C01-C168 |
| Down payment lines | order status ignores them VDR-C01-C096 | same |
| Negative billable (after return/refund) | refund lowers invoiced qty so billable turns positive again | return lowers delivered qty so billable turns negative; invoiced only when final VDR-C01-C089 |
Order-level status: any counted line to invoice -> to invoice (unless only special lines); all invoiced -> invoiced; all invoiced/upselling -> upselling VDR-C01-C036 VDR-C01-C090.
Automatic invoicing overrides policy: fully paid online orders are billed on ordered quantities; partial payment makes a down payment; the setting is switched off when the default policy is not ordered VDR-C01-C092 VDR-C01-C093.
State list (line):
- no -> to invoice [ordered: confirmation; delivered: delivered quantity rises]
- to invoice -> invoiced [draft invoice created; quantity invoiced >= ordered, or delivered-basis closure]
- invoiced -> to invoice [invoice cancelled, credit note, or return lowers delivered under delivered basis]
- * -> upselling [ordered basis, delivered > ordered, nothing to bill]
- * -> no [order not in state sale]

## CAP-C01-03 Return and credit-note chain

**Function-ID(s):** `SDV-F06` (return via reverse transfer, pre-invoice), `SDV-F07` (return after invoicing via credit note); supporting `SDV-F05` (valuation timing). Refund without return: FUNCTION MAPPING REQUIRED.

### D1 — Business purpose and process semantics
