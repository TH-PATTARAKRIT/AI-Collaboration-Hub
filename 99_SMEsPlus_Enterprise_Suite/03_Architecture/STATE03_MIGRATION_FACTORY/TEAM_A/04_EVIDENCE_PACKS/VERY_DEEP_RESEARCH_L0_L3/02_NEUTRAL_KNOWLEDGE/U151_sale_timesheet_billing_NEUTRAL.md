## KEY FINDINGS

1. Two billing models coexist: delivery-based (invoice from actual hours, locked after posting) and ordered-prepaid (invoice at confirmation, upsell trigger on over-delivery).
2. The SOL-to-timesheet chain flows in both directions: the order item drives delivered-quantity calculation, and the time entry carries a computed-then-stored back-reference to the order item.
3. Three project pricing modes (task rate, fixed rate, employee rate) determine which order item each time entry references; the employee-rate mode uses a dedicated mapping table.
4. Invoice linking is a post-creation step that stamps a reference on qualifying time entries; removal of invoice lines reverses this stamp without disturbing task/project assignments.
5. Credit notes and modify-reversals include explicit handlers to restore or re-link time entries so that the billable state remains consistent throughout the invoice lifecycle.
