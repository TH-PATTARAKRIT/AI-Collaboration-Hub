# U131 — Function-ID Mapping G10–G16 (neutral knowledge)

> NEUTRAL DISTRIBUTION — TECHNICAL CODE REFERENCES REMOVED
> Status: DEEPSEEK-REPORTED / PENDING CLAUDE VERIFICATION
- Unit: U131
- Scope: GAP-034 — Function-ID mapping for units U47–U68
- Date: 2026-10-02

---

## Summary

This unit closes GAP-034 by assigning Function-IDs to the 22 research units spanning G10–G16 capability groups (U47 through U68). Thirty F-ID mapping claims are recorded. Twelve new domain prefixes are introduced to cover capability areas not represented in the prior domain index. Two units (U66 and U67) have no mappable capabilities recorded in their handoff packets.
