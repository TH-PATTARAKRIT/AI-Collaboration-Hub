## ARCHITECTURE SUMMARY

The Community financial reporting engine is a data-driven framework where reports, lines, columns, and expressions are all ordinary database records. No hard-coded Python report classes exist for the financial statements themselves; the computation engines interpret the formula and date-scope metadata at runtime. The six computation engines cover the majority of statutory reporting needs without custom code. Carryover, cross-report aggregation, and composite multi-section reports are first-class framework features. The Enterprise `account_reports` addon adds P&L, Balance Sheet, and other statutory reports purely as XML data records that consume this framework, requiring no additional Python engine code.
