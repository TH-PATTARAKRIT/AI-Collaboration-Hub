## Overview

This document records twenty-five independent claim verifications performed against the Odoo Community 19 source code. Five prior evidence units were selected based on file size, and five representative claims were drawn from each. Every claim was checked by locating the referenced source file and line number, confirming the anchor text is present, and verifying that the technical statement matches what the source actually says.
