## Structural Summary

**What hr.version is:** A time-stamped snapshot of all employee employment conditions. One employee has one or more ordered version records. The version effective on a given date is the one with the highest effective date on or before that date. Together, the version history represents the complete employment record of an employee.

**What hr.contract was (prior releases):** A separate model representing a signed employment contract with a state machine (draft, open, close). It held wage, dates, job, and department. A Many2one `contract_id` on employee pointed to the current contract.

**The replacement:** In Odoo 19 CE, `hr.version` absorbs all contract fields. There is no separate contract record. Employees carry all their employment data in versioned snapshots. Contract dates (`contract_date_start`, `contract_date_end`) remain as fields on each version, enabling the period-based checks that previously relied on the contract state machine.

**Migration consequence:** Any integration, report, or customization that reads or writes `hr.contract` records, uses `hr.employee.contract_id`, or depends on `hr.contract.state` will fail in Odoo 19 CE and must be rewritten to use `hr.version`.
