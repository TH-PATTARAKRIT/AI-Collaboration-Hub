## Neutral Summary

The live chat system in Odoo 19 Community is built on three interlocking layers:

**Configuration layer** (`im_livechat.channel`): Named channel records group agents, URL rules, and session limits. There is no website-level isolation in Community. Each channel can have multiple chatbot scripts attached via rules that fire based on URL regex patterns and country.

**Session layer** (`discuss.channel` with channel_type=livechat`): Each visitor conversation is a unified messaging channel record. The session carries its own operator reference, a session end timestamp (NULL while open), a chatbot step pointer, and extensive history tracking. Sessions are closed by explicit visitor/operator departure or by garbage collection. Outcome and escalation status are recorded for reporting.

**Identity layer** (`mail.guest` / `res.users`): Anonymous visitors are tracked via signed cookie-backed guest records from the mail module. Authenticated visitors use their partner. Operators are internal users with optional livechat-specific display name, language preferences, and expertise.

**Key migration considerations:**
- Chat sessions, member history, chatbot messages, and ratings are all separate tables that must migrate together.
- Chatbot scripts have associated inactive partner records that must also migrate.
- The real-time notification infrastructure (websocket / bus) must be validated independently of the data migration.
- CRM lead creation requires the bridge module to be in scope.
- Multi-website scoping is not available in Community — any per-website channel routing must be designed around URL rules.
