## Summary

The tax computation engine in v19 introduces these key architectural changes versus earlier versions:

1. Price-include is now computed from a per-tax override plus company default rather than stored directly.
2. The internal engine works in three ordered passes (fixed, price-included, price-excluded) rather than a single loop.
3. Reverse-charge and withholding structures are detected automatically via the negative-factor flag and handled by a parallel counter-entry calculation.
4. Tax-grid tags moved to distribution lines in earlier versions and remain there in v19.
5. The Python-formula computation type is absent from the type selection list though its name persists in one guard clause.
