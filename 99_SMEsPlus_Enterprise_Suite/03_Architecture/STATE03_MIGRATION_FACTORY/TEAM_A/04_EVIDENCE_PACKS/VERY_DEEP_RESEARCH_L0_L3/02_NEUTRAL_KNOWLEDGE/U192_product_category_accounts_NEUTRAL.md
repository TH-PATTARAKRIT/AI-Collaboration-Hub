## Summary of Key Migration Implications

1. **ir.property → ir.default**: Any migration script that copies company-dependent product.category account values must target `ir.default`, not `ir.property`.
2. **Input/Output accounts removed**: v16 `property_stock_account_input_categ_id` and `property_stock_account_output_categ_id` do not exist in v19. The single `property_stock_valuation_account_id` plus `account_stock_variation_id` (on the account) replaces them.
3. **Three-tier resolution**: All three tiers (product, category, company) must be verified to be correctly populated during migration.
4. **Thai COA**: l10n_th requires manual category account assignment; no demo defaults are provided.
5. **Category tree**: Deep category trees can mask missing account assignments — always test resolution from leaf categories.
