# U101 Neutral Knowledge — Cash Basis Accounting
## DEEPSEEK-REPORTED / PENDING CLAUDE VERIFICATION
> NEUTRAL LAYER — no source paths, no class/method names, no file extensions, no snake_case, no backticks

| NR-ID | Statement |
|---|---|
| NR-U101-001 | Each tax record carries an exigibility setting that controls when the tax becomes due. The two allowed values are "based on invoice" (tax is due at invoice posting) and "based on payment" (tax is due only when payment is received). The default is based-on-invoice. |
| NR-U101-002 | A company-level visibility flag suppresses the exigibility selector on the tax record form when the company has not enabled the cash basis feature. This prevents configuration of payment-basis taxes before the feature is turned on. |
| NR-U101-003 | Each payment-basis tax requires a transition account. This is an intermediate balance-sheet account that holds the tax amount between invoice posting and payment. The account must be reconcilable. When the invoice is paid and reconciled, the balance on the transition account is cleared and the final tax account receives the amount. |
| NR-U101-004 | A validation rule enforces that whenever a tax is set to payment-basis mode, its transition account must be configured as a reconcilable account. An error is raised if the constraint is violated. |
| NR-U101-005 | At the company level, three settings govern the cash basis feature: (1) a master on/off switch; (2) the journal in which all cash basis entries will be recorded; (3) an optional account for tracking the base amounts of payment-basis taxes. |
| NR-U101-006 | All three company-level cash basis settings are exposed in the accounting configuration screen so administrators can manage them without navigating to company settings directly. |
| NR-U101-007 | The accounting settings screen prevents the cash basis feature from being disabled when any tax in the company is still configured as payment-basis. A warning is shown and the switch is forced back to enabled. |
| NR-U101-008 | Every journal entry carries three optional linkage fields supporting the cash basis feature: a reference to the partial reconciliation that triggered the cash basis entry, a reference to the original invoice from which the cash basis entry was generated, and an inverse list showing all cash basis entries produced by a given invoice. |
| NR-U101-009 | Journal entries that contain no receivable or payable lines are flagged as "always exigible." On these entries all taxes are immediately included in tax reporting regardless of payment-basis configuration, because there is no payment event to await. |
| NR-U101-010 | The always-exigible flag is computed from two conditions: the entry must not be an invoice-type document, and the entry must have no payment-basis lines requiring deferred treatment. |
| NR-U101-011 | When a reconciliation event occurs, the system first inspects the originating journal entry to determine what cash basis work is needed. It collects all payment-basis tax lines, all base lines associated with payment-basis taxes, and the receivable or payable lines, then computes the total outstanding balance. It returns a structured result or nothing if no deferred taxes are present. |
| NR-U101-012 | The partial reconciliation record is the coordination point for cash basis processing. It calls the per-move collection method for each side of a reconciliation, validates that a cash basis journal is configured on the company, and computes the proportion of the invoice that has now been paid (the reconciled percentage) along with the applicable exchange rate and settlement date. |
| NR-U101-013 | If the cash basis journal is not configured at the time a payment-basis tax is due, the system raises a user-facing error directing the user to configure the journal in accounting settings. |
| NR-U101-014 | The main cash basis entry creation routine builds one journal entry per partial reconciliation event. The entry is dated at the settlement date (minimum: the day after the fiscal lock date), references the original invoice name, is posted to the cash basis journal, and carries links back to both the triggering reconciliation and the originating invoice. |
| NR-U101-015 | Each cash basis journal entry contains pairs of lines: a line mirroring the tax or base amount at the proportion of the reconciled percentage, and a counterpart line using the transition account to clear the deferred balance. |
| NR-U101-016 | For tax base lines in a cash basis entry, the account used is the company-level base-tax-received account if configured; otherwise the original base line account is used. Only payment-basis taxes are included in the tax references on this line. |
| NR-U101-017 | For tax amount lines in a cash basis entry, the account resolution order is: the repartition line account, then the company base-tax-received account, then the original tax line account. The base amount is carried from the originating tax line. |
| NR-U101-018 | The counterpart line for a tax amount in a cash basis entry uses the original tax account from the invoice (the transition account), with debit and credit inverted. This is the line that clears the transition account. |
| NR-U101-019 | Cash basis entry creation is triggered inside the reconciliation plan execution. A local check tests whether the company has cash basis enabled and whether the lines being reconciled are on receivable or payable account types. Both conditions must be true for the cash basis entries to be generated. |
| NR-U101-020 | Two context flags can suppress cash basis entry creation: one used during move reversal (to avoid creating a new cash basis entry when the reversal itself is the cancellation mechanism), and one available for programmatic callers that need to suppress cash basis processing. |
| NR-U101-021 | When a partial reconciliation is deleted (undone), the system automatically locates all cash basis entries that were linked to that reconciliation and reverses them. This ensures the tax reporting always mirrors the actual payment state. |
| NR-U101-022 | A cash basis journal entry cannot be manually reset to draft. The system raises an error if either linkage field (to the originating reconciliation or to the originating invoice) is set. |
| NR-U101-023 | The tax reporting domain excludes original payment-basis tax lines from tax report results (they are not yet exigible). It includes: lines from cash basis entries (exigible by event), lines from entries with no payable/receivable lines (always exigible), lines with no tax associations, and lines from non-payment-basis taxes. |
| NR-U101-024 | At invoice posting time, the account assigned to a payment-basis tax line is the transition account (not the final tax account). This substitution is performed by the repartition line account resolution method, which checks the exigibility and the context before returning the transition account. |
| NR-U101-025 | Cash basis entries are created in posted state when both the invoice and the payment are already posted. If either document is still in draft at reconciliation time, the cash basis entry is created in draft and promoted to posted when both sides are confirmed. |
| NR-U101-026 | After cash basis entries are created, the system reconciles the original transition account lines with the corresponding counterpart lines in the new cash basis entries. This clears the transition account balance by matching equal and opposite amounts. |
| NR-U101-027 | A snapshot of the invoice state is stored on the partial reconciliation record at the time of reconciliation. On invoice posting, the snapshot is compared to the current state; if the invoice changed in a way that affects the cash basis entry, the reconciliation is undone and the user must redo it. |
| NR-U101-028 | When a draft invoice with existing reconciliations is posted, the system checks whether the stored snapshot matches the current invoice state. Draft cash basis entries that are still valid are posted alongside the invoice; invalid ones are discarded and must be recreated by rereconciling. |
| NR-U101-029 | There is no separate accounting module for cash basis taxes in Odoo 19 Community. All logic — configuration, entry generation, reconciliation triggering, reversal, and tax reporting exclusion — is contained within the core accounting module. |
