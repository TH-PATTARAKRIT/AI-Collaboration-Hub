## Summary of Migration Flags

1. **No `own` usage type** — v19 community has exactly 7 usage values; `own` does not exist.
2. **No `scrap_location` boolean field** — scrap locations are distinguished solely by `usage='inventory'`.
3. **No `return_location` boolean field** — not present in v19 community stock module.
4. **No `pos_location_id` field on location** — not present in v19 community stock module.
5. **`valuation_account_id`** lives in the `stock_account` module extension, not in the base stock module.
6. **`transit` usage** does NOT bypass reservation — migration scripts must not treat transit as virtual for reservation purposes.
7. **Global locations** (supplier, customer, inter-company transit) have no company assignment and are shared across all tenants.
