## Summary Finding

**GAP-018: CLOSED** — Runtime per-company COA isolation is enforced at posting time via an intersection check between each account's ownership list and the journal entry's company ancestry chain. The mechanism blocks posting of entries that use accounts owned exclusively by unrelated companies. The isolation is hierarchy-aware by design: parent-company accounts are accessible from child companies. A secondary gap remains (C-019, C-021) in that draft entries and line-level constraints do not enforce isolation, and the check fires only at posting.

Bank and Cash accounts carry an absolute sharing prohibition enforced at the field-write level, providing stronger isolation for liquid asset accounts.
