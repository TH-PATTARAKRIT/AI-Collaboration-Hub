# U30 - Tax-item synchronisation, document tax totals and cash-basis exigibility - Neutral Knowledge

> DEEPSEEK-REPORTED / PENDING CLAUDE VERIFICATION. Clean-room knowledge layer for Thai Tax Core unit U30 (study of Odoo 19 Community behaviour).
> Describes what a ledger system does to keep derived tax, payment-term, rounding and discount items consistent, how document tax totals are built, and how cash-basis tax entries arise and reverse. It states system behaviour only; statutory requirements are validated elsewhere and nothing here is a legal conclusion.
> No V-level, no Complete, no coverage percentage, no Gate PASS. Statements marked inferred or needing execution are not confirmed by running the system.

## CAP-U30-01 Synchronisation of derived journal items (framework, order, protection, balance check)

### WHAT
- [N-U30-001] An invoice or similar document carries journal items that the user does not type but the ledger derives: due-date items for the receivable or payable, tax items, cash-rounding items, early-payment-discount items, discount-allocation items, private-part items for partly deductible purchases, and an automatic balancing item on entries that use taxes. A synchronisation layer keeps all of them consistent with the user-entered lines whenever a document or one of its items is created, changed or deleted.
- [N-U30-002] Synchronisation covers invoice-like documents; ordinary journal entries are covered only when their items carry taxes, and cash-basis entries generated by the system are excluded from automatic balancing.

### WHY
- [N-U30-003] Users enter products, prices, taxes and settings only; the ledger derives the accounting items so totals, taxes and the receivable or payable agree and every entry stays balanced without manual bookkeeping. Line subtotals are computed apart from the synchronisation so users see figures on an unsaved draft.

### BUSINESS RULE
- [N-U30-004] Derived items follow a fixed chain with a numbered position for each rule: due-date items, automatic balancing, rounding items, discount allocation, tax items, private-part items, early-payment items, partner propagation. The before-state of each rule is captured in ascending order, but the recomputation after a change runs in reverse, so partner propagation and early-payment items come first, tax items in the middle, and due-date items last, reading final totals (inferred from the unwinding order; not executed).
- [N-U30-005] Tax items, rounding items, automatic balancing and private-part items are recomputed only while the document is not posted; posted documents are never re-derived.
- [N-U30-006] Each rule compares what it needs before and after the change and acts only if its own inputs moved; needs for items that were deleted during the change are dropped, and several needs with the same identity are added together, disappearing when they sum to zero.
- [N-U30-007] Manual input is respected: if the needs did not change nothing is touched, and if there was no need before while items already differ, those items are treated as created by hand. Otherwise items whose identity is no longer needed are deleted, missing identities are created and items whose values differ are rewritten.
- [N-U30-008] When one item must disappear and another must appear, the old item is rewritten in place so its identity, and any reconciliation attached to it, survives.
- [N-U30-009] After any creation, change or deletion of a document or item, the ledger asserts that debits equal credits after rounding in company currency and refuses the operation otherwise; only the outermost operation asserts, nested operations made by the system do not.
- [N-U30-010] Changes made by the synchronisation itself do not trigger a further synchronisation; the guard is kept per database connection, not per record.
- [N-U30-011] Values supplied by a caller for fields that would normally be recomputed are protected from recomputation during that operation, and derived items are created without leaking the defaults of the calling screen.

### STATE
- [N-U30-012] A document cannot be created already posted. While it is a draft its derived items are live; once posted, its items, date, partner, payment term, currency, fiscal position and cash-rounding method cannot be written, and the taxes of a posted item cannot be changed without resetting the document to draft.

### OPTIONALITY
- [N-U30-013] Callers may suppress the balance check or the synchronisation for a nested operation, and a read-only bypass exists for posted documents; no installed module suppresses the balance check, and the only module that does so (point of sale) is not installed.

### DEPENDENCY
- [N-U30-014] Sales, purchases, expenses and electronic-invoice import all call the same framework; business-model synchronisation for payments and bank statements is triggered after the ledger change with the names of the changed fields.

### CONSTRAINT
- [N-U30-015] A user cannot delete a tax item while the document has taxed items, nor a due-date item; only the synchronisation can. Items with an amount on a posted document, items of a locked (hashed) document, and documents posted once under the restrictive audit trail cannot be deleted; a numbered document that is not last in its sequence cannot be deleted by a non-manager. Access is by role and company: the invoicing group has full rights, the read-only accounting group read rights, and a company rule limits every user to the allowed companies.
- [N-U30-016] Changes that touch amounts, taxes or tags of posted items are checked against the tax lock date, and changes that touch accounts, journals, currencies or partners against the fiscal lock; changing a matched item dissolves its reconciliation; deleting items unreconciles them first and is recorded in the audit trail for documents posted before.

### RISK
- [N-U30-017] Because untouched manual items survive any edit that does not move their inputs, a hand-edited due-date or tax item can drift from the document later; and editing a tax amount in the totals summary re-runs the due-date derivation even where the recursion guard would otherwise skip synchronisation (inferred).

### UNKNOWN
- [N-U30-018] The numeric outcome of the reverse-order chain for combined edits (several lines, rounding item, tax items and due dates at once) has not been executed and needs runtime documents in baht and in one foreign currency.

## CAP-U30-02 Payment-term items (due-date items of receivable and payable)

### WHAT
- [N-U30-019] Receivable (sales) or payable (purchases) items, one per due date, are derived from the payment term, the document date, the document currency and the final totals; without a payment term a single item for the full signed total is derived at the due date.

### WHY
- [N-U30-020] The instalment computation is built so that the instalments always add up to the document total in both currencies, including when cash rounding applies.

### BUSINESS RULE
- [N-U30-021] A due-date item is identified by its document, due date and discount date; two instalments with the same identity are merged by adding their amounts.
- [N-U30-022] The last instalment always receives the remainder in each currency, so the sum equals the total exactly.
- [N-U30-023] Percent instalments are rounded independently in each currency from the respective totals; fixed instalments are converted to company currency at a rate implied by the totals (not the stored document rate) and rounded.
- [N-U30-024] With cash rounding each instalment except the last is rounded to the coin in document currency and its company amount re-derived from that rounded figure at the implied rate.
- [N-U30-025] An early-payment discount is carried on the due-date item as a discount date and discounted amounts in both currencies; it is computed on the total or on the untaxed amount depending on the mode, and is allowed only on a single 100 percent instalment with strictly positive percentage and days.
- [N-U30-026] The document due date is the latest instalment date, otherwise the existing due date, otherwise today.
- [N-U30-027] A document without product, section or note lines needs no due-date items.
- [N-U30-028] For an unsaved record the totals feeding the terms come from freshly computed tax figures; for a stored document they come from the stored amounts.

### STATE
- [N-U30-029] No explicit draft test limits the due-date derivation; on posted documents its inputs are frozen by the read-only and lock rules (inferred).

### OPTIONALITY
- [N-U30-030] A payment term is optional; without one the document still receives one due-date item.

### DEPENDENCY
- [N-U30-031] For documents created from company-paid expenses the due-date derivation is replaced: one item dated today, equal to minus the sum of all other items, on the expense destination account.

### CONSTRAINT
- [N-U30-032] On sales documents a due-date item must be on a receivable account and only such items may use it; payable accounts are refused. Purchases mirror the rule.

### RISK
- [N-U30-033] Changing the document date moves instalment dates and the default rate; instalments derive their rate from totals, so a manually set document rate does not enter them.

### UNKNOWN
- [N-U30-034] The sign passed to the instalment computation differs between the unsaved preview and the stored branch; only fixed-amount instalments use it, so a preview of such terms could show the opposite sign (inferred; needs execution).

## CAP-U30-03 Tax-item generation and update from base items

### WHAT
- [N-U30-035] Tax items are produced from the base items of a document. Base items are product, early-payment, rounding and private-part items; each tax item is identified by the tax distribution line, partner, currency, account, analytic split, tags, group tax and the later taxes it affects. Base items receive the tax tags and an amount equal to the direction sign times the line total plus any rounding delta.

### WHY
- [N-U30-036] Tax items are prepared as a comparison with the existing ones (update, delete, add) rather than recreated each time, so existing items keep their identity where their identity is unchanged.

### BUSINESS RULE
- [N-U30-037] For a draft the tax items are recomputed when a base item changes in price, quantity, discount, deductibility, account, partner, currency, analytic split or taxes; when the currency or the document type changes (full recomputation); or when only the document rate changes (foreign amounts kept, company amounts re-derived). The document-level partner and invoice date are captured but not compared.
- [N-U30-038] Hand-edited tax amounts are kept when the change does not involve taxes, when the set of tax items changed or when a tax item is protected, and they are replaced by computed amounts otherwise; a document created with all items and amounts already supplied is not recomputed.
- [N-U30-039] Existing tax items are matched to needed ones by identity: the first match is updated, extra duplicates are deleted, unmatched needs are created, zero-amount items are dropped, and persistence runs updates, then deletions, then creations, batched per currency. Analytic split is inherited only for taxes flagged as analytic cost or distribution lines not used for tax closing.
- [N-U30-040] Item amounts equal the direction sign (plus one for entries and outbound documents, minus one otherwise) times computed amounts; credit notes use the refund distribution lines; ordinary entries use the typed amount as a tax-excluded unit price; document amounts are positive for invoices and credit notes alike.
- [N-U30-041] Each tax item stores the sum of the signed base amounts of the lines that share its identity, and takes the tax name unless a manual name is supplied.
- [N-U30-042] For partly deductible purchase lines the ledger derives a private-share item per line (subtotal times the non-deductible percentage), a total item on the private-share account and one private-share tax item; these are rebuilt only when the product lines changed and removed when no private share carries tax.

### STATE
- [N-U30-043] Any document that is not a draft is skipped after a change: posted or cancelled documents never have their tax items re-derived.

### OPTIONALITY
- [N-U30-044] Partial deductibility is optional; its per-line field is revealed through a permission group that posting a partly deductible bill grants to the posting user.

### DEPENDENCY
- [N-U30-045] Expense documents add the expense to the tax-item identity so items are kept per expense, price-included treatment applies to employee-paid expenses, and company-paid expense payment entries request cash-basis tags.
- [N-U30-046] An imported electronic invoice is written through the same synchronisation; its tax amounts are aligned to the totals in the file only if the computed tax is within 0.03 in document currency and every tax is recognised.

### CONSTRAINT
- [N-U30-047] Taxes cannot be used on items of off-balance accounts. Tax records are limited by company rules, and only the accounting administrator can change them.

### RISK
- [N-U30-048] The alignment of tax amounts to existing tax items is keyed by tax, currency and refund flag, not by distribution line, so a manual change to one of several distribution lines of a tax is redistributed over the tax as a whole (inferred).

### UNKNOWN
- [N-U30-049] For a partly deductible bill in a foreign currency the private-part amounts appear swapped between the company balance and the foreign amount; with company-currency documents there is no difference. Not executed.

## CAP-U30-04 Rounding allocation across lines, tax items and currencies

### WHAT
- [N-U30-050] Each company picks one of two rounding methods. Per line rounds each tax and base on every line. Globally computes tax unrounded and rounds the total per tax once for the document, then spreads the difference to the line-level amounts. The studied company uses the global method; the rounding default is global.

### WHY
- [N-U30-051] The global method exists so the tax of the whole document equals the rounded tax computed on the whole document, regardless of how many lines it has (the engine documentation example shows two equal lines whose rounded line taxes miss the whole-document tax by one unit).

### BUSINESS RULE
- [N-U30-052] Amounts are rounded separately in the document currency and in the company currency; company amounts are the document amounts divided by the line rate, and are rounded only per line in per-line mode or in the global steps otherwise.
- [N-U30-053] Three document-level steps run in order: tax amounts per tax, base amounts per line, then alignment to existing tax items. Each difference is shared in whole smallest currency units in proportion to the size of each target, largest first, with leftover units one each to the largest; equal weights apply when all targets are zero.
- [N-U30-054] The base is rounded on total included when every non-zero tax on the lines of a group is price-included, and on total excluded as soon as one non-zero tax is price-excluded.
- [N-U30-055] Manual total-excluded and per-tax amounts override rounded amounts in document currency and are converted to company currency by the line rate; existing tax items supplied by the caller become the target for each tax.
- [N-U30-056] A tax amount is split over its distribution lines by factor and rounded per line; the leftover is distributed over the lines largest first.
- [N-U30-057] The total tax per tax does not change however many accounting identities result, because rounding is allocated before aggregation into tax items.
- [N-U30-058] A line subtotal is computed on the line alone and so excludes the document-level delta, while the accounting amount of its base item includes it; the two can differ by whole smallest units, and document totals use the accounting amounts.

### STATE
- [N-U30-059] The base delta is stored on each line during the computation and added to the base item amount when items are written; it is recomputed every time tax items are recomputed.

### OPTIONALITY
- [N-U30-060] The rounding method is a company setting (default global) read at every computation; a caller may override it for a single computation.

### DEPENDENCY
- [N-U30-061] Alignment to existing tax items and tax-item preparation exist only on the server; the client-side mirror of the engine lacks them, so a client preview of a document with edited tax items can differ from the saved result.

### CONSTRAINT
- [N-U30-062] Alignment to existing tax amounts is skipped for a tax whose computed total is zero, because there is nothing to redistribute over.

### RISK
- [N-U30-063] A line shown with one subtotal can carry a base item one unit higher or lower; the engine documentation shows a line subtotal of 17.79 against an accounting balance of 17.80.

### UNKNOWN
- [N-U30-064] The system rounds by document-level allocation, not by a rule on the third decimal of the tax amount; whether that agrees with the statutory rounding statement in the statutory register (S12-08) is a separate verification and nothing is concluded here.

## CAP-U30-05 Cash-rounding, early-payment, discount-allocation and balancing items

### WHAT
- [N-U30-065] Cash rounding is an optional per-document method that rounds the total to the smallest coin. It has a coin size above zero, a rounding direction (up, down, nearest) and one of two strategies: add a separate rounding item, or add the difference to the largest tax item.
- [N-U30-066] An early-payment discount can be computed on the total including tax, on the untaxed amount, or in a mixed mode. Only the mixed mode produces items on the document: per group of account, analytic split and taxes, a discount item with the taxes and an opposite counterpart without taxes, distributed over the lines by size; fixed-amount and code taxes are excluded.
- [N-U30-067] When the company configures a separate discount account, each line with a discount gets a pair of items moving the discount amount to that account in both currencies; the studied company has neither account set, so none arise.
- [N-U30-068] A non-posted entry that has or had taxes receives an automatic balancing item whose amount makes the entry balance; it carries no taxes and sits on the journal default account, else the company suspense account. Entries without taxes before and after the change are not auto-balanced.

### WHY
- [N-U30-069] Cash rounding serves currencies whose smallest coins are no longer in circulation, so a document paid in cash can be rounded to the smallest coin that exists.

### BUSINESS RULE
- [N-U30-070] The rounded amount is the sum of the foreign amounts of all items that are not receivable or payable, excluding the rounding item itself. A zero difference removes the item; removing the method deletes it; changing the strategy replaces it. The largest-tax strategy copies the account, tags and taxes of the tax item with the largest balance and does nothing when there is no tax; the add-an-item strategy uses a loss account when the difference is positive and a loss account exists, otherwise a profit account, and carries no tax.
- [N-U30-071] The company amount of the stored rounding item is converted at the market rate on the invoice date, while the totals summary estimates it from the rate implied by the totals; for a foreign-currency document with a manually set rate the two can differ (inferred).
- [N-U30-072] For the included mode the discount is settled at payment: base items on the discount loss account (sales) or gain account (purchases) and tax correction items on the inverse distribution lines, proportional to the share paid, with any rounding residual on the largest base item and a residual in company currency posted as an exchange-difference item.
- [N-U30-073] If all taxes were removed from an entry, its tax items are deleted and the tags cleared because tax synchronisation no longer applies.

### STATE
- [N-U30-074] Rounding items are recomputed only for non-posted invoices.

### OPTIONALITY
- [N-U30-075] Cash rounding is optional and gated by a dedicated permission group; the invoicing group can edit methods, the read-only accounting group can read them, and the document field is read-only after draft. The studied database defines no cash-rounding method.
- [N-U30-076] All ten payment terms in the studied database use the included computation and one has an early discount, so no mixed-mode items arise and the tax correction is created only when payment is registered.

### DEPENDENCY
- [N-U30-077] The add-an-item strategy depends on company-dependent profit and loss accounts.

### CONSTRAINT
- [N-U30-078] The coin size must be strictly positive.

### RISK
- [N-U30-079] Selecting the add-an-item strategy without a profit account only raises a screen warning; nothing prevents saving.

### UNKNOWN
- [N-U30-080] No cash-rounding method is defined, so behaviour with a missing profit or loss account, with several tax groups and with a recomputed tax item next to the rounding item needs execution.

## CAP-U30-06 Document tax totals summary (display, edit, stored versus recomputed)

### WHAT
- [N-U30-081] The totals summary shows, in document currency and company currency, the untaxed amount, the tax per tax group under subtotals, an optional rounding amount and the total. It is computed from the rounded base items with existing tax items kept; ordinary entries have none.

### WHY
- [N-U30-082] The summary field is where a user can edit tax amounts when rounding issues appear.

### BUSINESS RULE
- [N-U30-083] Tax groups are ordered by sequence then identifier; a group sits under its preceding-subtotal label, defaulting to Untaxed Amount, and each subtotal base adds the taxes of earlier subtotals. In the studied database five groups (four withholding percentages and VAT 7 percent) share one sequence and have no label, so there is one subtotal and the withholding groups print before VAT.
- [N-U30-084] The base shown per group is blank for fixed-only groups and adjusted for price-included division groups; a flag tells the view when all groups share one base so it is not repeated. Taxes that affect later taxes are propagated into those taxes base, and non-deductible taxes are shown separately and subtracted.
- [N-U30-085] Rounding amounts are read from existing rounding items when present and otherwise estimated from the document method; rounding is subtracted from the untaxed amount and added back in the total.
- [N-U30-086] A second summary in company currency is shown for sales documents in a foreign currency when the company option is on (it is on in the studied company) and tax groups exist; the invoice print adds it as a second block.
- [N-U30-087] Editing a group tax amount on a vendor document in draft (or in quick encoding) changes the first tax item of that group by the difference, then recomputes document amounts and re-derives due dates; a summary supplied at creation or write is applied after synchronisation.

### STATE
- [N-U30-088] Stored: the amounts of each item, the document untaxed, tax, total and residual in both currencies, the line subtotal and total, and the base amount on each tax item. Recomputed on demand and never stored: the summary structure itself.

### OPTIONALITY
- [N-U30-089] The Thai invoice print inherits the generic document and replaces only the title and branch details, so its totals blocks are the generic ones; the Rounding row appears only when the summary has a rounding amount.

### DEPENDENCY
- [N-U30-090] Sales and purchase orders compute the same summary from their lines with the same engine but have no accounting items to keep.

### CONSTRAINT
- [N-U30-091] The summary is editable only in draft and only for vendor documents or in quick-encoding mode.

### RISK
- [N-U30-092] A manually typed group tax amount changes only the first tax item of that group and is later treated as a hand edit that survives edits not involving taxes.
- [N-U30-093] A tax without a group takes the first group of its country; the Thai template leaves the group empty on zero-rated and exempt VAT, and in the studied database those taxes sit in the one-percent withholding group, so their bases would be listed under that group name with a zero tax amount, next to genuine one-percent withholding on the same document (inferred).

### UNKNOWN
- [N-U30-094] Whether the presentation of amounts by tax group in both currencies satisfies a statutory tax-invoice layout is a statutory question not decided here (rounding S12-08, rate S12-04).

## CAP-U30-07 Cash-basis tax exigibility, end to end

### WHAT
- [N-U30-095] Cash-basis tax becomes reportable only when the invoice is paid. At invoice time the tax sits on a transition account and carries no report tags; at each reconciliation the system creates a separate entry in a dedicated journal moving the paid share of tax and base to the real accounts with the report tags. A smart button on the origin document lists these entries; group taxes are flattened so an on-payment tax inside a group is honoured.

### WHY
- [N-U30-096] The transition account holds the tax of an unpaid document; at reconciliation the paid share is cancelled there and put on the regular tax account. An on-payment tax is due when payment is received.

### BUSINESS RULE
- [N-U30-097] Entries are triggered after matching of receivable or payable items when any involved company has the switch on, except for cancelling reversals or when a caller disables them; they are created only if the document has both due-date items and on-payment items in one currency (several currencies are skipped silently). Each side of a partial is evaluated, so invoice and payment, or invoice and credit note, can each generate entries.
- [N-U30-098] The paid percentage is the partial amount over the document total in company currency (or in document currency for a foreign-currency document); zero partials from exchange differences are skipped. On the last partial of a fully paid document the remaining tax residual is used so entries add up exactly.
- [N-U30-099] Company amounts of the entry use the payment rate: the ratio between the payment item company amount and its foreign amount, or the conversion rate at the payment date if currencies differ; they are not rounded until the item is created.
- [N-U30-100] The entry is dated the settlement date but never before the day after the fiscal lock date of the user; for an invoice matched with a credit note each uses its own rates and the settlement date is the later of the two.
- [N-U30-101] Base items go to the company base-tracking account or the original base account with base tags and are mirrored by opposite counterparts; tax items go to the distribution-line account (else the base-tracking account, else the original) with their tags, and their counterparts go to the transition account. Items sharing currency, partner, account, on-payment taxes, distribution line and analytic split are aggregated.
- [N-U30-102] The transition-account items of the origin and of the entry are then reconciled together.
- [N-U30-103] An item cannot mix on-payment and on-invoice taxes that share base tags, nor a tax that affects another tax of different exigibility.

### STATE
- [N-U30-104] Entries are posted at once when both reconciled documents are posted and created as drafts otherwise; a draft document that is later posted keeps its matching only if its cash-basis snapshot is unchanged, otherwise the matching is dissolved and must be redone.
- [N-U30-105] Undoing a matching reverses its posted entries (and exchange entries) on their own date, or the day after the latest violated lock, and deletes draft ones; a cash-basis entry cannot be reset to draft nor deleted once posted; cancelling reversals do not create entries.

### OPTIONALITY
- [N-U30-106] Cash basis needs a company switch (delegated to the root company, cannot be turned off while a tax uses it), a dedicated journal, an optional base-tracking account, and per-tax exigibility with a reconcilable transition account.
- [N-U30-107] The Thai template sets the company switch on explicitly although no shipped tax is cash-basis; the dedicated journal exists, the base-tracking account is not set, every reconciliation evaluates cash-basis need and finds nothing, and no entry can arise until a tax is authored as on-payment. Which tax-point rule applies to Thai taxes is a statutory question (see S05-01, S05-02) not decided here.

### DEPENDENCY
- [N-U30-108] Reports carry an option to include only exigible items, set on every studied report, and a helper domain and a query column define exigibility; the helper is not called by any Community module and the report engine honouring the option is outside Community.

### CONSTRAINT
- [N-U30-109] Reconciliation fails with an error if a cash-basis entry is needed and the company has no cash-basis journal; an on-payment tax whose transition account does not allow reconciliation is refused outside chart loading.
- [N-U30-110] Matching records can be created and deleted by the invoicing group (and the accounting user and administrator) and read by the read-only accounting group, so entries are created under the rights of the user reconciling.

### RISK
- [N-U30-111] Under cash basis, only tax items get the residual correction on the last partial payment; base items are scaled by percentage only, so over several partial payments the reported base can deviate by rounding units (inferred).

### UNKNOWN
- [N-U30-112] A branch for tax-account rounding on full reconciliation is read but never set by Community code, and how tax reports consume cash-basis entries needs the report engine or execution; nothing is concluded about Thai VAT or withholding on payment, and the tax-point rule is statutory (S05-01, S05-02).

## CAP-U30-08 Tax items on credit note, switch, reset, cancel and fiscal-position change

### WHAT
- [N-U30-113] A credit note is created by copying the invoice: tax and due-date items are copied as items, product item amounts are recomputed from unit prices, the document type changes to the credit-note type, and refund distribution lines apply. Reverse-and-modify keeps only product, section and note lines so tax and due-date items are rebuilt; a credit note keeps its payment term only for the mixed discount mode.

### WHY
- [N-U30-114] A cancelling reversal reconciles the receivable, payable or liquidity items of the original with its reverse so the original is neutralised.

### BUSINESS RULE
- [N-U30-115] An unnumbered draft can be switched between invoice and credit note: type, currency and fiscal position are rewritten, which forces a full recomputation of tax items and discards manual amounts; if the total is then negative, quantities and extra tax data are negated.
- [N-U30-116] The fiscal position is recomputed from partner, delivery address, company and type, and stays editable; changing it alone does not change existing line taxes. A visible action recomputes taxes and accounts from product and position, adjusts price when the price-included mix changes, and lets the synchroniser rebuild tax items; it is hidden for posted and cancelled documents.
- [N-U30-117] A document may not keep taxes from a country different from its tax country, whatever the fiscal position does.

### STATE
- [N-U30-118] Resetting to draft changes only the state: stored items stay and are re-derived only when an input next changes.
- [N-U30-119] Cancelling a document unreconciles it first, which dissolves its cash-basis entries; only reversals created to cancel (reverse-and-modify, entries) are posted and reconciled at once, without creating cash-basis entries; a credit note of an invoice is a separate draft to be posted and matched later.

### OPTIONALITY
- [N-U30-120] A credit note keeps the original payment term only when its early discount is in mixed mode; otherwise no payment term is set.

### DEPENDENCY
- [N-U30-121] Replacement documents rely on the synchroniser to rebuild tax and due-date items; documents from expenses lose their expense links on reversal or cancellation so expenses can be reimbursed again.

### CONSTRAINT
- [N-U30-122] Switching type is refused for numbered documents and for ordinary entries.

### RISK
- [N-U30-123] Because line taxes are recomputed only when the product or unit changes, a changed fiscal position leaves old taxes in place until the update action is used, and the action is unavailable after posting.

### UNKNOWN
- [N-U30-124] Whether a credit note created by copying ends with copied or recomputed tax amounts depends on the synchroniser branch taken at creation and needs execution.

## CAP-U30-09 Multi-currency tax items, rate date and exchange interplay

### WHAT
- [N-U30-125] Each invoice-like document stores a rate from company currency to document currency; by default it is the rate on the invoice date (today if empty), it can be edited in draft by users of the multi-currency group, must be strictly positive, and is recomputed when currency, company or invoice date change.

### WHY
- [N-U30-126] Every tax computation is carried in document currency and company currency together, using a rate carried on the base line.

### BUSINESS RULE
- [N-U30-127] Tax is computed in document currency; company amounts are the document amounts divided by the line rate; rounding is done in both currencies independently; and the line synchroniser derives the company balance from the foreign amount divided by the rate unless the balance was changed or protected.
- [N-U30-128] The rate on a date is the latest rate on or before the date for the root company or global rates, else the earliest rate available, else one; conversion rounds in the target currency.
- [N-U30-129] Invoice items use the stored document rate; other entries use the market rate on their date; base lines of other entries use the ratio of foreign amount to balance.
- [N-U30-130] Due-date items round independently per currency with the last item carrying the remainder, so the sum in company currency equals the company total whatever the document rate.
- [N-U30-131] Cash-basis entries use the payment rate rather than the invoice rate and no exchange difference is posted on tax accounts for that change; exchange differences are posted by the matching on the receivable and payable items, in entries created always exigible, on the gain or loss exchange account.

### STATE
- [N-U30-132] A rate-only change in draft keeps foreign tax amounts and re-derives company amounts; a currency change recomputes everything; posting a sales document without invoice date sets the date to today while protecting a manual rate; a draft with a manual rate has a button to reset to the expected rate.

### OPTIONALITY
- [N-U30-133] The currency and its rate are shown on the document only to the multi-currency group, and the rate is read-only after draft.

### DEPENDENCY
- [N-U30-134] The rate date is the invoice date only; the supply date is an empty stub that does not enter it and no installed module overrides the date method. Which rate source and date the Thai VAT base requires is statutory (S12-03, S12-04) and is not decided here; rates must be loaded as data.

### CONSTRAINT
- [N-U30-135] A foreign-currency invoice must have a strictly positive rate.

### RISK
- [N-U30-136] With no rate rows the studied database converts at one for both active currencies, so any foreign-currency document silently takes a rate of one until rates are loaded.

### UNKNOWN
- [N-U30-137] Numeric consistency between the document rate, the rounding item rate and tax item balances for foreign-currency documents needs execution with loaded rates.
