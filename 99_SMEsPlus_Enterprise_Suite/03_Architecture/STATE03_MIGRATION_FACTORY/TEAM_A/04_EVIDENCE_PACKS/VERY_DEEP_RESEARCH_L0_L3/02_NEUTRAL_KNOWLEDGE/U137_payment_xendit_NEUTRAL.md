## KEY FINDING SUMMARY

The Xendit payment provider module contains complete native Thai payment infrastructure despite its manifest description mentioning only Indonesia and the Philippines. Thai Baht is a supported currency with zero-decimal integer processing. Three Thailand-native payment channels (a national QR scheme, a messaging-platform wallet, and a regional e-commerce wallet) are registered as default methods. Three major Thai banks support direct debit via mobile banking channel codes. All Thai e-wallet and bank payments use a redirect flow to a gateway-hosted invoice page. Card payments use an inline direct flow with browser-side 3DS handling. Accounting journal entries are generated entirely by the parent payment module with no Xendit-specific override.
