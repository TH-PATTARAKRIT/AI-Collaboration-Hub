## 1. Business capability
- On an invoice or vendor bill, adds a "Refunds" tab listing the credit notes created from it (date, number, origin, reference, total, status). (account_invoice_refund_link/views/account_invoice_view.xml:17-43)
- On a credit note, shows a read-only "Invoice reference" link back to the original document. (views/account_invoice_view.xml:44-53)
- Links each credit-note line to the original invoice line it was created from, so refunds can be traced line by line. (account_invoice_refund_link/models/account_move_line.py:12-25)
- At install, retroactively links lines of already-existing credit notes to original lines by matching on product, or otherwise on description. (account_invoice_refund_link/hooks.py:6-34)
