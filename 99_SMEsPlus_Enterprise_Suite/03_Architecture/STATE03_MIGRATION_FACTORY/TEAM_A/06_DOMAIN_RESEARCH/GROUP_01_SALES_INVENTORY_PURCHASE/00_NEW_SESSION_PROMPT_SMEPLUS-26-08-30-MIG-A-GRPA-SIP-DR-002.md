## 3.1 Audit VETO — Evidence / Governance Challenge

Consider:

- Is the source evidence materially complete for the claim being made?
- If Source Code and Database evidence conflict, can the conflict be preserved rather than silently resolved?
- Is a discovered item a Business Fact or only a technical artifact such as model/table/field/state/method/ID?
- Does a source state name have proven business meaning?
- Does a quantity name have proven business meaning, or only a suggestive field label?
- Are unsupported or absent cases marked `UNKNOWN / EVIDENCE_MISSING / CONFLICTING EVIDENCE`?
- Is any observation drifting into target design or vendor architecture copying?
- Are Accounting/Tax dependencies being mapped only as interfaces rather than reopening Accounting Core/COA design?

## 3.2 TBRAC — Thailand Business Reality Challenge

Consider where evidence exists:

- How do Thai businesses actually initiate Sales and Purchase transactions?
- Does physical warehouse activity ever occur before/after ERP documents in real operations?
- How do partial delivery, partial receipt, shortage, over/under receipt, wrong item, return and late documents occur in practice?
- Which documents are actual business evidence versus ERP-generated artifacts?
- How do Company / Branch / Warehouse / Tax Branch differ in actual Thai operations?
- Which personas need to understand the process, and where do real users rely on Excel/manual workaround?
- Which approvals are company policy versus statutory requirement?
- Which observed practices are industry-dependent or company-dependent?
- Which claims require authoritative Thai evidence or real-user validation?

Do not declare a Thailand requirement without primary/authoritative evidence appropriate to the claim.

## 3.3 EXPERT IBPV — Business Process & Design Challenge

Consider:

- Where does the evidence-backed Sales lifecycle actually begin/end?
- Where does the evidence-backed Purchase lifecycle actually begin/end?
- Which domain owns physical Inventory facts?
- Are commercial intent, physical execution and financial consequence separate facts in the source reality?
- Who owns each material state and data object?
- What event changes each state?
- What happens on partial, retry, rejection, cancellation, return, correction and reversal?
- Is cross-module ownership/handoff traceable?
- Which approval/SoD observations materially change business state and which are permission-only?
