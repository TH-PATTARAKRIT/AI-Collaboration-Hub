## 2. The three questions, answered

### Q1 — Must the technical re-grader itself be independent?

**NO — and no controlling record says it must.**

A *technical re-grade* of `E2E-04` is the application of the node-by-node traversal test to the chain
given the current specification: does node `5` now carry a supply-raised exit? That is an **evaluative
engineering act on a specification**, and it is the same class of act as building the `12`-node chain at
`10_` §1, which SMEs Core performed and no record objects to.

**What the records require independence *of* is the ASSURANCE, not the ACT.** `SC-04` §5's heading is
*"Independence — the distinction preserved"*, and what it preserves is that **`SC-03`'s output may not be
counted as an independent pass** — the same distinction `09_` §2 keeps for `EC-07` (*"approving the act
and performing the pass are different events"*).

### Q2 — Or is separation satisfied because SMT authors and an independent B-7 verifies?

**YES, and this is Boss's own construction.** `B8′`'s operative clause is a **certification** bar:

> *"**the party performing the re-grade shall not certify its own independent verification**"*

**Boss did not write *"the party performing the re-grade shall be independent."*** The bar Boss imposed
falls on **certification**, and Structure A satisfies it by construction: SMT performs, **B-7 certifies**,
and B-7's independence is separately constituted (`B7′`: different vendor/model, new clean session, no
transcript or session inheritance, no authoring role, read-only baseline).

### Q3 — Do `SC-03` / `SC-04` prohibit SMT from performing the technical re-grade, or only from self-certifying?

**Only from self-certifying — and, more precisely, from having its output *counted as* assurance.**

**Neither record contains a prohibition on SMT performing technical work.** Both are scope statements
about what SMT's output **is**. And SMT's demonstrated function is precisely technical challenge:
`SC-03` §1 records **`11` challenges raised, `6` of which changed an SMEs Core conclusion, three of them
against work published one file earlier in the same round.** A body barred from technical work could not
have produced that. `SC-04` §5 says so in terms: *"Its productivity was real … **and high productivity is
not independence**."*

> **The records separate PRODUCTIVITY from INDEPENDENCE. `B8′` assigns SMT the productive act and
> reserves independence to B-7. The two instruments are consistent, and `B7-F-11`'s structural
> conclusion is DISPROVED on Boss's own wording.**
