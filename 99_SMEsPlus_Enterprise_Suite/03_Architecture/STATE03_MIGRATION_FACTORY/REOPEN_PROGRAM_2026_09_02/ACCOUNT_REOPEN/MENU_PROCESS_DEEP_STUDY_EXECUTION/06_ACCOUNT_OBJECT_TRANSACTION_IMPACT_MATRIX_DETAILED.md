## 0. Reading guide

- **Account Type vocabulary** = the 19 business-facing types of Boss ruling AJ: Receivable, Bank and Cash, Current Assets, Non-current Assets, Prepayments, Fixed Assets, Payable, Credit Card, Current Liabilities, Non-current Liabilities, Equity, Current Year Earnings, Income, Other Income, Expenses, Other Expenses, Depreciation, Cost of Revenue, Off-Balance Sheet. "Dr / Cr" statements are business meaning, not a posting schema.
- **Thai template accounts** are quoted as `code Thai name` from `ACCOUNT/01 ACCOUNT/SOURCE CODE/02 OTHER/l10n_th/data/template/account.account-th.csv` (144 rows, 15 types instantiated — Boss AJ) and the tax / tax-group / asset CSVs in the same folder. They are benchmark template facts; the SMEsPlus canonical chart does not exist yet (COA-G01..G03 HOLD). Where the template has **no** account for a needed concept (prepayments, contract liability, bad-debt expense, gain/loss on disposal, stock clearing, variance, count difference, inter-company) the cell says so — these are COA-G02/G03 inputs, not decisions.
- **Scenario IDs** `SC-nn` refer to file 08 §2; other variants are named inline.
- **Every Thai statutory statement** (VAT tax point, PP30/PND forms, CIT deductibility, WHT rates, filing deadlines, legal reserve, retention) is labelled `LEGAL_TAX_REVIEW_REQUIRED`; no authoritative Revenue Department / DBD source was available (prior G-B1: VAT/CIT research = zero).
- **Benchmark facts vs SMEsPlus candidate:** the Dr/Cr, timing, reversal and reconciliation columns describe the *business effect a Thai SME needs* as the candidate reading; where the benchmark behaves differently or is unevidenced this is stated inside the cell with its Team A / WHT / Inventory citation. Nothing here says SMEsPlus must follow the benchmark.
- Objects flagged `Joint` (OBJ-01, 03, 04, 46-52) carry Inventory-owned facts; the Account side records what it must receive and never decides valuation.
- Columns 14-18 (evidence, owner, verifier, gate, status) are generated from the same `object_register.json` as file 03 and are identical to it by construction.
