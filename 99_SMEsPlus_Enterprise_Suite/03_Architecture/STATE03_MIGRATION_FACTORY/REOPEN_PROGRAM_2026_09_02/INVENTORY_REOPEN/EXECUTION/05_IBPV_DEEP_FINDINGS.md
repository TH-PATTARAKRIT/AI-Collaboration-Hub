## 5. Detailed Findings — Council Challenge (CP-03)

### 5.1 Scope and posture

The Council pass re-read DR-002's physical-stock-lifecycle sections directly (§A3, A4, A6, A7, A8, A13, A14) together with CORR-005's residual register, IDR-007's own recomputation, and CORR-007B's `N-A7-01` proof (file 02), internal audit (file 04), and clean-room compliance review (file 12) — a fresh, targeted re-read formed independently of any other Council mandate's output, per the Charter's anti-groupthink rule. Its headline finding is that the evidence base for this mandate is, across nine corrective rounds, unusually deep and honest about its own residual gaps: no fabrication was found anywhere in this pass, and no source code was found reused.

### 5.2 The core ownership-boundary question — a qualified yes, for the reference pattern

The reference system keeps Sales, Purchase, and Manufacturing out of Inventory's physical-fact ownership with genuine, method-body-level rigor: each domain writes only its own commercial or BOM-level intent (`sale.order.line.product_uom_qty`, `purchase.order.line.product_qty`, `mrp.production`'s BOM/routing structure) and reads Inventory's done-move quantities one-directionally. `stock.move` and `stock.quant` are consistently the single choke point for physical mutation, and no second, competing "stock truth" table was found operating alongside them anywhere in the modules read (DR-002 §A8, §A13 §4/§9). Manufacturing is a clean example on its own: raw-material consumption and finished-goods receipt are both recorded as ordinary `stock.move` rows tagged back to the MO, not a parallel manufacturing-only ledger.

The qualification the Council pass insists on is structural, not evidentiary: everything above describes the *reference* system. No SMEsPlus target schema for the physical-stock lifecycle has been authorized or drafted — Team B remains unauthorized throughout this entire evidence chain — so "semantically complete without overlapping ownership" is not yet a testable property of the actual product. It is, so far, a well-evidenced property of the pattern the eventual design will learn from, and this track's verdict must reflect that distinction rather than collapse it.

### 5.3 Full lifecycle checklist — reconfirmed, with two elevated gaps

Demand, reservation, receipt, delivery, internal transfer, backorder, returns, and scrap are all reconfirmed `CLOSED_WITH_EVIDENCE` against DR-002's own citations with no contradictions found this pass (see §8 for the full item-by-item register). Two gaps survive this pass's own re-reading and are elevated beyond their prior treatment:

**Partial fulfillment** has no single cross-domain representation — Sale expresses it as a header-level `delivery_status` enum, Purchase as an uncapped `qty_received` running total, and Manufacturing as an explicit MO-split into a genuinely separate backorder record. Three domain-specific idioms are bolted onto one shared `stock.move`/backorder primitive, with no evidence SMEsPlus's design has yet decided whether to unify or deliberately preserve that heterogeneity.

**Over-fulfillment and over-receipt are unguarded anywhere in the reference system** — confirmed independently through two separate evidence paths (this lineage's own direct read of `purchase.order.line._compute_qty_received()`, and GROUP A's DB-forensics-corroborated finding), and named among the evidence register's own "four strongest, most consequential invariant-gap candidates." Despite that severity, this gap has never received the kind of named, options-based design-policy brief that the closely analogous count-freeze question (`N-A7-01`) received. The Council pass regards this as an inconsistency in how design-policy gaps have been triaged for Boss's attention, and recommends an equivalent options document be produced before Inventory Design Freeze.

### 5.4 `N-A7-01` — primary review

The Council pass independently re-read CORR-007B's file 02 in full. The conclusion — Odoo's reference behavior is soft conflict-detection only (`is_outdated` + `action_apply_inventory()` + the `stock.inventory.conflict` wizard), with no hard freeze field, method, or constraint found anywhere in `stock_quant.py` or its two wizards — is well-supported by genuine full-method-body reads, a stronger standard than the grep-only checks performed by CORR-006 and IDR-007's own spot-check. The Council pass concurs with the "RESOLVED AS SOURCE BEHAVIOR; DESIGN POLICY REQUIRED" disposition and found no contrary evidence.

### 6.4 `N-A7-01` — primary review, extended one file further than CORR-007B itself

CORR-007B's own proof (`stock_quant.py`'s `inventory_diff_quantity`/`is_outdated` fields, `action_apply_inventory`'s routing to the `stock.inventory.conflict` wizard) was independently re-verified line-for-line against the reference source and found accurate. This pass went one step further than CORR-007B's own scope by also grepping `stock_move.py` directly for any field, decorator, or constraint referencing count-in-progress state — zero hits — closing the one residual gap in the prior trace. The finding is confirmed for a fourth independent time: the reference system implements soft conflict-detection only, with no hard freeze anywhere. The four design-policy options for Team B are analyzed here along two axes this pass identifies as composable rather than mutually exclusive: **when** a freeze applies (none / per-quant / per-location) and **who** resolves a conflict (self-service / manager-approval). A location-level lock paired with a manager-approval resolution step, for instance, would address both the operational-continuity concern with a full hard freeze and the internal-controls/segregation-of-duties gap in the current self-service wizard — offered as structuring input for Team B's eventual decision, not as a selection, which remains explicitly out of this session's authority.

### 6.5 `N-A7-04` — newly resolved this session

Full account in §4.2. In summary: this Low-Medium item carried `EVIDENCE_MISSING` through DR-002, CORR-005, and IDR-007 because none of those passes searched outside the stock module. This pass located the mechanism by searching the Accounting module and its Inventory bridge — `res.company`'s lock-date framework, consumed by Sales/Purchase/Tax only inside Accounting's own journal-entry logic, and reaching Inventory only through `stock_account`'s `stock_picking.py` constraint pair, at the picking level, with a documented admin bypass. This precisely and mechanistically confirms CORR-007B's one-line `G-3` finding under `N-A12-01` ("backdate enforcement at picking- not move-level") and surfaces a new architecture question this track flags for Team B: since Boss's governance ruling now defines Inventory as an independent Stock Truth Backbone rather than a component merged into Accounting, should Inventory own a native temporal-integrity guard of its own, rather than depending entirely on the Accounting bridge module being installed and active?

### 6.6 Stockable/Consumable/Service routing (`INV-FP-13`) — first substantive research in this evidence chain

No prior deliverable on this question exists anywhere in the evidence chain; this pass performed the first substantive research. Boss's hypothesis of a flat three-way split is refined rather than confirmed as literally stated: the reference system's actual mechanism is two-axis — `product.type` (only three values: `consu`/Goods, `service`/Service, `combo`/Combo — there is no separate "Stockable" or "Consumable" type value) crossed with a boolean `is_storable` that is compute-forced to `False` for anything not `type='consu'`. The two precise gating points were traced directly: `sale_order_line.py`'s `_action_launch_stock_rule` skips any line whose product type is not `consu`, so Service and Combo lines never generate a `stock.move` at all; separately, `stock_move_line.py`'s `_synchronize_quant` returns immediately when `is_storable` is `False`, meaning a "Goods, not tracked" product still generates ordinary delivery/receipt documents and picking workflow, but never receives a `stock.quant` row or persistent on-hand balance. This maps well onto real low-friction Thai SME scenarios (for example, small spare parts billed on a repair invoice without formal cycle-counting) and is not merely an academic distinction.

A genuinely new and reassuring discovery is `product.py`'s `_reset_inventory()` method: when a product transitions from Consumable to Stockable, the system does not simply zero the balance — it replays the product's entire done `stock.move.line` history (which was always being recorded, since move/picking creation is gated only by `type`, not by `is_storable`) to backfill accurate `stock.quant` rows at the moment of promotion. This proves the ledger/snapshot split is a deliberate, coherent design choice, not an oversight, and is offered as a pattern worth Team B's consideration if SMEsPlus adopts an analogous distinction. Combo is a fourth bucket entirely outside Boss's original hypothesis; this pass identified its exclusion from direct stock-move creation but did not fully trace its component-explosion mechanism, and registers that as an open item rather than a closed one.
