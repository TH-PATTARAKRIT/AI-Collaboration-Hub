## 4. Is the registry's license-parsing itself reliable? (control check)

Sampled other `02_OTHER` rows that are unambiguously Community-only, LGPL-3 modules
(`base`, `hr_skills`, `mail`, `project`, `web`) — **all five are correctly recorded `LGPL-3`**
in the registry. Registry-wide split for `02_OTHER`: 700 rows `OEEL-1`, 666 rows `LGPL-3` (not
a blanket "everything in 02_OTHER is OEEL-1" defect — the registry does distinguish license
per-row correctly in the general case).

**This rules out a systemic registry-parsing bug.** The anomaly is specific to these two
(and possibly other, unaudited) individual rows, not a broad extraction defect.

## 5. Root cause — determination

Two remaining candidate explanations, and why neither can be fully closed from this session:

- **(a) Registry/extraction defect on these specific rows** — the AST parser mis-read or
  mis-mapped the `license` field for these two modules during the 2026-08-28 scan, while
  correctly parsing it for the surrounding rows. Possible but has no independent corroborating
  evidence located.
- **(b) The customer's actual deployed copy of these two modules carries a locally-modified
  manifest** (`license: 'OEEL-1'` written into the physical file in the customer's Enterprise-
  merged tree, e.g. by an Odoo Enterprise packaging/build step that re-stamps selected
  Community modules when bundled into the Enterprise distribution, or by a manual/local edit
  in the customer's deployment). This is consistent with `A1_SOURCE_LANDSCAPE.md`'s own
  description of `02 OTHER` as a **combined community+enterprise tree**, not Community-only —
  and is the single most direct explanation for "identical content, different license byte."

**I cannot fully close (a) vs (b) from this session.** The customer's actual source files
(`SOURCE CODE/02 OTHER.zip` and its extracted tree) are marked restricted — "never commit,"
stored off-repo on `/Volumes/iMacSys/...`, and are not reachable by this session under the
clean-room/anchor-only evidence rule. Determining which byte is actually on disk in the
customer's tree requires reading that restricted archive directly, which is outside this
session's access and outside the clean-room discipline this pipeline itself enforces (MD-07/08).

**Working determination (evidence-grounded, not final legal disposition):** most likely (b) —
the registry is probably reporting the license value that is actually present in the customer's
Enterprise-partition copy of these two modules, which differs from the public Community upstream
value. This is **not proof** the registry mis-extracted; it is a genuine **source-tree license
divergence** between what the customer's deployment ships and what upstream `odoo/odoo`
publishes for the same module content.
