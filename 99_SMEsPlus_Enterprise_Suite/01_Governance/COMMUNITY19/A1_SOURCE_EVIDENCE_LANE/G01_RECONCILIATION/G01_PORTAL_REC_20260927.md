## 1. Classification rules (predeclared)

- **MATCH**: A1 and A2 agree (or an A2 omission is a purely structural static fact), and the source supports it. Runtime corroboration is optional, not required.
- **CONTRADICTION**: A2 marked the A1 claim PARTIAL because part of A1's statement is inaccurate or its scope is materially wrong. Also used when an A2 omission directly contradicts part of an A1 statement.
- **UNKNOWN_PENDING_PROOF**: A1 and A2 agree at source level (or A2 predicts a behaviour statically), but the claim or its risk is inherently runtime- or cross-module-dependent. This covers A2 label MISSING_REQUIRED_RUNTIME_PROOF and any item that has an A2 PR-P case.
- **GAP**: evidence is missing, and nothing in this scope (static or runtime case) is defined to settle it.
- **Lane B column**: UNCORROBORATED (behavioural; no runtime observation exists) or NOT_APPLICABLE (declarative or structural, or a source-only gap). It is never FAIL.
- **Scope of REC items**:
  - A1 claims C01–C17.
  - A2's explicitly strengthened C09 reading (S1/S2) as its own item.
  - A1 gap G8, which A2 settled statically.
  - A2 omissions O1–O9.
