### 3.1 Observations recorded during execution (for A3; not predeclared, so they carry no PASS or FAIL)

- **R1: PR-U3 precondition not supported by source.**
  - `utm.source` has **no active or archive field**. A1 §3 already said "Source: no archive state".
  - A2 PR-U3 ("Archive a source…") therefore cannot be executed as written. The same limit applies to A2 O2's phrase "including an archived one" for sources; it applies to mediums and campaigns.
  - The predeclared PC-UTM-17 is kept as recorded. §4 adds a **ready-to-run amended variant (PC-UTM-17a, medium instead of source)**, marked as a post-predeclaration amendment.
- **R2: the two identity rules also diverge on archived records.**
  - The generator's candidate query runs in the ordinary environment, so archived records are filtered out by default. Free-text find-or-create explicitly includes them.
  - Statically, a manual create or rename of a medium or campaign to a name that exactly equals an **archived** record's name is not suffixed. It would then hit the plain uniqueness constraint (a database-level rejection instead of a suffix).
  - This is runtime-unproven. Suggested runtime check: archive medium "X1", then create medium "X1" manually.
  - It strengthens A2 S1 and is relevant to G01-UTM-Q002 and Q018 (lineage only).
- **R3: accent handling.** Where the database's unaccent option is enabled, the case-insensitive like-operators also fold accents. Free-text resolution may then additionally merge accent variants that the case-sensitive and accent-sensitive uniqueness keeps apart. This is a third axis of the S1 divergence and needs runtime confirmation.
- **R4 (C15 detail, consistent with A2).** The content-mixin multi-record rename refusal raises a plain value error (programming-level), as A2 noted.
