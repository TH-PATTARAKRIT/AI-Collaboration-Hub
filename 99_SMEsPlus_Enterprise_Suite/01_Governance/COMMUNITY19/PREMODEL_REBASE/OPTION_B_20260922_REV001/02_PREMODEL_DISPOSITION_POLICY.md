# Pre-Model Research Disposition Policy

## Permitted

- preserve immutable evidence and provenance;
- derive source-neutral GMVQ authoring inputs, gaps and control questions;
- use findings to challenge whether the frozen bank needs a future controlled revision;
- cite the external seal and original lineage in governance records.

## Prohibited

- represent S1/S2 as answers to frozen QIDs;
- backfill QIDs into old answers and imply they were originally answered;
- count candidate functions, rules or scenarios as Canonical Function-IDs or Formal Coverage;
- dispatch A2 against the old S1/S2 answer shape;
- release any restricted/source-specific expression to ROOM B;
- modify a frozen bank in place.
