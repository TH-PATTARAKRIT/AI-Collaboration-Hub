## 2. Interpretation

The model is not a document-production framework.

It establishes that:

- documentation alone is not proof of understanding;
- file delivery alone is not proof of knowledge transfer;
- verified understanding must be evidence-backed;
- material Unknowns must be resolved or explicitly controlled;
- Teach-Back may be required where misunderstanding could materially affect downstream work;
- independent teams receive verified knowledge, not a predetermined Answer Key;
- evidence is captured during work and preserved canonically afterward;
- Special Team findings must be absorbed by the owning team before applicable Gate closure where material.
