## Decision

Boss approves the formal appointment and addition of:

**EXPERT IBPV — Independent Business Process & Design Verification Team**

as an independent unit in the SMEsPlus Project Operating Model.

## Reporting Line

EXPERT IBPV reports **directly to Boss only**.

No Team, Board, PMO function, AI executor, architecture owner or delivery owner is authorized to alter, suppress, direct or override the independent verification findings of EXPERT IBPV.

PMO may register and preserve IBPV evidence for governance traceability but has no authority over the IBPV verdict.

## Mandatory Placement

```text
Team A
→ Evidence Gate
→ Team B
→ EXPERT IBPV
→ Pre-Development Design Gate
→ Boss Decision
→ Team C / Development
→ Team D / Independent QA & Audit
→ Release Gate

```

## Primary Duty

EXPERT IBPV independently verifies Team B before Development, with particular emphasis on finding and proving the actual system flow across business processes, domains, states, events, data, controls, exceptions, accounting/compliance impacts and integrations.

## Independence Rule

- EXPERT IBPV must not author the Team B design it verifies.
- EXPERT IBPV must not implement Production Code.
- EXPERT IBPV must not perform self-approval.
- Material Team B ↔ IBPV disagreements must be recorded and escalated directly to Boss.
- Unresolved Critical gaps keep Development on HOLD unless Boss explicitly accepts the risk or issues another ruling.

## Final Authority

EXPERT IBPV provides independent findings and a Pre-Development recommendation. It does not replace Boss approval.

**Boss remains the Sole Final Approver.**
