# BOSS DECISION — EXPERT IESA APPOINTMENT

Decision ID: SMEPLUS-BDR-IESA-2026-08-30-001
Project: SMEsPlus Enterprise Suite
Date: 2026-08-30
Authority: Boss
Status: APPROVED / EFFECTIVE

## Decision

Boss approves the formal appointment and addition of:

**EXPERT IESA — Independent ERP & SaaS Intelligence Assurance Team**

as an independent project assurance unit.

## Boss Intent

The project must not release a system to customers merely because development and ordinary testing appear complete. The project must independently prove that the completed system is operationally coherent, ERP-integrated, SaaS-ready, resilient and production-ready before customer use.

Boss authorizes additional process controls or lifecycle controls where necessary to achieve a complete and defensible outcome.

## Reporting Line

EXPERT IESA reports **directly to Boss only**.

No Team, Board, PMO function, AI executor, architecture owner, engineering owner or delivery owner may alter, suppress, direct or override IESA's independent findings.

PMO may register and preserve IESA evidence for governance traceability but has no authority over the IESA verdict.

## Mandatory Placement

```text
Team A
→ Team B
→ Figma / UX
→ EXPERT IBPV
→ Pre-Development Design Gate
→ Boss Decision
→ Team C / Development
→ Team D / Independent QA & Audit
→ EXPERT IESA
→ Pre-Production Enterprise & SaaS Assurance Gate
→ Boss Release / Production Decision
→ Production / Customer Use
```

## Primary Duty

EXPERT IESA independently evaluates the completed solution after controlled Figma/UX, Team C and Team D evidence exists, focusing on system-level ERP integrity, cross-domain correctness, SaaS architecture, tenant isolation, resilience, performance, scalability, operational readiness and production fitness.

## Independence Rule

- IESA must not write or repair the Production Code it assesses.
- IESA must not rewrite Team D evidence.
- IESA must not self-approve its own remediation.
- Material delivery-team ↔ IESA disagreements must be recorded and escalated directly to Boss.
- Unresolved Critical IESA gaps keep Release/Production on HOLD unless Boss explicitly accepts the risk or issues another written ruling.

## Lifecycle Ruling

A new numbered STATE is **not required at this time**. The approved control is established as a mandatory **Pre-Production Enterprise & SaaS Assurance Gate** before Production/customer release.

Reason: IESA is an independent assurance/control layer spanning system, QA, infrastructure and operational evidence. Treating it as a mandatory Gate preserves existing STATE numbering and historical traceability while still preventing Production progression.

If later evidence shows the assurance workload requires a standalone lifecycle STATE, PMO may propose a controlled STATE-baseline Change Request for Boss approval. No STATE renumbering is authorized by this decision.

## Final Authority

IESA provides independent findings and a Release/Production-readiness recommendation. It does not replace Boss approval.

**Boss remains the Sole Final Approver.**

**No Evidence = No Progress**

**Never Skip Gate**
