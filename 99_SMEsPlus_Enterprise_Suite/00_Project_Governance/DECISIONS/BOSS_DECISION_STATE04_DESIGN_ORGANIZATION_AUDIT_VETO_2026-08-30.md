## Decision

Boss approves the STATE04 operating model consisting of:

1. Ten permanent design responsibilities / design teams.
2. Team11 — Independent Design Assurance & Audit Veto Office.
3. Ten independent Audit Veto Expert cells, one paired to each design team.
4. One Cross-Domain Audit Veto Lead reporting directly to Boss.
5. Mandatory independent challenge before any STATE04 design may reach Boss Gate.

Approved principle:

> Every Design Team has its own Independent Challenger. No Design reaches Boss Gate without Audit Veto disposition.

02 SaaS & Platform Solution Design

## Team11

Team11 is independent from the ten design teams, Boards, PMO delivery ownership and implementation teams. Team11 may challenge, request evidence, issue findings, return work for rework, place a HOLD, or issue VETO. Team11 cannot final-approve; Boss remains the sole Final Approver.

- 11.1 Functional & Domain Design Audit Expert
- 11.2 SaaS & Platform Audit Expert
- 11.3 Data & Database Audit Expert
- 11.4 Integration / API / Event Audit Expert
- 11.5 Security / IAM / Approval / Audit Expert
- 11.6 Reporting & Analytics Audit Expert

- 11.8 Migration & Canonical Mapping Audit Expert
- 11.9 NFR / Reliability / Operability Audit Expert
- 11.10 AI Capability & Automation Audit Expert
- Cross-Domain Audit Veto Lead

## Mandatory Checkpoints

Each design team is subject to D0 Intake Review, D1 Design Direction Review, D2 Detailed Design Challenge, and D3 Final Design Assurance.

Allowed Audit Veto dispositions: PASS, PASS WITH CONTROL, RETURN FOR REWORK, HOLD, VETO.

A VETO blocks design-baseline freeze and handoff to STATE05/STATE06 unless Boss issues an explicit written override / risk-acceptance record.
