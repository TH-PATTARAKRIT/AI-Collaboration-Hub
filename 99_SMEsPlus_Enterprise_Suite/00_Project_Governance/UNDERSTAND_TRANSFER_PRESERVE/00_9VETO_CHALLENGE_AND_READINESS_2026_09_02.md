## 1. Boss Intent

Boss approves the lifecycle operating principle:

`Understand deeply -> Transfer accurately -> Document & Preserve verified understanding.`

The purpose is to prevent shallow learning, document-only handoff, loss of context between Teams/Sessions, and repeated relearning downstream.

## 2. Delta From Existing Governance

This is a material governance delta because existing controls strongly govern evidence, Gate separation, independent verification and traceability, but do not yet state one concise cross-lifecycle rule that makes verified understanding and successful knowledge transfer explicit prerequisites for a controlled handoff.

This does not replace existing Gates. It strengthens the quality of what is handed across them.

## 5. Anti-Endless-Research Control

The standard must not be interpreted as requiring absolute knowledge before progression.

Target condition:

`MATERIAL UNDERSTANDING COMPLETE = Material Unknowns resolved OR explicitly controlled with owner, evidence status and Gate impact.`

Unknown does not equal failure when evidence genuinely does not exist. Hidden or silently assumed Unknown is a control failure.

## 6. Handoff Independence Control

At independent lifecycle boundaries, especially `Team A -> Team B`, successful transfer means the receiving team correctly understands:

- verified facts;
- observed business semantics;
- evidence provenance;
- known boundaries;
- unknowns/conflicts;
- what must not be assumed.

It does **not** mean Team A tells Team B what target architecture it must design.

`Transfer knowledge, not predetermined conclusions.`

## 8. Core Control Statements

`Understand first.`  
`Transfer understanding second.`  
`Document & Preserve verified understanding third.`  
`Documentation != Proof of Understanding.`  
`Transfer Knowledge != Transfer an Answer Key.`  
`Material Unknowns must be resolved or explicitly controlled.`  
`Special Team may solve the hard problem; the owning team must absorb the learning before Gate closure where applicable.`  
`No Evidence = No Progress.`  
`Never Skip Gate.`
