## 3. Required Corrections

### 3.1 Terminology Correction

The filename and wording `MISSING_DOCUMENT_CREATION_REGISTER` are not accepted as the controlling terminology.

A document may be described as `MISSING` or `LOST` only when prior existence is evidenced. Where no prior evidence exists, the correct status is `NOT CREATED` or `NO EVIDENCE OF PRIOR CREATION`.

For this cycle, the 14 declared functions were found. The corrected register is:

`STATE02_DOCUMENT_EXISTENCE_AND_CREATION_STATUS_REGISTER_v0.2.md`

### 3.2 Verification Status Correction

`FOUND` and `content opened` prove physical existence and inspection. They do not by themselves prove:

- current authority
- effective approval
- named independent review
- named verification
- canonical status
- Gate PASS

Accordingly, documents without named Reviewer/Verifier or independent approval evidence must remain `PARTIALLY VERIFIED` or `PENDING VERIFICATION`.
