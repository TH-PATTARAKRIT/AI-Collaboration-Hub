# SMEsPlus VERY DEEP RESEARCH 8-CRITERIA UNIVERSAL EXIT CONSTITUTION

Constitution ID: `SMEPLUS-DR-EXIT-8C-001`
Status: `BOSS APPROVED / PROJECT-WIDE MANDATORY`
Effective Date: `2026-09-04`
Scope: `ALL MODULES / ALL STATES / ALL FUTURE VERY DEEP RESEARCH`
Research Depth Baseline: `VERY DEEP / L99999.99999`
Authority: `Boss = Sole Final Approver`

## 1. Constitutional Intent

SMEsPlus shall prioritize completeness, correctness, evidence quality, and operational understanding over speed or arbitrary deadlines for Very Deep Research.

Time shall not be used as a reason to weaken research depth, skip required evidence, reduce challenge coverage, bypass Gate controls, or advance an incomplete Module/State.

The governing objective is:

`Understand deeply -> Transfer accurately -> Preserve verifiably -> Prove convergence -> Advance only with evidence.`

Core rules remain mandatory:

- `No Evidence = No Progress.`
- `Never Skip Gate.`
- `Boss is the sole Final Approver.`
- `PARTIAL != PASS.`
- `No repeated question without a material delta.`
- `No time pressure may override evidence or Gate requirements.`

## 2. Universal Application

This Constitution applies to:

1. Every SMEsPlus Module undergoing Very Deep Research.
2. Every Domain/Wave inside a Module.
3. Every STATE before advancement to the next STATE.
4. Cross-Module and Whole-System State Integration Review.
5. All future research programmes designated by Boss as Very Deep Research.

Domain-specific details may be added, but none of the eight constitutional Exit Criteria may be weakened, removed, bypassed, or treated as optional without explicit Boss approval.

## 3. Mandatory Eight Exit Criteria

A Module or State may advance only when ALL eight criteria are satisfied with evidence.

### EC-01 — Scope Bounded

The material research universe is explicitly defined and bounded.

Where applicable, verified populations/denominators must cover menus, views, fields, actions, states, models, source paths, database structures, security paths, events, reports, integrations, tenant/company boundaries, migration paths, failure paths, unknowns, contradictions, and negative claims.

If a material denominator cannot be proven, it must be declared `UNBOUNDED / NOT YET ENUMERABLE` and the Gate remains blocked unless evidence proves the remainder non-gating.

### EC-02 — Enumeration Converged

The research method must demonstrate convergence.

Research is not complete merely because multiple rounds were performed or because no reviewer issued a veto.

Convergence requires systematic enumeration, repeatable evidence paths, material-delta stability, and no unresolved enumeration defect capable of changing architecture, semantics, controls, SaaS boundaries, migration requirements, or Gate outcome.

### EC-03 — Unknown Exhausted

Every material unknown must have a final disposition:

- `CLOSED`
- `NON-GATING`
- `ROUTED TO LATER WAVE/STATE`
- `OUT OF SCOPE WITH EVIDENCE`
- `BOSS DECISION REQUIRED`

No unclassified `GATING UNKNOWN` may remain when advancing.

A later-wave/state route may not be used to hide a blocker belonging to the current scope.

### EC-04 — Tolerance-Zero Closed

All applicable tolerance-zero boundaries must be evidence-closed before advancement.

Examples include, where applicable:

- tenant isolation
- company isolation
- financial integrity
- unauthorized/duplicate posting
- immutable posted facts
- critical security/privilege boundaries
- irrecoverable controlled-data loss
- critical inventory/data integrity
- exact statutory/security invariants defined by project policy

`CONDITIONAL PASS` may not bypass a tolerance-zero risk.

### EC-05 — Contradiction Resolution Complete

Every material contradiction between primary research, source/database/UI evidence, independent reviewers, AAS+, PMO, or prior canonical evidence must be dispositioned with traceable evidence and lineage.

No material contradiction may remain merely as an unresolved difference of opinion.

### EC-06 — Negative Claim Controlled

All material negative claims must comply with the SMEsPlus Negative Claim Control.

`NO EVIDENCE FOUND != FUNCTION DOES NOT EXIST.`

Every material statement such as `never`, `always`, `cannot`, `does not exist`, `not supported`, `no validation`, or similar system-wide negative must declare and prove the search boundary proportional to the claim.

Allowed classifications include:

- `VERIFIED ABSENCE`
- `NOT FOUND IN SEARCHED SCOPE`
- `NOT YET SEARCHED`
- `UNKNOWN`
- `CONTRADICTED`

### EC-07 — Two Consecutive Clean Independent Passes

Before Final Research Gate, at least two consecutive fresh independent passes must complete without any of the following:

- new material population
- new material finding class
- new gating unknown
- reopened tolerance-zero issue
- new Gate-changing contradiction
- evidence-integrity failure

Reviewer findings must themselves be independently verified before acceptance.

`Independent Review != Truth.`
`Verified Evidence = Truth Basis.`

### EC-08 — Final Knowledge Package Complete

The Final Knowledge Package must be complete, traceable, and reproducible.

At minimum, where applicable, it shall include:

- Domain/State Semantic Model
- Function Coverage Register
- Source-of-Truth Register
- Event/State Model
- Cross-Module / Cross-State Dependency Map
- Control Matrix
- Failure / Edge Case Register
- Unknown Register
- Contradiction Register
- Negative Claim Register
- Evidence Manifest / SHA-256
- Boss Decision Register
- Clean-room Semantic Transfer / AAS+ Design Input
- Final Gate Report
- Repository / Branch / File Path / Commit SHA
- Jira lineage where required by project governance

Missing mandatory evidence means the package is not complete.

## 4. Module Exit Rule

No Module may advance to its next controlled phase or State unless:

`EC-01 PASS`
`EC-02 PASS`
`EC-03 PASS`
`EC-04 PASS`
`EC-05 PASS`
`EC-06 PASS`
`EC-07 PASS`
`EC-08 PASS`

Then and only then may the Module be presented to Boss for Final Exit Decision.

AI/PMO may issue only a recommendation. Boss alone decides Final Exit.

## 5. State Exit Rule

Passing all Module gates inside a State is necessary but not sufficient for State advancement.

After all in-scope Modules satisfy their Module 8-Criteria Exit Gates, the State must undergo a State Integration Very Deep Review using the same eight criteria at State level.

Mandatory sequence:

`Module Very Deep Research`
-> `Module 8-Criteria Exit Gate`
-> `All in-scope Modules evidence-ready`
-> `State Integration Very Deep Review`
-> `State 8-Criteria Exit Gate`
-> `Boss Final State Decision`
-> `Next State`

No State may advance on aggregate percentage, elapsed time, schedule pressure, or partial Module completion.

## 6. Research Depth Rule

`L1-L12` remains the minimum structured Deep Research framework.

For Boss-designated Very Deep Research, the operating research depth is:

`VERY DEEP / L99999.99999`

This is not a numeric claim that knowledge is literally infinite. It is the project control label meaning research must continue beyond L12 whenever evidence, unknowns, contradictions, failure classes, dependencies, or convergence defects require deeper investigation.

Research depth is driven by evidence and remaining uncertainty, not by timebox.

## 7. Time and Schedule Governance

For Very Deep Research, schedule is a planning aid, not an Exit Criterion.

The following are prohibited:

- declaring PASS because a deadline arrived
- reducing research depth to meet a date
- treating elapsed time as evidence of completeness
- skipping independent review because of schedule
- accepting unresolved gating unknowns to preserve timeline
- bypassing tolerance-zero issues through schedule pressure

PMO may report time consumed, forecast, workload, and resource use, but may not convert schedule pressure into Gate approval.

Work quality and evidence completeness govern advancement.

## 8. Reopen Rule

A previously passed Module or State may be selectively reopened when later evidence introduces a material contradiction, new material population, new tolerance-zero risk, or evidence-integrity failure.

Reopen is not a reset.

Existing approved evidence remains Audit Lineage. Revalidation must be DELTA-FIRST and limited to materially affected scope unless evidence proves broader impact.

## 9. AAS+ / Design Handoff Rule

AAS+ may explore designs in parallel only as `PROVISIONAL / NON-CANONICAL` while parent Very Deep Research is not yet complete.

Canonical redesign must use the final converged evidence baseline and must perform full delta revalidation against all corrections, contradictions, unknown dispositions, and Boss decisions.

No provisional AAS+ output may be treated as implementation authority.

## 10. Final Gate Outcomes

Allowed Research Exit recommendations are:

- `RECOMMEND PASS`
- `RECOMMEND CONDITIONAL PASS` only for explicitly non-tolerance-zero residuals permitted by policy
- `RECOMMEND HOLD`
- `RECOMMEND FAIL`

Boss alone may issue the Final Decision.

## 11. Project-Wide Constitutional Rule

Effective immediately:

> NO MODULE MAY ADVANCE TO THE NEXT CONTROLLED STATE UNLESS ALL EIGHT VERY DEEP RESEARCH EXIT CRITERIA ARE SATISFIED WITH VERIFIED EVIDENCE AND BOSS ISSUES THE FINAL EXIT DECISION.

And at State level:

> NO STATE MAY ADVANCE TO THE NEXT STATE UNLESS ALL IN-SCOPE MODULES HAVE PASSED THEIR MODULE EXIT GATES, THE STATE ITSELF HAS PASSED THE SAME EIGHT-CRITERIA INTEGRATION EXIT GATE, AND BOSS ISSUES THE FINAL STATE DECISION.

This Constitution is mandatory project-wide until superseded by an explicit Boss-approved constitutional revision.
