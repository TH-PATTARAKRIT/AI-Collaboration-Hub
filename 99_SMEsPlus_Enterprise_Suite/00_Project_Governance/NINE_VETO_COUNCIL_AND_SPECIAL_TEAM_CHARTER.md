## 1. Mandate

Boss establishes two directly accountable challenge layers:

1. `9 Veto Challenge Council` — PRIMARY standing challenge function.
2. `9 Special Team Challenge` — SECONDARY deep-investigation function.

Both report directly to Boss for authority purposes.

PMO / Secretary coordinates evidence and continuity only; PMO is not the superior authority of either challenge layer.

---

## 2. Primary Function — 9 Veto Challenge Council

The Council must challenge every controlled Session and Prompt before final Prompt issuance.

The Council is responsible for:

- asking material questions;
- identifying contradictions, blind spots and unsupported assumptions;
- challenging lifecycle / Gate / Scope / authority errors;
- determining whether deeper Special Team investigation is required;
- reviewing Special Team findings;
- recommending `READY / HOLD / FAIL-FROZEN` to the controlled Prompt Readiness process.

The Council does not execute Team A / B / C / D work and does not self-approve progression.

---

## 3. Secondary Function — 9 Special Team Challenge

Special Teams are activated only when a material Council concern requires deeper investigation.

They are responsible for:

- targeted deep research;
- evidence re-performance;
- contradiction investigation;
- root-cause investigation;
- official / authoritative evidence verification where required;
- technical / business / risk forensic analysis within the assigned mandate.

Special Teams return findings to the Council and Boss-controlled governance path.

They do not vote themselves into execution authority.

## 5. Direct-to-Boss Rule

Council and Special Team findings are not filtered for convenience.

PMO may consolidate duplicates and improve traceability, but must preserve the original material meaning, originating mandate and evidence reference.

If a material finding is suppressed from Boss because it is inconvenient, that is a governance control failure.

---

## 6. No Majority Vote

Neither the Council nor Special Teams decide by majority vote.

A single material Veto may be enough to place a Prompt on HOLD if evidence supports the concern.

Boss alone makes final business / Scope / Gate / override decisions.

---

## 7. Challenge Continuity Rule

The Council must challenge from accumulated project learning, not from a blank state.

Before each Prompt:

1. load previous challenge ledger;
2. load prior decisions and evidence;
3. build delta;
4. independently challenge the delta;
5. suppress unchanged duplicate questions;
6. reopen only with a documented Delta Trigger.

`No reset-to-zero challenge.`  
`No repeated question without a material delta.`

---

## 8. Anti-Groupthink Rule

For material work, the 9 Council mandates perform independent first-pass review before convergence.

Fresh Council findings must not be shown to other Council mandates before their first pass where practical.

PMO consolidates only after first-pass independence is preserved.

---

## 9. Scope Boundary

Challenge can reveal a missing capability but cannot silently add Functional Scope.

`Challenge -> Evidence -> Baseline Check -> In-Scope Action OR Change Request -> Boss Decision.`

No Council or Special Team may authorize Development, Release or Production by itself.
