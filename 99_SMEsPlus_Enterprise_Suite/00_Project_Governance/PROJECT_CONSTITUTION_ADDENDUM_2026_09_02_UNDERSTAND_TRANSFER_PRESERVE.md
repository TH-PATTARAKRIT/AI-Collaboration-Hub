## 1. Constitutional Addendum Ruling

Boss declares the following cross-lifecycle principle mandatory:

`Understand deeply -> Transfer accurately -> Document & Preserve verified understanding.`

Thai operating statement:

`เข้าใจให้จริง -> ส่งต่อให้ตรง -> รักษาไว้ด้วยหลักฐาน`

No existing constitutional control is weakened or superseded by this addendum.

## 2. Cross-Lifecycle Applicability

The principle applies, where material, across:

`Team A -> Team B -> Figma/UX -> EXPERT IBPV -> Pre-Development Gate -> Team C -> Team D/Testing -> EXPERT IDTM -> EXPERT IESA -> Operations`

and to other controlled Session/Prompt/team handoffs under the project.

## 3. Mandatory Constitutional Controls Added

1. Documentation completion alone is not proof that the owning team understands the subject.
2. A material handoff is not complete merely because files, links or tickets were sent.
3. The owning team must be able to explain and defend material understanding with evidence, while explicit Unknowns remain controlled.
4. The receiving team must receive the correct meaning, boundaries, Unknowns and no-assumption controls required for its role.
5. Teach-Back may be required where misunderstanding would materially affect downstream work.
6. Independent-team boundaries remain mandatory. Knowledge transfer must not become a predetermined Answer Key for a downstream independent team.
7. Evidence must be captured during work and preserved canonically after verification/handoff.
8. Material Unknowns must be resolved or explicitly controlled; artificial certainty is prohibited.
9. When a Special Team materially solves an owning team's problem, the owning team must absorb the relevant learning before applicable Gate closure.
10. Existing Evidence Gates, 9 Veto Challenge, formal independent verification, Team boundaries and Boss approval remain unchanged.
