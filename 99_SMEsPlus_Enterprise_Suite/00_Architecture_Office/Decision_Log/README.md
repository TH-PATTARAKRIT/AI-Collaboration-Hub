# Decision Log

## Purpose
Centralized record of all architecture decisions and approvals.

## Structure
- **DL-YYYY-MM-DD-NNN**: Decision Log entry ID
- One entry per major decision
- Cross-reference to ADR when applicable

## Entry Contents
- **Decision Date**: When decision was made
- **Decision ID**: Unique identifier
- **Title**: Clear description
- **Owner**: Decision maker/owner
- **Status**: Approved/Conditional/Rejected
- **Approver**: Who approved (Boss signature)
- **Rationale**: Why this decision
- **Implementation Start**: When work begins
- **Related ADR**: Link to architecture decision record
- **Impact**: Who and what is affected
- **Conditions**: Any approval conditions
- **Evidence**: Links to supporting documents

## Retention Policy
- **Active Decisions**: Keep for duration of system
- **Archived Decisions**: Keep for 5 years
- **Superseded Decisions**: Keep but mark as superseded

## Query Examples
- Decisions by date range
- Decisions by topic/system
- Decisions by approver
- Pending approvals
- Conditional decisions
- Rejected decisions
