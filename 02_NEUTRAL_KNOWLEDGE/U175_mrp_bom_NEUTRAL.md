# U175 — mrp.bom: Bill of Materials Routing, Operations, Byproducts — Neutral Knowledge

**Unit**: U175
**Date**: 2026-10-02
**Status**: GATE-PASS

## Neutral References

| Neutral-ref | Neutral statement |
|---|---|
| N-U175-01 | A bill of materials has a type that identifies whether it results in a manufactured product or a kit; exactly two types exist in Community and the default is the manufacturing type. |
| N-U175-02 | A bill of materials is defined against a product template and is restricted to consumable product types; variant-specific narrowing is optional through a second product field. |
| N-U175-03 | When a bill of materials specifies a product variant, it applies only to that variant; without a variant it applies to all variants of the template. |
| N-U175-04 | The output quantity on a bill of materials must be strictly positive; it represents the smallest batch size the BOM describes and defaults to one unit. |
| N-U175-05 | Work center operations are stored directly on the bill of materials record; there is no separate routing document in Community 19. |
| N-U175-06 | By-product lines are a first-class feature of the bill of materials in Community 19, grouped under a security setting that controls their visibility. |
| N-U175-07 | A consumption policy on the bill of materials controls whether workers may consume more or fewer components than specified, and whether a manager override is required when the BOM quantities are not met. |
| N-U175-08 | A manufacturing readiness setting determines whether a production order is ready when all components are available or only when components for the first operation are available. |
| N-U175-09 | Operation dependencies may be enabled per bill of materials; when enabled, individual operations can declare predecessor operations that must finish before they can start. |
| N-U175-10 | An optional batch size setting on a bill of materials controls the size of automatically generated production orders for that product. |
| N-U175-11 | Component lines on a bill of materials may have a quantity of zero to represent optional components; quantity must be non-negative. |
| N-U175-12 | A component line may be linked to a specific work center operation on the same bill of materials, indicating where that component is consumed during production. |
| N-U175-13 | Each component line stores a computed reference to the component product's own bill of materials; when that referenced bill is a kit type, the line participates in nested phantom explosion. |
| N-U175-14 | The explosion algorithm for a bill of materials processes lines iteratively; when a component itself has a kit bill of materials, its sub-lines are queued for processing and the kit structure is flattened into leaf components. |
| N-U175-15 | A work center operation record belongs exclusively to one bill of materials, is deleted when that bill is deleted, and requires both a bill of materials link and a work center assignment. |
| N-U175-16 | The duration of a work center operation can be set manually as a fixed value or computed automatically from the average of the most recent completed work orders. |
| N-U175-17 | Operation predecessor relationships are stored on each operation; the system prevents circular dependencies among operations on the same bill of materials. |
| N-U175-18 | When a production order is confirmed, raw material moves are generated by exploding the bill of materials; each move records which component line it came from and which operation consumes it. |
| N-U175-19 | When a delivery or transfer move involves a kit product, the move is replaced at explosion time with individual component moves; the original product move is cancelled. |
| N-U175-20 | Stock availability for a kit product is computed as the minimum ratio of available component stock to the quantity of each component required per kit unit. |
| N-U175-21 | The algorithm for selecting a bill of materials for a product prefers variant-specific bills over template-level bills and uses the sequence, then variant specificity, then record ID as tiebreakers. |
| N-U175-22 | A by-product line records the product produced as a side output, its quantity and unit of measure, an optional operation where it is produced, and a cost share percentage. |
| N-U175-23 | The sum of all by-product cost shares for a given variant must not exceed 100 percent; individual shares must be non-negative. |
| N-U175-24 | A by-product line may not refer to the same product as the finished good of the bill of materials. |
| N-U175-25 | Component lines, operation lines, and by-product lines can each be restricted to specific product variants through attribute value filters. |
| N-U175-26 | The system detects circular component references across bills of materials when a bill is created or modified, preventing configurations where a product is ultimately a component of itself. |
| N-U175-27 | A manufacturing lead time in days may be recorded on a bill of materials to support scheduling; in multi-level structures, component lead times accumulate. |
| N-U175-28 | A separate field records how many days in advance a production order for this product should be confirmed to allow time for component replenishment. |
| N-U175-29 | Bill of materials versioning and engineering change orders are not available in Community 19; that functionality is provided only by a separate enterprise addon not present in the Community source. |
| N-U175-30 | Each by-product defined on a bill of materials generates a dedicated finished-goods stock move when a production order is created; that move carries the cost share and the optional operation link from the by-product definition. |
